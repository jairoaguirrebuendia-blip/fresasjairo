# Reconocer sugerencias (LIST-101)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", pp. 76–79, 96; Chapter 1 "Contractions", *Let's*, pp. 9–10.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Detectar en un diálogo corto las fórmulas de sugerencia y responder correctamente a *What does the man/woman suggest?*

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Si la pregunta es *What does X suggest/recommend?*, busca en la segunda intervención una de estas señales; lo que sigue a la fórmula **es** la respuesta:
- **Why don't you/we…?** (no pide razones)
- **How about / What about + -ing?**
- **Let's…** · **Shall we…?**
- **You'd better…** · **You should…** · **Have you tried…?**
Cuidado con *Why don't you…?* en tono de pregunta real ("¿por qué no viniste?" se dice *Why didn't you…?*, en pasado).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Problema (hablante 1)` → `Why don't you / How about / Have you tried + acción` → **Respuesta = la acción**

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Why don't we see a film?" (p. 76)
- "How about going to 'Mad Doctor Brown'?" (p. 76)
- "Why don't we start with an aerobics class twice a week…?" (p. 79)
- "How about Torquay? The weather's usually good…" (p. 96)
- "Have you tried adjusting the aerial?" (p. 93)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ Elegir una opción que habla de *razones* ante *Why don't we…?*
- ❌ Confundir quién sugiere: la pregunta del examen especifica *the man* o *the woman*.

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Fórmula | Función | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| Why don't we/you…? | sugerencia | verbo base |
| How about…? | sugerencia | + -ing / sustantivo |
| Have you tried…? | sugerencia de solución | + -ing |
| You'd better… | consejo fuerte | p. 88 "You'd better hurry" |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Woman: I can never find a seat in the library during exam week. Man: Have you tried going there before the morning rush? What does the man suggest?"
  opciones: ["Arriving at the library early", "Studying at home instead", "Asking the librarian for a seat", "Rushing through her exams"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Have you tried + -ing' introduce una sugerencia: ir antes de la hora pico de la mañana, es decir, temprano."
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: My back hurts after sitting at the computer all day. Woman: You'd better take a break every hour. What does the woman suggest?"
  opciones: ["Resting regularly", "Buying a new computer", "Seeing a doctor immediately", "Working fewer days"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'You'd better + verbo' = consejo: hacer pausas cada hora."
```
