# Teléfono, recados y citas (FUNC-207)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", Calling someone at work / Leaving a message / Making an appointment (pp. 77–78), diálogo telefónico (p. 98).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Entender las fórmulas telefónicas, casi todas con phrasal verbs (*hold on, put through, call back, get through*), y cómo se propone y acepta una cita.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Identificarse: **This is + nombre** (no *I am* al teléfono).
- Esperar: **Hold the line / Hold on / Hang on (a moment)**.
- Conectar: **I'll put you through.**
- Ausencia: **I'm afraid he's out at the moment / He's in a meeting. Can I take a message?**
- Recado: **Can you ask him to give me a call?** · **I'll see he gets your message.**
- Cita: **I'd like to make an appointment…** → **How about next Thursday at 10?** → **That sounds fine.**

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Could I speak to ___, please?` → `Hold the line, please. I'll put you through.` / `I'm afraid ___ is out. Can I take a message?`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Hello. This is Kenneth Beare. May I speak to Ms Sunshine, please?" (p. 77)
- "Hold the line a moment, I'll check if she is in her office." (p. 77)
- "I'm afraid he's out at the moment. Can I take a message?" (p. 77)
- "How about next Thursday at 10 o'clock?" — "That sounds fine." (p. 78)
- "I'm sorry, the line's busy, will you hold?" — "Yes, I'll hold." (p. 98)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I am Fred* (al teléfono) → ✅ *This is Fred.*
- ⚠️ El libro escribe "I'll put your through" (p. 77, errata) → ✅ *I'll put you through.*
- ⚠️ "enquire" (p. 77) es BrE → AmE *inquire*.
