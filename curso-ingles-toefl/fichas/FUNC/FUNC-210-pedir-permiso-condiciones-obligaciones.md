# Pedir permiso, condiciones y obligaciones (FUNC-210)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", Borrowing (pp. 85–86), Rules (pp. 96–97).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Pedir permiso (*Can I borrow…?*), entender un permiso condicionado (*OK then, provided…*) y distinguir *have to / must / don't have to*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Permiso: **Can I borrow + objeto?** (*borrow* = tomar prestado; *lend* = prestar).
- Permiso condicionado: **Well, OK then, provided (that) + cláusula** = sí, pero solo si…
- Obligación: **have to** (regla externa), **must** (obligación fuerte/regla). Pregunta: **Do I have to…?**
- **No, but you must…** = eso no es obligatorio, pero esto otro sí.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Can I borrow + N?` · `OK, provided (that) + S + V` · `Do I have to + V?` · `You don't have to + V` (no es necesario) ≠ `You mustn't + V` (prohibido)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Dad. Can I borrow the car tomorrow?" (p. 85)
- "I promise I'll drive carefully this time." (p. 86)
- "Well, OK then, provided you're home by 8." (p. 86)
- "Do I have to wear a uniform?" — "No, but you must wear a tie." (p. 96)
- "Yes, you have to work overtime every other Saturday." (p. 97)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Can you borrow me your car?* → ✅ *Can you lend me your car?* / *Can I borrow your car?*
- ❌ *I must to say…* → ✅ *I must say…* (modal + verbo base; cap. 7, p. 106)
- ❌ *We better don't tell others.* → ✅ *We had better not tell others.* (cap. 7, p. 111)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| provided (that) | siempre que, con tal de que | = *as long as* |
| fill it up | llenar el tanque | *petrol* (BrE) = *gas* (AmE) |
| every other Saturday | un sábado sí y otro no | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: Can I borrow your laptop this afternoon? Woman: Sure, as long as you bring it back before my seminar at five. What does the woman mean?"
  opciones: ["The man may use it if he returns it by five.", "She needs the laptop all afternoon.", "The man must attend her seminar.", "She will buy a new laptop at five."]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Sure, as long as…' (= provided) concede permiso con una condición: devolverlo antes de las cinco."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Visitors ____ wear a tie, but a jacket is not required."
  opciones: ["must", "don't have to", "must not", "had better not"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Se contrasta una obligación (corbata) con algo no requerido (saco): 'must'. 'Don't have to' indicaría falta de obligación."
```
