# Rechazar invitaciones y dar excusas (FUNC-206)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", What shall we do? (p. 76), Plans and excuses (pp. 87–88).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer un "no" que no contiene la palabra *no*: disculpa + excusa, preferencia contraria o aplazamiento vago.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
En inglés el rechazo cortés casi nunca es directo. Patrón típico:
1. **Marcador de duda o disculpa:** *Let me see… / Oh, I'm sorry / I'm afraid / I'd love to, but…*
2. **Excusa:** *I'm having dinner with a friend / I go to evening class on Tuesdays.*
3. **Alternativa o cierre vago:** *How about the following Tuesday? / Maybe some other time? — Yeah, I'll give you a call.*
*I'd rather not* = prefiero no (rechazo suave a una propuesta).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`(I'm) sorry, I can't. + I'm + V-ing (plan futuro)` · `I'd rather not.` · `Maybe some other time.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Let me see ..... Oh, I am sorry, I'm having dinner with a friend." (p. 87)
- "I'm sorry, I can't. I'm having dinner with a friend." (p. 88)
- "I go to evening class on Tuesdays." — "Oh, maybe some other time?" (p. 88)
- "Let's see 'Action Man 4'." — "I'd rather not. I don't like violent films." (p. 76)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ Interpretar *Maybe some other time* como aceptación → es un aplazamiento sin compromiso (≈ no).
- ❌ *I'd rather not to go* → ✅ *I'd rather not go* (*would rather* + verbo base).

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| Let me see… | déjame ver… | duda; suele anticipar un problema |
| I'd love to, but… | me encantaría, pero… | casi siempre rechazo |
| some other time | en otra ocasión | aplazamiento vago |
| I'll give you a call | te llamo | cierre cortés |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: los dos diálogos "Plans and excuses", pp. 87–88 (uno acepta, otro rechaza: buen contraste).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: A few of us are going hiking on Saturday. Want to come along? Woman: I'd love to, but I promised my sister I'd help her move. What does the woman mean?"
  opciones: ["She will go hiking with her sister.", "She loves hiking more than moving.", "She cannot go hiking on Saturday.", "She will help the man move on Saturday."]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'I'd love to, but…' + excusa = rechazo cortés. Ya tiene un compromiso (ayudar a su hermana a mudarse)."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
