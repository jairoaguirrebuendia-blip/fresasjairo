# Quejarse, disculparse y expresar incredulidad (FUNC-209)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", Winning the lotto (pp. 81–82), A faulty TV / A bad holiday (pp. 93–94).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer las fórmulas de queja, de respuesta empática, de disculpa formal y de incredulidad (*You must be joking*), y la actitud del hablante.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Queja: **I'd like to make a complaint about…** · **I want a refund.** · **I want to see the manager.**
- Respuesta empática: **I'm sorry to hear that. What exactly was the problem?** · **Oh dear.**
- Disculpa formal: **I do apologise** (el *do* enfático refuerza la disculpa).
- Negativa cortés: **I'm afraid it isn't our policy to give refunds.**
- Incredulidad/rechazo: **You must be joking / You've got to be joking** = no lo puedo creer / eso es inaceptable. *To cap it all* = para colmo.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`I'd like to make a complaint about + N` · `I'm sorry to hear that.` · `I do apologise.` · `You must be joking!`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "I'd like to make a complaint about my holiday in Portugal last week." — "I'm sorry to hear that." (p. 94)
- "And to cap it all the food in the hotel restaurant was awful." (p. 94)
- "I do apologise. I'd like to offer you a 20% discount…" (p. 94)
- "A 20% discount, you must be joking. I want to see the manager." (p. 94)
- "I'm afraid it isn't our policy to give refunds, sir." (p. 93)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ Tomar *You must be joking* como "debes estar bromeando" literal → expresa rechazo/indignación.
- ❌ *I'm sorry for hear that* → ✅ *I'm sorry to hear that.*
- ❌ *Don't feel anger with me!* → ✅ *Don't be mad at me!* (cap. 7, p. 108)
