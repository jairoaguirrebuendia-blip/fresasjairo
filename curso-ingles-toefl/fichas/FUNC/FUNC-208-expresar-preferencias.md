# Expresar preferencias (FUNC-208)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", In the gym (p. 79), Booking a flight (p. 89), Barbara and Ted… (p. 96).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer y usar correctamente *would rather*, *would prefer*, *prefer X to Y*, *don't mind* y *don't fancy*, y entender qué prefiere realmente cada hablante.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
| Fórmula | Forma que sigue |
|---|---|
| **I'd rather** (not) | verbo base |
| **I'd prefer** | *to* + verbo |
| **I prefer X to Y** | sustantivo/-ing *to* sustantivo/-ing |
| **I don't mind** | -ing (= no me molesta, acepto) |
| **I don't fancy** (BrE) | sustantivo/-ing (= no me apetece) |
| **I may as well** | verbo base (= entonces mejor…, elección por descarte) |

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`I'd rather + V` · `I'd prefer to + V` · `I prefer V-ing to V-ing` · `I don't mind + V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "I'd rather do something more exciting this year." (p. 96)
- "I prefer lying on a beach to hang-gliding and canoeing." (p. 96)
- "I'd prefer to do something different this time." (p. 96)
- "I like doing aerobics, but I hate jogging. I don't mind doing some weight-lifting, though." (p. 79)
- "I may as well go with XYZ then." (p. 89)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I'd rather to stay home.* → ✅ *I'd rather stay home.*
- ❌ *I prefer lying on a beach than hang-gliding.* → ✅ *…to hang-gliding.* (*prefer X to Y*)
- ❌ *I don't mind to do it.* → ✅ *I don't mind doing it.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| You have a point | tienes razón (en parte) | acuerdo parcial, p. 96 |
| Sounds OK to me | me parece bien | aceptación |
| I don't fancy | no me apetece | BrE; AmE *I don't feel like* |
| may as well | da igual, entonces… | elección por descarte |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: p. 96 (diálogo de vacaciones, rico en estructuras de preferencia).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Woman: Do you want to take the bus or walk to the museum? Man: I'd rather not walk in this heat. What does the man mean?"
  opciones: ["He would like to walk to the museum.", "He prefers to take the bus.", "He doesn't want to go to the museum.", "He doesn't mind the heat."]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'I'd rather not walk' = prefiero no caminar; entre las dos opciones, elige el autobús. No rechaza el museo."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Many students prefer studying in groups ____ alone."
  opciones: ["than study", "rather study", "that studying", "to studying"]
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Patrón 'prefer X to Y' con formas paralelas: prefer studying … to studying."
```
