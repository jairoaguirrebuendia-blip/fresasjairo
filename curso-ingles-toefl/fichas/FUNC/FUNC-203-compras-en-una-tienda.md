# Comprar en una tienda (FUNC-203)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", Shopping for a sweater (pp. 72–73), Clothes shop / Electrical shop (pp. 86–87).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Seguir una compra: ofrecimiento del vendedor, talla, probarse ropa, quejas sobre la prenda y la decisión final (*I'll take it / I'll have it*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- El vendedor abre con **Can I help you? / May I help you?**; el cliente responde **I'm looking for…**
- Para probarse: **Can I try it on?** (*try on* = phrasal verb separable).
- Problema con la prenda: **It's too large / They're a little long / It's a little expensive** + pedir alternativa **Do you have a smaller/cheaper one?**
- Decisión: **I'll take it / I'll have it** = lo compro.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`It's too + adj.` / `It's a little + adj.` → `Do you have (anything) + comparativo?` → `I'll take it.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Yes, I'm looking for a sweater." (p. 73)
- "Yes, that's nice. Can I try it on?" (p. 73)
- "It's too large. Do you have a smaller size?" (p. 73)
- "It's a little expensive. Do you have a cheaper one?" — "Yes. This one's only £150." — "I'll take it." (p. 87)
- "Do you take credit cards?" — "Yes, we do." (p. 73)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Can I try on it?* → ✅ *Can I try it on?* (con pronombre, el phrasal verb separable se separa obligatoriamente)
- ❌ *It's too much large* → ✅ *It's too large* / *It's much too large*.
