# Hacer sugerencias e invitaciones (FUNC-205)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 6 "Conversation", What shall we do? (pp. 76–77), Plans and excuses (p. 87); Chapter 1 "Contractions", Let's (pp. 9–10).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Identificar las fórmulas de sugerencia e invitación y sus respuestas de aceptación.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Fórmulas de sugerencia (todas significan "propongo que…"):
- **Why don't we + verbo base?**
- **How about / What about + -ing / sustantivo?**
- **Let's + verbo base** (negativo: *let's not*; el libro menciona también *don't let's*, poco usado).
- **Shall we + verbo base?** (más británico/formal).
- **Would you like to + verbo?** (invitación).
Aceptar: **That sounds good (to me) / Sounds great / Great idea! / I'd love to / Sure.**

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Why don't we + V?` · `How about + V-ing?` · `Let's + V.` · `Would you like to + V?`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Hi Chris, would you like to do something with me this weekend?" — "Sure. What shall we do?" (p. 76)
- "Why don't we see a film?" (p. 76)
- "How about going to 'Mad Doctor Brown'?" (p. 76)
- "What about going to that new Italian restaurant?" — "Great idea! Let's meet there at six." (p. 77)
- "Let's keep this just between us." (sugerencia con *let's*, p. 10)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *How about go to the movies?* → ✅ *How about going to the movies?* (*how about* + gerundio)
- ❌ *Why don't we to see a film?* → ✅ *Why don't we see a film?* (verbo base)
- ❌ *Let's to go!* → ✅ *Let's go!*
- ⚠️ "That's sounds good to me" (p. 76) es errata del libro → ✅ *That sounds good to me.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| Why don't we…? | ¿por qué no…? = propongo | NO pide una razón |
| How about…? | ¿qué tal si…? | + -ing |
| have a bite to eat | comer algo ligero | p. 76 |
| When is it on? | ¿a qué hora es (la función)? | *be on* = tener lugar |

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
*Shall we…?* es más frecuente en BrE; en AmE se prefiere *Should we…?* o *Do you want to…?*. *Film* (BrE) = *movie* (AmE).
