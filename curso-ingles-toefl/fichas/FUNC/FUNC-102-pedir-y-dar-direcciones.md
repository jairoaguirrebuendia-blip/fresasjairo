# Pedir y dar direcciones (FUNC-102)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheets 14a "Map dictation" y 14b "Place cards", teacher's notes (s/p).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Seguir instrucciones de ubicación habladas y entender dónde está un lugar a partir de preposiciones de lugar.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Para pedir ubicación: *Excuse me, where's…, please?* Para responder se combinan **imperativos de movimiento** (*go past, turn right, take the second turning*) y **preposiciones de lugar** (*opposite, next to, between, behind, on the corner of, in front of*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Excuse me, where's + place (+ please)?`
- `It's + preposition + landmark.` → *It's next to the restaurant.*
- `Go past… / Turn right… / Take the first/second turning on the left/right. / Go to the end of…`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Excuse me, where's the Park School of English, please? — It's next to the Italian restaurant, opposite the cinema.
- Go past the hospital on the left and cross over the River Thames.
- At the roundabout, turn right.
- Take the second turning on the right. Go to the end and the museum is in front of you.
- Opposite the theatre is St Paul's church and next to that is the Black Prince pub.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *in front of* = "enfrente de (al otro lado de la calle)" → eso es ✅ *opposite*; *in front of* = delante de.
- ❌ *next of* → ✅ *next to*
- ❌ *between the museum with the river* → ✅ *between the museum **and** the river*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| opposite | enfrente (cruzando) | falso amigo con *in front of* |
| on the corner of X and Y | en la esquina de X y Y | |
| roundabout / crossroads | glorieta / cruce | británico; en EE. UU. *traffic circle / intersection* |
| turning | calle (para girar) | *take the second turning* |
| It's not very far. | no está muy lejos | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Woman: Excuse me, is the post office near here? Man: Sure. It's right across the street from the bank, on the corner. What does the man say about the post office?"
  opciones: ["It is inside the bank.", "It is opposite the bank.", "It is behind the bank.", "It is far from the bank."]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Across the street from' = opposite (enfrente, al otro lado de la calle). Es una paráfrasis típica del TOEFL."
```
