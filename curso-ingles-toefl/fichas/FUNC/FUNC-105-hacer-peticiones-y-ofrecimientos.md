# Hacer peticiones y ofrecimientos (FUNC-105)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 30 "Would you mind…?", teacher's notes (s/p).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir una **petición** (*Could you…?* = quiero que tú lo hagas) de un **ofrecimiento** (*Shall I…?* = yo lo hago por ti) y entender cómo se aceptan o rechazan.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Peticiones: *Can you…? · Could you…? · I wonder if you could…? · Would you mind + -ing?*
- Ofrecimientos: *Shall I do that?* — respuestas: *That's very kind of you. Thank you.* (acepta) / *No, it's all right, thank you. No, I'll do it.* (rechaza).
- *Would you mind + -ing?* pide que **el otro** haga algo; *Would you mind if I…?* pide permiso para hacerlo **yo** (ver FUNC-104).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Can/Could you + base verb?`
- `I wonder if you could + base verb.`
- `Would you mind + verb-ing?`
- `Shall I + base verb?`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Can you…? / Could you…? / I wonder if you could…? / Would you mind -ing?
- Shall I do that? — That's very kind of you.
- Thank you. / No, it's all right, thank you. / No, I'll do it.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Would you mind to close the door?* → ✅ *Would you mind **closing** the door?* (mind + gerundio)
- ❌ Confundir quién actúa: *Shall I…?* = lo hace el hablante; *Could you…?* = lo hace el oyente.
- ❌ *I wonder if could you help me.* → ✅ *I wonder if **you could** help me.* (orden afirmativo en pregunta indirecta)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| I wonder if you could… | me pregunto si podría… | petición muy cortés |
| Shall I…? | ¿quiere que (yo)…? | ofrecimiento |
| That's very kind of you. | muy amable | acepta |
| No, it's all right. | no hace falta | rechaza |

```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Woman: These boxes are so heavy, and I still have six more in the car. Man: Shall I give you a hand? What does the man mean?"
  opciones: ["He wants the woman to help him.", "He is offering to help carry the boxes.", "He thinks she bought too many things.", "He needs to borrow her car."]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Shall I…?' es un ofrecimiento y 'give someone a hand' = ayudar: se ofrece a cargar las cajas."
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: Would you mind turning the music down a little? I'm trying to study. Woman: Oh, sorry, I didn't realize it was so loud. What will the woman probably do?"
  opciones: ["Turn up the music.", "Lower the volume.", "Help the man study.", "Leave the room with the man."]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Would you mind + -ing' es una petición para que ella baje el volumen; su disculpa indica que lo hará."
```
