# Formular preguntas sociales correctamente (FUNC-211)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 7 "Common Errors", pp. 100–113.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Evitar los errores típicos de orden y de fórmula en preguntas cotidianas, sobre todo las **preguntas indirectas** (embedded), muy evaluadas en Structure.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
1. Pregunta directa: **auxiliar + sujeto + verbo** (*Why do you come here so often?*).
2. Pregunta indirecta (*Can you tell me…, I wonder…*): **orden afirmativo, sin auxiliar** (*…when your birthday is*).
3. Fórmulas fijas: **What do you think of…?** (no *How do you think…*), **What does he look like?** (aspecto), **How have you been?**

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- Directa: `Wh- + do/does/did + S + V?`
- Indirecta: `Can you tell me / I wonder + wh-/if + S + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Incorrect: *Why are you come here so often?* → Correct: *Why do you come here so often?* (p. 104)
- Incorrect: *Can you tell me when are you birthday date?* → Correct: *Can you tell me when your birthday is?* (p. 110)
- Incorrect: *How do you think my idea?* → Correct: *What do you think of my idea?* (p. 113)
- Incorrect: *OK, tell me how does he like?* → Correct: *…what he looks like* (p. 109)
- Incorrect: *Why not come yesterday?* → Correct: *Why didn't you come yesterday?* (p. 102)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I wonder why are you keep study…* → ✅ *I wonder why you keep studying…* (p. 110)
- ❌ *Where is the students come from?* → ✅ *Where do the students come from?* (p. 111)
- ❌ *But you know a lot about computers, isn't it?* → ✅ *…, don't you?* (tag question con el auxiliar correcto; p. 107–108)
- ⚠️ El libro da como "Correct" *I wonder if you were a teacher before?* (p. 111): la forma natural es *I wonder if you have ever been a teacher.* (afirmación, sin signo de interrogación).

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| What do you think of…? | ¿qué opinas de…? | opinión |
| What is he like? | ¿cómo es (carácter)? | |
| What does he look like? | ¿cómo es (físico)? | |
| Come to think of it | ahora que lo pienso | p. 107 |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The receptionist asked me ____."
  opciones: ["when was my appointment", "when my appointment was", "when did my appointment", "my appointment was when"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Pregunta indirecta: después de 'asked me' se usa orden afirmativo (wh- + sujeto + verbo), sin inversión."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Could you {A|tell} me {B|where} {C|is the} {D|bookstore}?"
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "En la pregunta indirecta va 'where the bookstore is', no 'where is the bookstore'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
El capítulo recoge errores de chat de hablantes asiáticos; algunas "correcciones" del libro siguen siendo erróneas o poco naturales: *the same car like yours* (p. 112; ✅ *the same car as yours*), *I have been in abroad for 10 years* (p. 109; ✅ *I have lived abroad for 10 years*), *how beautiful your eyes look like* (p. 112; ✅ *how beautiful your eyes are*). No usarlas como modelo.
