# Confusing words I (advice/advise … weather/whether) (VOCAB-211)

> **Fuente:** *Building Vocabulary for College* — Smith, R. K., Chapters 1–14 (Part One), Mastering Confusing Words, pp. 15, 25, 36, 45, 71, 81, 91, 101, 111, 127, 136, 151.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir pares de palabras que se confunden por forma o sonido y detectar su mal uso en Written Expression.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Cada capítulo cierra con un par de palabras que se confunden por su parecido en forma o pronunciación. Muchas difieren en **categoría gramatical** (sustantivo vs verbo: *advice/advise*, *device/devise*, *effect/affect*), algo que el TOEFL evalúa en *Written Expression* (error de word form / word choice).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`término` → definición del libro (en inglés) + equivalente en español

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Ming's advice is to never buy a car with more than 30,000 miles on it. / Did Professor Morrison advise you to switch your major to biology?
- Jenna didn't think breaking up with him would affect her so much. / Salary raises had a wonderful effect on the morale of the staff.
- The large shopping mall was busier today than it was Saturday. / After working for a year, Jodi then plans to attend graduate school.
- Attending every class is a good principle for college students to remember.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Her advise was helpful* → ✅ *Her advice was helpful* (sustantivo).
- ❌ *The medicine had no affect* → ✅ *no effect* (sustantivo).
- ❌ *She is more tall then her sister* → ✅ *taller than*.
- ❌ *The company's personal department* → ✅ *personnel department*.

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The new regulations will {A|effect} thousands of workers {B|who} {C|have been} employed in the industry {D|for} many years."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Se necesita el verbo 'affect' (influir en); 'effect' es normalmente sustantivo (resultado)."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The second edition of the textbook is much longer ____ the first one."
  opciones: ["then", "than", "that", "as"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "En comparaciones con -er/more se usa 'than'; 'then' indica tiempo."
```
