# Colocaciones del capítulo Common Errors (VOCAB-107)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 7 "Common Errors", pp. 100–113.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Fijar colocaciones verbo+sustantivo y adjetivo/verbo+preposición que el hispanohablante suele traducir mal y que el TOEFL evalúa en *error ID*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Una colocación es una combinación fija que "suena natural": *do a favor* (no *give*), *good **at***, *interested **in***, *go abroad* (sin *to*). No hay regla lógica; se memorizan en bloque.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`do sb a favor` · `go on a picnic` · `be in a bit of a hurry` · `go abroad` · `be good at` · `be interested in` · `invest in` · `talk to sb` · `be of great help to sb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Incorrect: *I am going to a picnic.* → Correct: *I am going on a picnic.* (p. 100)
- Incorrect: *Give me a favour!* → Correct: *Please do me a favour!* (p. 102)
- Incorrect: *He went to abroad…* → Correct: *He went abroad…* (p. 103)
- Incorrect: *are you investing on stock market?* → Correct: *…investing in the stock market?* (p. 106)
- Incorrect: *He will be great help for you.* → Correct: *He will be of great help to you.* (p. 110)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *good in computers* → ✅ *good at computers* (p. 110)
- ❌ *What sport are you interest?* → ✅ *What sports are you interested in?* (p. 110)
- ❌ *I want to talk someone.* → ✅ *I want to talk to someone.* (p. 106)
- ❌ *far away with China* → ✅ *far away from China* (p. 109)
- ❌ *I am difficult to learn English.* → ✅ *It is difficult for me to learn English.* (p. 101)
- ❌ *many softwares* → ✅ *a lot of software* (sustantivo incontable, p. 112)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| do (sb) a favor | hacer un favor | AmE *favor* |
| in a bit of a hurry | con algo de prisa | p. 100 |
| take sb out on a date | invitar a salir | p. 100 |
| give (the cat) a bath | bañar | p. 103 |
| join in (the talk) | unirse a | p. 106 |
| have difficulty (in) + V-ing | tener dificultad para | p. 109 |
| bored vs boring | aburrido (sentir) vs aburrido (causar) | p. 102 |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Many students {A|who are} {B|interested on} environmental issues {C|join} the club {D|every year}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "La colocación correcta es 'interested in', no 'interested on'."
- tipo: vocab
  pregunta: "Could you ____ me a favor and close the window?"
  opciones: ["make", "give", "do", "take"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Colocación fija: 'do someone a favor'. 'Give/make a favor' son calcos del español."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
Algunas "correcciones" del libro son erróneas: *the same car like yours* (p. 112) → ✅ *the same car as yours*; *have been in abroad* (p. 109) → ✅ *have lived abroad*; *the latest typhoon has cost the people 400 million damage* (p. 107) es poco natural (✅ *caused 400 million in damage*). El libro usa ortografía británica (*favour*).
