# Phrasal verbs de relaciones personales (VOCAB-101)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 9 "Phrasal Verbs", pp. 119–181.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Entender los phrasal verbs que describen trato, parecido, conflicto y reconciliación entre personas.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Muchos se distinguen solo por la partícula: *look **up to*** (admirar) vs *look **down on*** (menospreciar); *make **up*** (reconciliarse) vs *make up **for*** (compensar). Hay que aprender la combinación completa.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`take after + persona` (parecerse a un familiar) · `put up with + N` (tolerar) · `run into + persona` (encontrarse por casualidad)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Take after** = resemble: "He takes after his mother." (p. 181)
- **Put up with** = tolerate: "I can't put up with my neighbour's noise any longer…" (p. 169)
- **Run into** = meet by accident: "I ran into James in a bar in the City on Friday." (p. 171)
- **Make up** = stop being angry: "They are always arguing, but they make up again very quickly." (p. 163)
- **Look down on** = have a low opinion of: "He looks down on his colleagues…" (p. 161)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *put up* (alojar) ≠ ✅ *put up with* (tolerar).
- ❌ *run into* solo = chocar: también = **cost** ("has run into millions of dollars", p. 171) y encontrarse con alguien.
- ⚠️ El libro escribe "She's looks up to her mother" (p. 162, errata) → ✅ *She looks up to her mother.*
