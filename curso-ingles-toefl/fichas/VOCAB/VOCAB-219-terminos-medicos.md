# Medical terms and word parts (VOCAB-219)

> **Fuente:** *Building Vocabulary for College* — Smith, R. K., Chapter 28 — Medical Terms, Medical Terms: Word Parts; Sets One–Two, pp. 276–281.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer y comprender este set de vocabulario cuando aparezca en lecturas académicas del examen.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
El Cap. 28 inicia con 15 partes de palabras médicas (algia, arteri, arthr, cardi, derm, gastr, hem, itis, my, neur, osteo, phleb, psych, pulmo, tomy) y luego dos sets de términos médicos.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`término` → definición del libro (en inglés) + equivalente en español

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Arthritis is inflammation of the joints.
- The patient was relieved when the doctor told her that the small growth on her neck was benign.
- My cousin has suffered from chronic digestive problems since he was born.
- At present, there is no cure for Parkinson's disease, but palliative care often enables people with this disease to live more comfortably.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ Traducir por 'falso amigo' (parecido al español) sin verificar → ✅ comprobar con la definición y el contexto.
- ❌ Memorizar la palabra aislada → ✅ aprenderla con la oración de ejemplo y su categoría gramatical.

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: reading
  pregunta: "The infection was asymptomatic for several months, so the patients had no idea that they were carrying the virus. The word 'asymptomatic' is closest in meaning to"
  opciones: ["extremely painful", "showing no signs of illness", "spreading rapidly", "easily cured"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "a- = without (Cap. 3) + symptom; además 'had no idea' confirma que no había síntomas."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
Partes médicas: algia = pain; arteri = blood vessel; arthr = joint; cardi = heart; derm = skin; gastr = stomach; hem = blood; itis = inflammation; my = muscle; neur = nerve; osteo = bone; phleb = vein; psych = mind; pulmo = lung; tomy = act of cutting.
