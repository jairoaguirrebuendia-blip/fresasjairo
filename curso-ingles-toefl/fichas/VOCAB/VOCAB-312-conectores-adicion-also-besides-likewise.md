# Conectores de adición y semejanza: also / besides / in addition / likewise (VOCAB-312)

> **Fuente:** English Synonyms and Antonyms — Fernald, Part I, entrada ALSO, PDF p. 36.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir los conectores que **solo añaden** información de los que añaden algo **semejante**, y colocarlos bien en la oración.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Fernald divide el grupo en dos:
1. **Solo añaden un dato**: *as well as, besides, in addition, too* (y el arcaico *withal*).
2. **Añaden algo parecido a lo anterior**: *also, in like manner, likewise, similarly*.
- **as well** va **después** de la palabra o frase a la que se une: *the players, and the singers as well*.
- **as well as** une elementos: *the singers as well as the players*.
- Antónimos (conectores de contraste): *but, nevertheless, on the contrary, on the other hand, yet, in spite of, notwithstanding*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`..., and X as well.` · `X as well as Y` · `In addition, + clause` · `In addition to + noun/-ing` · `Besides + noun/-ing` · `Likewise / Similarly, + clause`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- We can say the singers as well as the players, or the players, and the singers as well.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *In addition to raise prices, ...* → ✅ *In addition to **raising** prices* (*to* es preposición → -ing).
- ❌ *Besides of the cost* → ✅ *Besides the cost*.
- ❌ Usar *likewise* para un dato que no es semejante → ✅ usa *in addition / moreover*.
- ❌ *The teacher, as well as the students, **are** here.* → ✅ *The teacher, as well as the students, **is** here.* (el verbo concuerda con el primer sustantivo; *as well as* no forma sujeto plural).

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Conector | Matiz en español | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| also | también (semejanza) | Bats also use echoes. |
| too / as well | también (final de frase) | She speaks French, too. |
| as well as | así como, además de | wheat as well as corn |
| besides | además (de) | Besides, it was late. |
| in addition (to) | además (de) | In addition, costs rose. |
| moreover / furthermore | además (formal) | Moreover, the data were incomplete. |
| likewise / similarly | de igual modo | Similarly, birds migrate. |
