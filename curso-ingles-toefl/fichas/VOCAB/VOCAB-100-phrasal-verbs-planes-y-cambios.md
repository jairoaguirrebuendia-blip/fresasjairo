# Phrasal verbs de planes y cambios (VOCAB-100)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 9 "Phrasal Verbs", pp. 120–187 (entradas alfabéticas).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer los phrasal verbs que en los diálogos del TOEFL indican que un plan se cancela, se pospone, fracasa o cambia.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Un phrasal verb es un verbo distinto de su verbo base (*put* ≠ *put off*). En Listening, la opción correcta suele **parafrasear** el phrasal verb con un verbo de una palabra (*called off* → *cancelled*; *put off* → *postponed*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`call off / put off + objeto` (separables) · `back out (of)` · `fall through` (intransitivo) · `end up + V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Call off** = cancel: "The concert had to be called off because the singer went down with a bad case of flu." (p. 130)
- **Put off** = postpone: "The concert's been put off until next month…" (p. 168)
- **Back out** = fail to keep an arrangement or promise: "He backed out two days before the holiday…" (p. 120)
- **Fall through** = be unsuccessful: "The plans fell through when planning permission was refused." (p. 144)
- **End up** = become or do something unplanned: "…so we ended up going to Turkey instead." (p. 143)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ Confundir *put off* (posponer) con *put out* (apagar, p. 169) o *put on* (subir de peso, p. 168).
- ❌ *We ended up to go* → ✅ *We ended up going.*
- ❌ *Turn down* = solo "bajar volumen": también = **reject an offer** ("They offered her the job, but she turned it down", p. 187).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: vocab
  pregunta: "Because of the storm, the outdoor graduation ceremony was put off until Sunday. 'Put off' is closest in meaning to:"
  opciones: ["cancelled", "postponed", "moved indoors", "shortened"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Put off … until Sunday' = posponer. 'Cancelled' sería 'called off'."
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: Are you still going to Mexico in June? Woman: No, the trip fell through when my roommate backed out. What does the woman mean?"
  opciones: ["She fell during the trip to Mexico.", "Her roommate went to Mexico without her.", "The trip was moved to a later month.", "The trip did not happen because her roommate decided not to go."]
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "'Fall through' = el plan fracasó; 'back out' = echarse atrás de un compromiso."
```
