# Tipos de multi-word verbs (VOCAB-104)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 9 "Phrasal Verbs" (pp. 118–119) y Chapter 10 "Prepositional Verbs" (pp. 192–193).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir los tres tipos de multi-word verbs y saber dónde va el objeto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
El libro: "a multi-word verb is still a verb… 'Get' and 'get up' are two different verbs" (p. 118). Tres tipos:
1. **Prepositional verbs** (verbo + preposición): *look after, believe in, wait for*. **Inseparables**: siempre tienen objeto y este va después.
2. **Phrasal verbs** (verbo + adverbio): *look up, turn on, pick up*. Los transitivos suelen ser **separables**; con pronombre, el pronombre va en medio (*look it up*).
3. **Phrasal-prepositional verbs** (verbo + adverbio + preposición): *get on with, put up with, look forward to, run out of*. **Inseparables**.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `look after the baby` ✅ / `look the baby after` ❌
- `look up the number` ✅ / `look the number up` ✅ / `look it up` ✅ / `look up it` ❌
- `We ran out of fuel` ✅ / `We ran out of it` ✅

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- "Who is looking after the baby?" (p. 118)
- "You can look up my number in the telephone directory." (p. 119)
- "I look forward to meeting you." (p. 119)
- "We must say 'look after the baby'. We cannot say 'look the baby after'." (p. 192)
- "I won't put up with your attitude." (p. 193)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I look forward to meet you.* → ✅ *I look forward to meeting you.* (*to* es preposición → gerundio)
- ❌ *Turn off it.* → ✅ *Turn it off.*
- ❌ *Wait me* → ✅ *Wait for me.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| look after | cuidar | inseparable |
| look up | buscar (información) | separable |
| look forward to | esperar con ilusión | + -ing |
| run out of | agotarse | inseparable |
