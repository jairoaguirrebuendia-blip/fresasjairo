# Phrasal verbs de estudio y trabajo (VOCAB-102)

> **Fuente:** Spoken English: Flourish Your Language — Carmen, R. (ed.), Chapter 9 "Phrasal Verbs", pp. 122–166.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Dominar los phrasal verbs que más aparecen en diálogos de campus (tareas, clases, investigación, carga de trabajo).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Los diálogos del TOEFL ocurren casi siempre en una universidad estadounidense: entregar trabajos, ponerse al día, investigar, abandonar un curso. Estos verbos permiten entender la situación y la paráfrasis de la respuesta.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`hand in + N` · `keep up with + N` · `fill + persona + in on + N` · `be snowed under (with work)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Hand in** = submit work: "I handed my homework in late as usual." (p. 155)
- **Keep up with** = move at the same rate / stay up to date: "It's hard to keep up with all the latest improvements…" (p. 157)
- **Fill in on** = give information: "…could you fill me in on what happened." (p. 145)
- **Be snowed under** = have too much work: "We're completely snowed under at work…" (p. 122)
- **Brush up** = improve a skill quickly: "She took a two-week course to brush up her Spanish…" (p. 127)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *pick up* solo = recoger: también = **learn quickly** ("She picked up Spanish in six months", p. 166) y **improve** ("Sales picked up", p. 166).
- ❌ *be up to* = "estar arriba": ✅ *He's not up to the job* = no es lo bastante bueno (p. 122); *What are those kids up to?* = ¿qué traman? (p. 122).
- AmE: *brush up **on** your Spanish* es más común que *brush up your Spanish*.

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Man: Can you come to the movies tonight? Woman: I wish I could, but I'm snowed under with lab reports. What does the woman mean?"
  opciones: ["The weather is too bad to go out.", "She already saw the movie.", "She has too much work to go.", "She finished her lab reports early."]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'Be snowed under' = tener demasiado trabajo; no tiene nada que ver con el clima (trampa literal)."
- tipo: vocab
  pregunta: "I missed Monday's lecture. Could you fill me in on what the professor said? 'Fill me in on' means:"
  opciones: ["write down", "complete a form about", "repeat loudly", "tell me about"]
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "'Fill someone in on something' = informar a alguien de lo que se perdió."
```
