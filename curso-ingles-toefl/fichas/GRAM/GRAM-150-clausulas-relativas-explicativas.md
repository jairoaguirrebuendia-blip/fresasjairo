# Cláusulas relativas explicativas (GRAM-150)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 35, §272(4–5), pp. 358–359; §274, pp. 362–363.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar relativas que añaden información (con comas) y los patrones *quantifier + of whom/which*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- La **adding clause** da información extra; si se quita, la oración sigue teniendo sentido. Va entre **comas** (o rayas/paréntesis). Típica del estilo escrito formal.
- Solo **who, whom, whose, which** — **nunca that** — y el pronombre **no se puede omitir**.
- Preposición delante (*on which*) o al final (*which he spends … on*).
- **Cuantificador + of whom / of which**: *all of which, some of which, one of whom, most of whom* (no *all of them* con coma: eso sería comma splice).
- **Connective clause**: cuenta lo que pasó después (*I shouted to the man, who ran off*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`N, who/which + V…, …` · `…, some/all/one + of + whom/which + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Einstein, **who** failed his university entrance exam, discovered relativity.
- Tim's hobby is photography, **on which** he spends most of his spare cash.
- The police received a number of bomb warnings, **all of which** turned out to be false alarms.
- In the chair lift were two people, **one of whom** was slightly injured.
- The driver of the car, which hit a tree, was killed. (solo había un coche)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Mount Everest, that is in Nepal, …* → ✅ *Mount Everest, **which** is in Nepal, …*
- ❌ *He has three sons, all of them are doctors.* → ✅ *…, **all of whom** are doctors.*
- ❌ *Paris, is the capital of France, …* → ✅ *Paris, **which** is the capital…*
- ❌ *dozens of channels, some of that…* → ✅ *…, **some of which**…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| most of whom | la mayoría de los cuales | personas |
| none of which | ninguno de los cuales | cosas |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The expedition included twelve scientists, ____ had worked in Antarctica before."
  opciones: ["most of them", "most of whom", "most of which", "who most"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Tras coma, para personas: 'most of whom'. 'most of them' crearía dos cláusulas unidas solo por coma."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The Amazon River, {A|that} carries more water than any other river, {B|flows} {C|through} {D|several} countries."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "En una relativa explicativa (entre comas) no se usa 'that': 'which carries'."
```
