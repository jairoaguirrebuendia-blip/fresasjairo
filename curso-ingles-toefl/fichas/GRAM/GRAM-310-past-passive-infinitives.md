# Past infinitive (to have + pp) and passive infinitive (to be / to have been + pp) (GRAM-310)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 9 — Infinitives (Part 4), Grammar Notes 7 — Simple, Past, and Passive Infinitives, p. 147.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir entre infinitivo simple, pasado (*to have + pp*) y pasivo (*to be / to have been + pp*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Infinitivo simple: misma franja temporal que el verbo principal. Infinitivo pasado *to have + pp*: acción anterior. Pasivo presente: *to be* (o *get*) + pp; pasivo pasado: *to have been + pp*, para acción/estado anterior al verbo principal.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`to + base | to have + past participle | to be + past participle | to have been + past participle`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I expected you **to call**.
- You seem **to have forgotten** the report that was due today.
- Arnie appears **not to have understood** what you said.
- The work is supposed **to be finished** by tomorrow.
- The work was **to have been done** before now.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *You seem to has forgotten…* → ✅ *to have forgotten*
- ❌ *Arnie appears to have understand…* → ✅ *to have understood* (participio)
- ❌ *They expect to invite.* (si ellos son los invitados) → ✅ *They expect to be invited.*
- ❌ *The work is supposed to finished.* → ✅ *to be finished*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be supposed to | se supone que, deber | The work is supposed to be finished |
| appear / seem + to have pp | parecer haber… |  |
