# Must, mustn't, can, can't (GRAM-606)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheets 26 "My ideal world", 27 "Challenges" y 28 "Written and unwritten rules around the world", teacher's notes (s/p).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar obligación (*must*), prohibición (*mustn't, can't*), permiso (*can*) y habilidad (*can, could*), con la forma modal + verbo base.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **must** = obligación · **mustn't** = prohibición (no es "no hace falta": eso es *don't have to*).
- **can / can't** = permiso / prohibición (reglas, leyes).
- **can / could** = habilidad presente / pasada.
- Todos van seguidos del **verbo base sin to** y no llevan -s en 3.ª persona.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`subject + must/mustn't/can/can't/could + base verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- A good friend must lend me her clothes and listen to my problems.
- A good friend mustn't tell anybody my secrets or forget my birthday!
- You can get a driving licence when you're 15 in New Zealand — but you can't drive after 10 o'clock in the evening.
- (Worksheet 27) Los alumnos resumen *what they could and couldn't do* en los retos.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She musts go.* → ✅ *She must go.*
- ❌ *You must to wear…* / *can to drive* → ✅ *must wear* / *can drive*
- ❌ Confundir *mustn't* (prohibido) con *don't have to* (no es necesario)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| driving licence | licencia de manejo | británico (*driver's license*) |
| rules and regulations | normas | |
| be able to | poder (habilidad) | sustituto de *can* en otros tiempos |
