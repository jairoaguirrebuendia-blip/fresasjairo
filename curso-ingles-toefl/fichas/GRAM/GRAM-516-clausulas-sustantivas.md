# Noun clauses: that, what, whether y orden S + V (GRAM-516)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter III (Day 23) "Noun Clauses / Noun Clauses Function", pp. 126–129.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer cláusulas sustantivas como sujeto, objeto o complemento y construirlas con orden de afirmación.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- A **noun clause** does the job of a noun: **subject**, **direct object**, **object of a preposition**, or **predicate noun** (after *be*).
- Introducers: *that, what, whatever, who, whoever, where, when, why, how, whether, if*.
- Inside a noun clause use **statement word order** (subject + verb), never question order: *I know where **she lives***.
- A noun clause as subject takes a **singular** verb (*What you are doing is…*).
- *that* can be omitted when the clause is an object (*I assume they will…*), but not when it begins the sentence as subject.
- With **it** as an expletive, the real subject is the noun clause at the end: *It is not clear whether…*.
- Use **whether** (not *if*) at the start of a subject clause and after a preposition.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`That/What/Whether + S + V + verb (singular)…` · `S + V + wh-word/that/whether + S + V` · `It + be + adj + that/whether + S + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- What you are doing is detrimental to your health. (subject)
- I remember that you promised you would never leave. (object)
- The message was that we should surrender. (predicate noun)
- I assume they will attack our right flank. (*that* omitted)
- It is not clear whether they will vote for me. → Whether they will vote for me is not clear.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Do you know where can I stay?* → ✅ *Do you know where I can stay?*
- ❌ *I wonder what is his name.* → ✅ *I wonder what his name is.*
- ❌ *That the earth is round were…* → ✅ *…was…* (cláusula sujeto = singular)
- ❌ *If the plan works is uncertain.* → ✅ *Whether the plan works is uncertain.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whether (or not) | si (o no) | sujeto / tras preposición |
| whoever | quienquiera que | sujeto de su cláusula |
| expletive *it* | *it* introductorio | sujeto aparente |

  explicacion: "Una cláusula sustantiva sujeto se trata como singular → 'was'."

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** "Do you know where can stay for the night?" (p. 127; falta el sujeto y usa orden de pregunta → *where I can stay*); "We discussed about what type of clothing…" (p. 127; *discuss* no lleva *about*); "Adalyn is focused in…" (p. 127; *focused on*); "to whoever is in duty" (p. 127; *on duty*); "Please tell her that I love her?" (signo de interrogación erróneo); escribe "A word in opposition" por *apposition* (p. 128).
- El libro exige *whether* (y no *if*) tras *ask, know, wonder…* (p. 129); en inglés actual *if* es correcto en esos casos (*Ask her if she has finished*). *Whether* es obligatorio al inicio como sujeto, tras preposición y antes de un infinitivo.
