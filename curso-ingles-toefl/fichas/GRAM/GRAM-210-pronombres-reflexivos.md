# Reflexive pronouns (GRAM-210)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 20 "Reflexive pronouns", p. 23 (ver también Units 53–54, pp. 56–57).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *myself, yourself, himself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves* cuando el objeto es el mismo que el sujeto, o para énfasis, y no usarlos donde el español sí pone "se".

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Formas: *myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself / ourselves, yourselves, themselves* (dos formas para la 2.ª persona). No existen *hisself* ni *theirselves*.
- Se usan como objeto (directo, indirecto o de preposición) cuando se refiere al mismo sujeto de la cláusula: *I blame myself.*
- Énfasis: *The town itself...*, *I myself...*; al final = sin ayuda: *She had printed the card herself.*
- *by + reflexivo* = solo / sin ayuda (= *on my own*).
- **No** se usan con verbos de acciones que uno se hace normalmente a sí mismo (*shave, wash, dress*), a diferencia del español.
- Tras *with* (= acompañado de) y con lugares se usa pronombre personal: *He would have to bring Judy with him.*
- *each other / one another* **no** son reflexivos (acción recíproca).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Subject + verb + reflexive (same person)`
- `by + reflexive` = alone / without help

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He introduced himself to me.
- I was thoroughly ashamed of myself.
- I myself have never read the book.
- She was certain she could manage by herself.
- I usually shave before breakfast.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *They enjoyed theirselves.* → ✅ *They enjoyed **themselves**.*
- ❌ *She washed herself very quickly and rushed downstairs* (correcto pero poco natural) → ✅ *She washed very quickly...*
- ❌ *You should have your notes in front of yourself.* → ✅ *...in front of **you**.*
- ❌ *The two leaders congratulated themselves* (si significa uno al otro) → ✅ *...congratulated **each other**.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| by oneself | solo, sin ayuda | = on one's own |
| make fools of themselves | hacer el ridículo | |
| each other / one another | mutuamente | recíproco |
