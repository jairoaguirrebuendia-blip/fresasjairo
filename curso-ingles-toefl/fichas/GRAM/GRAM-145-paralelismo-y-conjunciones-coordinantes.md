# Paralelismo y conjunciones coordinantes (GRAM-145)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 2, §13, p. 13; Chapter 30, §244–245, pp. 323–324.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Mantener la misma forma gramatical en los elementos unidos por *and, or, but* y por los pares correlativos.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- *and / or* unen elementos del **mismo tipo**: sustantivos, adjetivos, adverbios, verbos, cláusulas (*a cold and windy day*; *smoothly, quietly and very efficiently*). En listas, la conjunción va solo antes del último.
- **Correlativas** — cada parte va seguida de la **misma categoría**:
  - *both A and B* (enfático de *and*)
  - *not only A but (also) B*
  - *either A or B* (enfático de *or*)
  - *neither A nor B* (negativo, más formal que *not … or*)
- *as well as / in addition to / besides* son preposiciones → + sustantivo o **gerundio**.
- Para el examen: verbo con verbo, -ing con -ing, infinitivo con infinitivo, sustantivo con sustantivo, adjetivo con adjetivo.
- Concordancia con *either…or / neither…nor*: con el elemento más cercano (ver GRAM-100).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`X, Y(,) and Z` (misma forma) · `both + X + and + X'` · `not only + X + but also + X'` · `either X or X'` · `neither X nor X'`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The work went smoothly, quietly and very efficiently.
- Gene Tunney was **both** a boxer **and** a Shakespeare scholar.
- He was **not only** a boxer, **but** he **also** lectured at Yale University.
- You can go **either** right **or** left.
- A deaf-mute is someone who can **neither** hear **nor** speak.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The job requires typing, filing, and to answer phones.* → ✅ *…typing, filing, and **answering** phones.*
- ❌ *She is intelligent, hard-working, and has ambition.* → ✅ *…and **ambitious**.*
- ❌ *Both the cost and how long it takes…* → ✅ *Both the cost and the **duration**…*
- ❌ *neither … or* → ✅ *neither … **nor***
- ❌ *not only fast but also it is cheap* → ✅ *not only fast but also **cheap***

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Benjamin Franklin was a printer, a scientist, {A|an inventor}, and {B|he was} {C|a} {D|diplomat}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Falta de paralelismo: la lista es de sustantivos → 'a printer, a scientist, an inventor, and a diplomat'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The new material is not only lighter than steel ____."
  opciones: ["but also stronger", "but it is also strong", "and stronger also", "but also strength"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'not only + comparativo' exige 'but also + comparativo' (lighter / stronger)."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Regular exercise helps people {A|reduce} stress, {B|sleep} better, and {C|maintaining} a {D|healthy} weight."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Serie de bare infinitives tras 'help people': reduce, sleep, and maintain."
```
