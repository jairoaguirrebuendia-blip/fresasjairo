# Adverbios de enlace y puntuación (GRAM-148)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 25, §216, pp. 276–277; Chapter 7, §56, pp. 69–72.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir conjunciones (unen cláusulas) de adverbios de enlace (no las unen) y evitar oraciones mal unidas.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Linking adverbs** relacionan con la oración anterior: *however, nevertheless, therefore, consequently, as a result, furthermore, moreover, in addition, otherwise, instead, likewise; firstly, in conclusion, in other words, for example, after all, anyway*.
- **No son conjunciones**: dos cláusulas principales deben unirse con **conjunción** (*and, but, so*) **o** separarse con **punto / punto y coma**.
- *Shakespeare wrote plays. He also acted.* / *Shakespeare wrote plays; he also acted.* / *…plays, and he also acted.* — pero **NOT** *He wrote plays, he also acted.* (comma splice).
- Linking adverbs suelen llevar coma: *There wasn't much to eat, however.*
- Comas: tras una cláusula adverbial inicial; no antes de un infinitivo de propósito; no en noun clauses ni en relativas especificativas; sí en relativas explicativas.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`S + V. However, S + V.` · `S + V; therefore, S + V.` · `S + V, and/but/so + S + V.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- When Beethoven was fourteen, he was forced to give lessons… **However**, he still found time to take a few violin lessons.
- If you pay the bill in full within 25 days you won't be charged interest. **Otherwise** you are charged interest…
- Shakespeare wrote plays; he also acted on the stage.
- NOT *He wrote plays, he also acted.*
- The country had suffered greatly during the war. **Furthermore/Moreover**, it had no money.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The data were incomplete, therefore the study was delayed.* → ✅ *…incomplete; therefore, the study… / …incomplete, so the study…*
- ❌ *Moreover of the cost…* → ✅ *In addition to the cost…* (preposición) / *Moreover, it costs…*
- ❌ Usar *however* para unir dos cláusulas con coma.

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| moreover / furthermore | además | formal |

```quiz
  explicacion: "Tras punto y coma se necesita un adverbio de enlace de contraste: 'nevertheless'. 'although' y 'despite' no funcionan así."

  pregunta: "Water expands when it freezes, ____ pipes sometimes burst in winter."
  opciones: ["therefore", "so", "however", "moreover"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Con coma, solo una conjunción coordinante puede unir dos cláusulas: 'so'. 'therefore' produciría un comma splice."
```
