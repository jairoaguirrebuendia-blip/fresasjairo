# Singular nouns, plural nouns and collective nouns (GRAM-207)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 14 "Singular and plural", p. 17.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer sustantivos que solo existen en singular o en plural (en cierto significado) y los colectivos, para concordar verbo y pronombre.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Singular nouns**: solo en singular con determinante; unos con *the* por ser únicos (*the sun, the sky, the world, the future*), otros con *a* (*a wash, a walk, a rest, a chance*).
- **Plural nouns**: solo en plural en ese significado (*clothes, troops, goods, expenses, feelings*). *Police* es plural aunque no termine en *-s*.
- Objetos de **dos partes** (*trousers, scissors, glasses, jeans*) son plurales; para uno se dice *a pair of*.
- **Colectivos** (*army, audience, committee, family, government, staff, team, public*): verbo y pronombre en singular o plural según se vea el grupo como unidad o como individuos.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `These scissors are...` · `a pair of + plural noun (+ singular verb)`
- `The police are/were...`
- `collective noun + singular verb (unidad) / plural verb (individuos)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The sun was shining.
- His clothes looked terribly dirty.
- The police were informed immediately.
- These scissors are sharp.
- Our family isn't poor any more. / My family are perfectly normal.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The police was informed.* → ✅ *The police **were** informed.*
- ❌ *This trousers is too long.* → ✅ *These trousers **are** too long.* / *This pair of trousers is...*
- ❌ *a clothe* → ✅ *a piece of clothing / an item of clothing*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| goods | mercancías | solo plural |
| expenses | gastos | solo plural |
| staff | personal | colectivo |
| a pair of | un par de | objetos de dos partes |
