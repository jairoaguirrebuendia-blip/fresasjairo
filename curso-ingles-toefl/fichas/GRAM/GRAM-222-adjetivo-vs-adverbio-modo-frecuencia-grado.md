# Adjectives vs adverbs; adverbials of manner, frequency and degree (GRAM-222)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Units 38, 39, 41, 43 y 73 (pp. 41, 42, 44, 46, 76).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir adjetivo o adverbio según lo que se modifica (sustantivo/verbo copulativo vs verbo de acción/adjetivo), y colocar bien los adverbios de frecuencia y *so/such, too/enough*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Adverbio de modo** = adjetivo + *-ly* (*careful → carefully*); cambios: *gentle → gently, easy → easily, automatic → automatically, true → truly, full → fully, public → publicly*.
- Adjetivos en *-ly* (*friendly*) no forman adverbio → *in a friendly way*.
- Igual forma adjetivo/adverbio: *fast, hard, late*. *hardly* (= apenas) y *lately* (= últimamente) tienen otro sentido.
- *good* (adj.) → *well* (adv.); *well* = adjetivo de salud.
- Tras **verbos copulativos** (*be, become, feel, get, look, seem, smell, sound, taste, remain, stay*) va **adjetivo**, no adverbio: *Sue felt happy*.
- Orden habitual tras el verbo/objeto: **modo – lugar – tiempo**.
- **Frecuencia/probabilidad** (*always, often, never, probably*): antes del verbo principal, después del auxiliar/modal, después de *be*. *a lot* va al final; *ever* en preguntas, negativas, *if* y tras superlativo.
- **Grado**: *very, extremely, fairly, rather, quite* + adj/adv. *so* + adj/adv o *so many/much/few/little*; *such* + *a* + (adj) + sustantivo singular o + plural. *too* + adj/adv (= más de lo debido); **enough va después** del adj/adv.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `action verb + adverb` · `link verb + adjective` · `adverb + adjective`
- `aux + always/never + main verb` · `be + always`
- `so + adj` · `such + a + adj + noun` · `adj + enough` · `too + adj`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He is a good dancer. / He dances well.
- He smiled at me in a friendly way.
- He is always careful with his money.
- There was such a noise we couldn't hear.
- He didn't work quickly enough.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The soup tastes deliciously.* → ✅ *...tastes **delicious**.*
- ❌ *She sings beautiful.* → ✅ *She sings **beautifully**.*
- ❌ *a remarkable fast runner* → ✅ *a **remarkably** fast runner* (adverbio modifica adjetivo)
- ❌ *enough old* → ✅ *old **enough***
- ❌ *so a big house* → ✅ ***such** a big house*
- ❌ *He works hardly.* (= apenas trabaja) → ✅ *He works **hard**.*

| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| hardly ever | casi nunca | |
| fairly / rather | bastante | |
| link verb | verbo copulativo | + adjetivo |
