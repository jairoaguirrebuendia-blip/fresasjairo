# Participial phrases y dangling modifiers (GRAM-517)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 10 reglas 10, 12–13, pp. 64–65; Day 25 "Participle verbal / Four kinds of Participles / Dangling participle", pp. 135–136, 142; Day 27 "Gerunds (A)", pp. 147–148.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar frases participiales correctamente y detectar modificadores que no se refieren al sujeto lógico.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- A **participle** is a verb form used as an adjective: **present participle** (-ing, active meaning), **past participle** (-ed/-en, passive meaning), **perfect participle** (*having + pp*, action completed before the main action).
- A participial phrase at the start of a sentence must refer to the **subject** of the main clause. If it does not, it is a **dangling participle / dangling modifier**.
- The same applies to *preposition + gerund* openers (*After receiving…, Upon arriving…*).
- Fix: make the doer the subject of the main clause, or turn the phrase into a full clause (*After I worked harder, …*).
- Keep modifiers close to the words they modify.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`V-ing phrase, + subject who does V…` · `V-ed/-en phrase, + subject that receives V…` · `Having + pp, + subject…`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He complained to the waiter that he was given a bent fork.
- Having finished his homework, Lance called his teacher.
- ❌ *Working harder, my supervisor gave me a nod of approval.* → ✅ *After I worked harder, the supervisor gave me a nod of approval.*
- ❌ *After receiving the notice, the deposit was withdrawn.* → ✅ *Upon receiving the notice, I withdrew the deposit.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Walking along the beach, the sunset looked beautiful.* → ✅ *Walking along the beach, we saw a beautiful sunset.*
- ❌ *Frightening by the noise, the baby cried.* → ✅ *Frightened by the noise…* (significado pasivo)
- ❌ *Written in 1850, the author…* → ✅ *Written in 1850, the novel…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| dangling modifier | modificador "colgante" | sin sujeto lógico |
| present / past participle | participio presente / pasado | activo / pasivo |
| perfect participle | having + pp | anterioridad |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ by the loud noise, the baby began to cry."
  opciones: ["Frightening", "Frightened", "Having frightened", "To frighten"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El bebé recibe la acción (fue asustado) → participio pasado 'Frightened'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Walking} along the beach, {B|the sunset} {C|looked} {D|spectacular} to us."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Modificador colgante: el atardecer no camina. El sujeto debe ser quien camina: 'we saw a spectacular sunset'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ the documents carefully, the lawyer signed the contract."
  opciones: ["Having read", "Read", "Having been read", "To have reading"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Acción activa completada antes de firmar, con el abogado como sujeto → 'Having read'."
```
