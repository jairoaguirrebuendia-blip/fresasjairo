# Too, too much/many, (not) enough (GRAM-609)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 33 "A good place to live", teacher's notes (s/p). Repaso en Progress check 36–40b.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar exceso e insuficiencia con *too* y *enough*, colocándolos en la posición correcta.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **too + adjetivo** (*too far, too noisy*)
- **too much + incontable / too many + contable plural** (*too many cinemas, too many cars*)
- **adjetivo + enough** (*sunny enough*) — *enough* va **después** del adjetivo…
- …pero **enough + sustantivo** (*enough theatres*) — **antes** del sustantivo.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `too + adj` · `too much/many + noun`
- `(not) + adj + enough` · `(not) enough + noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I think that Oxford's too far from the sea and it's not sunny enough.
- There are too many cinemas and there aren't enough theatres.
- There are too many young people in Oxford and it's too noisy.
- There are too many cars in the city centre.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *enough sunny* → ✅ *sunny enough*
- ❌ *theatres enough* → ✅ *enough theatres*
- ❌ *too much cars* → ✅ *too many cars*
- ❌ *very hot to drink* (con sentido de imposibilidad) → ✅ *too hot to drink*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| too | demasiado | sentido negativo |
| (not) enough | (no) suficiente | |
| facilities | instalaciones, servicios | *features and facilities of a place* |

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Many early settlers left the region because there was not ____ to grow crops."
  opciones: ["water enough", "enough water", "too much water", "enough of water"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Enough va antes del sustantivo: 'enough water'. 'Too much water' contradice el sentido (no podían cultivar por falta de agua)."
```
