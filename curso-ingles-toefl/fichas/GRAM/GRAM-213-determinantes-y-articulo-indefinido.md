# Determiners and a/an (GRAM-213)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 23 "Determiners", p. 26; Unit 26 "'A' and 'an'", p. 29.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el determinante según el tipo de sustantivo (singular contable, plural, incontable) y usar *a/an* correctamente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- El determinante va al **inicio** del noun group, antes de números y adjetivos.
- **Específicos** (el oyente sabe cuál): *the; this, that, these, those; my, your...*
- **Generales** (primera mención o en general): *a, an, another, any, each, every, few, many, much, little, several, some...*
- Con **singular contable**: *a, an, another, any, each, either, every, neither, no*.
- Con **plural**: *all, both, few, many, several, some, more, most...*
- Con **incontable**: *all, any, enough, less, little, more, most, much, no, some*.
- **Nunca** con incontables: *a, an, another, both, each, either, every, few, many, neither, several*.
- *a* ante **sonido** consonante (*a university, a European*), *an* ante sonido vocal (*an hour, an honest man*).
- *a/an* tras *be* para profesiones o para describir: *She is a model and an artist.*
- Plural sin determinante = afirmación general (*I love dogs*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`determiner + (number) + (adjective) + noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I met the two Swedish girls in London.
- There was little applause.
- He became a school teacher.
- The information was contained in an article on biology.
- Many adults don't listen to children.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She is doctor.* → ✅ *She is **a** doctor.*
- ❌ *an university* → ✅ ***a** university* (sonido /j/)
- ❌ *a hour* → ✅ ***an** hour*
- ❌ *several information* → ✅ *some information*
- ❌ *every students* → ✅ *every student*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| specific determiner | determinante específico | the, this, my |
| general determiner | determinante general | a, some, many |
| a hundred / a half | cien / la mitad | *a* = one |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Marie Curie, {A|who} won two Nobel Prizes, was {B|an} {C|unusually} gifted scientist and {D|a} honorable person."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "'honorable' empieza con sonido vocal (la h es muda): 'an honorable person'. 'an unusually' es correcto."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "After the storm, ____ houses in the village needed new roofs."
  opciones: ["much", "every", "several", "a little"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'houses' es plural contable: 'several'. 'much' y 'a little' son para incontables; 'every' exige singular."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
Casi todos los determinantes funcionan también como pronombres, excepto *the, a, an, every, no* y los posesivos; en su lugar se usa *one, none, each*: *There are none left.*
