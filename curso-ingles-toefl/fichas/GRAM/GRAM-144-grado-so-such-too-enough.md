# Grado: so, such, too, enough (GRAM-144)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 25, §212, pp. 270–273; Chapter 14, §123(4), p. 154; §126(3), p. 157.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Colocar correctamente los adverbios de grado, sobre todo *enough*, *too* y *so/such*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **very / extremely / rather / fairly** + adjetivo o adverbio. Con adjetivos "extremos" (*impossible, delighted, useless, perfect*) se usa *completely / absolutely / quite* (no *very useless*).
- **enough** va **después** del adjetivo/adverbio (*warm enough, quickly enough*) pero **antes** del sustantivo (*enough money*).
- **too** = exceso (negativo): *too small (for me)*. **too / enough + to-infinitive**; con sujeto propio: *too small for the whole family to eat in*.
- **so + adj/adv**; **such + (a/an) + (adj) + N**: *such a tall girl* (no *a so tall girl*). *too/as/so + adj + a + N*: *too short a piece*.
- Antes de comparativo: *much, a lot, a bit, slightly* (no *very*). Antes de superlativo: *by far, easily*.
- *quite*: "bastante" con adjetivos graduables, "completamente" con extremos (*quite impossible*). *not quite* = casi.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`adj/adv + enough (+ to V1)` · `enough + N` · `too + adj (+ for sb) + to V1` · `such + a + adj + N` · `so + adj`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Are you warm **enough**? / I'm not rich enough. / I haven't **enough money**.
- The coffee was **too hot to drink**.
- This rucksack isn't **big enough to get** everything in.
- She's **such a tall girl**. NOT *a so tall girl*
- This tin opener is **completely useless**. NOT *very useless*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The ice is not enough thick to walk on.* → ✅ *…not **thick enough**…*
- ❌ *It was so a difficult test.* → ✅ *It was **such a** difficult test / **so** difficult a test.*
- ❌ *very more expensive* → ✅ *much more expensive*
- ❌ *too much expensive* → ✅ ***much too** expensive / too expensive*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave

|---|---|---|
| hardly / scarcely | apenas | grado negativo |
