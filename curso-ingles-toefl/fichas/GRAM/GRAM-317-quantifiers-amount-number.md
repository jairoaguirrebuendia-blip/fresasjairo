# Quantifiers with count/non-count nouns; amount vs. number (GRAM-317)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 12 — Quantifiers (Part 5), Grammar Notes 1–3 — Definition of Quantifiers; Quantifiers + Nouns; Amount, Number, p. 198.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Combinar cada cuantificador con el tipo de sustantivo correcto y distinguir *amount of* (incontable) de *number of* (contable).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Con singular contable: one, each, every. Con plural contable: two, both, a couple of, several, a few, few, many, a great many, a number of. Con incontable: a little, little, much, a great deal of, amount of, a bit of. Con ambos: no, any, some, enough, a lot of, lots of, plenty of, most, all. *Amount* + incontable; *number* + contable.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`each/every + singular noun | many/several/a number of + plural noun | much/a great deal of/amount of + non-count noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Each student** in the class has a computer.
- **A number of students** in the class come from other countries.
- I had **a great deal of difficulty** getting to class today.
- **The amount of stress** in people's lives seems to be growing.
- **The number of mistakes** I made on the exam was incredible.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The amount of students has increased.* → ✅ *The number of students…*
- ❌ *Every students has a laptop.* → ✅ *Every student has…*
- ❌ *the number of traffic* → ✅ *the amount of traffic*
- ❌ *The number of students have increased.* → ✅ *has* (*the number of* = singular; *a number of* = plural)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| a great deal of | una gran cantidad de | incontable, formal |
| a great many | muchísimos | contable, formal |
| plenty of | de sobra | ambos tipos |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The ____ of species living in tropical rain forests is enormous."
  opciones: ["amount", "number", "much", "deal"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*Species* es contable → *the number of*. *Amount* es para incontables."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Every} {B|students} in the program {C|receives} a laptop {D|at} orientation."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*Every* va con sustantivo contable singular: *every student* (el verbo *receives* ya está en singular)."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Researchers spent a great deal of ____ analyzing the data."
  opciones: ["times", "hours", "time", "days"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "*A great deal of* solo va con incontables: *time*. Con *hours/days* haría falta *a great many* o *many*."
```
