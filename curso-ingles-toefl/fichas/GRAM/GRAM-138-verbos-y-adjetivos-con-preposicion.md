# Verbos y adjetivos con preposición (GRAM-138)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 28, §233–237, pp. 308–316.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Memorizar las preposiciones que "dependen" de verbos, adjetivos y sustantivos frecuentes, y detectar preposiciones sobrantes.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- La preposición tras un verbo es sobre todo **idiomática**: *depend on, consist of, belong to, apply for, benefit from, cope with, insist on, participate in, refer to, rely on, suffer from, succeed in*.
- **Verbos SIN preposición** (objeto directo): *accompany, answer, approach, discuss, enter, influence, lack, marry, reach, resemble, request, seek, suit* (*NOT entered into the room*). Pero el **sustantivo** sí la lleva: *her entry into the room, their control over the city*.
- **Verbo + objeto + preposición:** *spend money on, invite us to, regard … as*.
- **Adjetivo + preposición:** *afraid of, aware of, capable of, dependent on, different from/to, famous for, interested in, responsible for, similar to, superior to, typical of, accustomed to*.
- **Sustantivo + preposición:** *effect on, influence on, increase in* (*an increase of ten per cent*), *difference between, alternative to, substitute for, relationship with/between*.
- A veces cambia el significado: *think about* (reflexionar) / *think of* (venir a la mente, opinión); *annoyed at* (algo) / *annoyed with* (alguien).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`V + prep + objeto` · `V + objeto` (discuss, enter, reach…) · `adj + prep` · `N + prep`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The price **depends on** when you travel. / The flat **consists of** four rooms.
- Elizabeth Taylor **entered** the room. NOT *She entered into the room.*
- We were **discussing** house prices.
- The new law has had some **effect on** people's behaviour.
- a town **famous for** its history / **responsible for** our safety

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *They discussed about the problem.* → ✅ *They **discussed** the problem.*
- ❌ *The results depend of the weather.* → ✅ *…depend **on**…*
- ❌ *She resembles to her mother.* → ✅ *She **resembles** her mother.*
- ❌ *an increase of crime* (tema) → ✅ *an increase **in** crime*
- ❌ *similar with* → ✅ *similar **to***

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| consist of | consistir en | ≠ "consist in" en español |
| be capable of | ser capaz de | + -ing |
| have an effect on | tener efecto en | effect (sust.) vs affect (verbo) |
| an increase in | un aumento de (algo) | of + cifra |
