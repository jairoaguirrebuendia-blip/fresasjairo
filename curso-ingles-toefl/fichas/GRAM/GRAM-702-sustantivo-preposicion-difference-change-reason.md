# Noun + dependent preposition: difference, change, cause, reason (GRAM-702)

> **Fuente:** English Synonyms and Antonyms — Fernald, Part I, notas "Prepositions" de DIFFERENCE, CHANGE (n.), CAUSE, REASON (n.), NECESSITY, ANALOGY, PROGRESS, DESIRE, BARRIER y ACQUAINTANCE.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer la preposición que exige cada sustantivo académico frecuente y detectar la preposición equivocada.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
| Sustantivo | Preposición(es) según el texto | Uso |
|---|---|---|
| difference | **between** A and B; **among** (varios); **in** (aspecto); A different **from** B (*incorrectly to*, dice Fernald) | *a difference in character* |
| change | **from** X **to/into** Y; **in** (aspecto); **for** the better; **of** X to Y | *a change in quality* |
| cause | **of** (lo causado); **for** (motivo para actuar) | *the cause of the disaster; cause for interference* |
| reason | **for** (hoy general); *of* (arcaico) | *the reason for a thing* |
| necessity / need | **of**; **for** (acción) | *a necessity for action* |
| analogy | **between** A and B; **of/to**; **with** | *the analogy of sound to light* |
| progress | **in** (campo); **toward** (meta); **from** X **to** Y | *progress in virtue; toward perfection* |
| desire | **for** (hoy general); *of* (antiguo) | *a desire for excellence* |
| barrier | **to** (algo que se impide); **against**; **between** | *a barrier to progress* |
| acquaintance | **with** (un tema o persona) | *acquaintance with a subject* |

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`the difference between A and B` · `a change in + aspect` · `the cause of + effect` · `the reason for + noun/-ing` · `a need for + noun` · `progress in/toward` · `a barrier to + noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Difference between the old and the new; differences among men; a difference in character.
- We have made a change for the better; the change from winter to spring.
- The cause of the disaster; cause for interference.
- The progress of truth; progress in virtue; toward perfection.
- A barrier to progress, against invasion; a barrier between nations.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *the difference of A and B* → ✅ *the difference **between** A and B*.
- ❌ *the reason of his absence* → ✅ *the reason **for** his absence*.
- ⚠️ *a change of clothes / of government* (sustitución) vs. ✅ *a change **in** temperature* (variación de un aspecto: lo más común en textos científicos).
- ❌ *a barrier for trade* → ✅ *a barrier **to** trade*.
- ❌ *the cause for the fire* (cuando es lo que lo provocó) → ✅ *the cause **of** the fire*.

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "High tariffs can act as a barrier ____ international trade."

- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Scientists {A|still} debate the {B|main} reason {C|of} the {D|sudden} extinction of the dinosaurs."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "En inglés actual se dice 'the reason for'; 'reason of' es arcaico."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The {A|most} obvious difference {B|of} the two species {C|is} the {D|shape} of their beaks."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'The difference between A and B': between the two species."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Modernización:** Fernald distingue *the reason of* (explicar algo) y *the reason for* (hacer algo), y acepta *the desire of fame*. Hoy ambos usos con *of* son arcaicos: usa *reason for* y *desire for*.
- *Different from* es la forma estándar (y la del TOEFL); *different than* es común en EE. UU. informal; *different to*, en Reino Unido. Fernald ya marca *to* como incorrecto.
