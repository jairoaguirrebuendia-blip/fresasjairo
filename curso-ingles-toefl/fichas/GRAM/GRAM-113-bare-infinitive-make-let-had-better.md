# Bare infinitive: make, let, had better (GRAM-113)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 14, §127, p. 158.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Saber cuándo el infinitivo va **sin to** y no confundir *make/let* con *force/allow/get*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Modal + base sin to:** *can, should, must, could…* (pero *ought to, have to, be able to, be allowed to, be going to* sí llevan *to*).
- **had better, would rather/sooner, rather than** + base.
- **make, let, have + objeto + base**. En cambio **force, allow, get** + objeto + **to**-infinitive.
- **Verbos de percepción** (*see, hear, watch, feel*) + objeto + base (acción completa).
- En **pasiva**, el patrón con base pasa a **to**: *The men were seen to leave…* (igual: *was made to…*).
- Tras *except / but* (= excepto): *I do everything except cook.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`make/let/have + obj + V1` · `force/allow/get + obj + to V1` · `be made/seen + to V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The official **made me fill in** a form. / The official **forced me to fill in** a form.
- The headmaster **let the pupils go** home early.
- We**'d better not be** late. / I'd rather **have stayed** at home.
- Someone **saw the men leave** the building. / The men **were seen to leave** the building.
- You've done nothing **but grumble** all day.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The teacher made us to rewrite the essay.* → ✅ *…made us **rewrite**…*
- ❌ *You had better to leave now.* → ✅ *You had better **leave**.*
- ❌ *They let him to go.* → ✅ *They let him **go**.*
- ❌ *He was made apologize.* → ✅ *He was made **to apologize**.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| had better | más vale que | + base, sin to |
| would rather | preferiría | + base |
| let | dejar, permitir | + obj + base |
