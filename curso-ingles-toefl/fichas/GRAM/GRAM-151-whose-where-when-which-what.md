# Whose, where, when, which (oración) y what (GRAM-151)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 35, §275, p. 363; §278–281, pp. 365–366.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar los relativos especiales y no confundir *what* con *which/that*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **whose + N** = posesivo (personas y también países, organizaciones, cosas): *people whose cars were damaged*. Sin artículo tras *whose* (*NOT whose the courage*). Alternativa con cosas: *the rules of which*.
- **where** (lugar), **when** (tiempo), **why** (tras *reason*). *where* = *preposición + which*: *the house where I lived = the house (that) I lived in*. Con comas no se omiten ni se cambian por *that*.
- **, which** puede referirse a **toda la cláusula anterior** (con coma; no *that* ni *what*): *…, which doesn't surprise me*.
- **what** = *the thing(s) that*; **no lleva antecedente**: *NOT the coat what I wanted*.
- **whoever, whatever, whichever** = no importa quién/qué/cuál (*who* solo no sirve aquí).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`N + whose + N + V` · `place + where + S + V` · `…, which + V` (toda la oración) · `what + S + V` (sin antecedente)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Madame Tussaud, **whose** waxworks are a popular attraction, died in 1850.
- The house **where** I used to live has been knocked down.
- The team has lost all its matches, **which** doesn't surprise me.
- I couldn't find **what** I wanted. NOT *the coat what I wanted*
- **Whoever** designed this building ought to be shot.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The city which I was born…* → ✅ *The city **where** I was born / **in which** I was born…*
- ❌ *a writer who his novels…* → ✅ *a writer **whose** novels…*
- ❌ *Everything what he said* → ✅ *Everything **that** he said*
- ❌ *He failed, what surprised everyone.* → ✅ *…, **which** surprised everyone.*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Florence is the city ____ the Renaissance began in the fourteenth century."
  opciones: ["which", "where", "whose", "what"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Relativo de lugar sin preposición → 'where' (= in which)."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Scientists still cannot fully explain {A|what} {B|causes} some birds to migrate, {C|what} makes the topic {D|fascinating}."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Para referirse a toda la cláusula anterior se usa ', which': 'which makes the topic fascinating'. El primer 'what' es correcto (= the thing that)."
```
