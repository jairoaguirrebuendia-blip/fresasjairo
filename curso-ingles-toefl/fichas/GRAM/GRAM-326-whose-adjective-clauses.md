# Whose (possessive relative pronoun) (GRAM-326)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 14 — Adjective Clauses: Introduction (Part 6), Grammar Notes 4 — Whose in Adjective Clauses, p. 234.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *whose* + sustantivo para indicar posesión, con personas y con cosas, sin omitirlo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*Whose* introduce una cláusula de posesión y reemplaza a *his/her/its/their* + sustantivo. Sirve para personas y cosas. No puede omitirse.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + whose + noun + (subject) + verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Ken is the man. + We met his wife. = Ken is the man **whose wife** we met.
- It's a theory **whose origins** go back many years.
- Harvey, **whose house** we're renting, is a lawyer. NOT *Harvey, house we're renting, is a lawyer.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *the man who's car we borrowed* → ✅ *whose car* (who's = who is)
- ❌ *Ken is the man who his wife we met.* → ✅ *whose wife we met*
- ❌ *Today I got my results, whose were good.* → ✅ *which were good* (*whose* necesita un sustantivo detrás)
- ❌ *Harvey, house we're renting…* → ✅ *Harvey, whose house…* (no se omite)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whose | cuyo/cuya/cuyos | también para cosas: a theory whose origins… |
