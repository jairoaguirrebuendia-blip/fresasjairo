# Modals: can, may, should, must + have + pp (GRAM-510)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 11 "English Modals", pp. 71–74.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar cada modal con su significado y siempre seguido de la forma base (o de *have + participio* para el pasado).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Modals are auxiliaries: **modal + base form** (no *to*, no *-s*, no *-ed*, no *-ing*). Excepción: *ought **to***, *have to*.
- **can / could**: ability, possibility, permission; *could* = general past ability; *be able to* for other tenses.
- **may / might**: permission, possibility; *may have + pp* = past possibility.
- **should / ought to**: mild obligation/advice, also probability; *should have + pp* = obligation **not** fulfilled.
- **must**: strong necessity or strong deduction (*must be*); *must have + pp* = deduction about the past.
- **have to**: necessity in any tense (*had to, will have to*); negatives/questions with *do/does/did* (*You don't have to*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`modal + base form` · `modal + have + past participle` (pasado) · `ought to / have to + base form`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Luzviminda could speak Spanish when she was young.
- The bus may arrive late.
- Lito may have gone to school or he may have gone home.
- You should have finished your book report.
- This bag must be yours: it has your name on it.
- She had to take the exam. · Do I have to do it?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Students should to review…* → ✅ *should review*
- ❌ *She can speaks three languages.* → ✅ *can speak*
- ❌ *He must went home.* → ✅ *He must have gone home.*
- ❌ *You ought study.* → ✅ *You ought to study.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| must have + pp | debe de haber… | deducción pasada |
| should have + pp | debería haber… | no se hizo |
| don't have to | no es necesario | ≠ mustn't (prohibido) |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The lights in the office are off; everyone ____ home already."
  opciones: ["must go", "must have gone", "should go", "can have gone"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Deducción lógica sobre el pasado (already) → 'must have + participio'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Students {A|should} {B|to} review their notes {C|before} the {D|exam}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Los modales van seguidos de forma base sin 'to': 'should review'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Long before the compass was invented, sailors ____ navigate by the stars."
  opciones: ["could", "can", "must have", "should"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Habilidad general en el pasado → 'could + base'. 'must have' exigiría participio ('must have navigated')."
```
