# Verbos que piden solo to-infinitive o solo gerund (GRAM-108)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 14, §120–121, pp. 147–149.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Saber qué verbos van seguidos de `to + base` y cuáles de `-ing`, porque el TOEFL lo evalúa constantemente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **+ to-infinitive:** afford, agree, aim, appear, arrange, attempt, choose, claim, decide, demand, expect, fail, hope, learn, manage, offer, plan, prepare, pretend, promise, refuse, seem, tend, threaten, wish…
- **+ gerund:** admit, anticipate, appreciate, avoid, can't help, consider, delay, deny, dislike, enjoy, escape, finish, give up, imagine, involve, keep (on), mention, mind, miss, postpone, practise, put off, quit, recommend, resent, resist, risk, suggest, tolerate…
- *seem, appear, happen, tend, turn out, prove* + to-infinitive dicen algo sobre la verdad o el modo del hecho.
- *advise, recommend, allow, permit*: + gerund sin objeto; **+ objeto + to-infinitive** con objeto.
- Con algunos verbos (*refuse*) no se puede usar una cláusula con *that*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`decide / manage / refuse + to + V1` · `avoid / enjoy / suggest + V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I decided **to take** a taxi. / I suggested **taking** a taxi.
- People are refusing **to pay** the new tax.
- Brian agreed **to pay** half the cost. NOT *Brian accepted to pay half.*
- I advised **taking** a taxi. / I advised the girls **to take** a taxi.
- The debate turned out **to be** very interesting.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The company avoided to raise prices.* → ✅ *…avoided **raising** prices.*
- ❌ *She managed finishing on time.* → ✅ *She managed **to finish**…*
- ❌ *He suggested us to leave.* → ✅ *He suggested **that we leave** / suggested **leaving**.*
- ❌ *They enjoy to work outdoors.* → ✅ *They enjoy **working** outdoors.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| can't help + -ing | no poder evitar | *I can't help feeling depressed* |
| put off + -ing | posponer | phrasal verb + gerund |
| tend to | tender a | + infinitive |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Early sailors could not afford ____ far from the coast without reliable navigation tools."
  opciones: ["venturing", "to venture", "ventured", "to venturing"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'afford' va seguido de to-infinitive: 'afford to venture'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Many patients {A|delay} {B|to see} a doctor {C|until} their symptoms {D|become} severe."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'delay' pide gerundio: 'delay seeing'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- *afford* y *stand* (= tolerar) suelen ir con *can/could/be able to*: *I can't stand sitting around.*
- *help* puede omitir *to*: *We all helped (to) put up the tent.*
