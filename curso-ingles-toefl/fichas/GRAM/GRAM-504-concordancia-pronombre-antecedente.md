# Pronoun–antecedent agreement (GRAM-504)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter II (Day 4), reglas 4–7 sobre pronombre y antecedente, pp. 37–38.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Hacer que el pronombre concuerde con su antecedente en número, persona y género.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- A pronoun must agree with its **antecedent** (the noun it replaces) in **person, number and gender**.
- Compound antecedent with *and* (different people/things) → plural pronoun (*their*).
- If the two nouns joined by *and* refer to the **same** person → singular.
- *all, any, some, none*: plural when they refer to a number of items; singular when they refer to a quantity/mass.
- **Collective nouns** (*class, jury, team, audience, family*): singular (*its*) when the group acts as a unit; plural (*their*) when members act individually.
- Cosas, instituciones, animales en general → *it/its*, nunca *their* si el antecedente es singular.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`singular antecedent → he/she/it, his/her/its` · `plural or X and Y → they, their`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The coach and the players agreed on their game plan.
- Francis and his father postponed their trip.
- All were waiting their turn. (all = plural)
- There is no bread in the box. All of it has been eaten. (all = singular)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The museum reopened, and their new wing is popular.* → ✅ *its new wing*
- ❌ *Each of the girls brought their own lunch* (en TOEFL tradicional) → ✅ *her own lunch*
- ❌ *The jury announced their verdict* (actúa como unidad) → ✅ *its verdict*
- ❌ *Bees… it* → ✅ *Bees… they* (antecedente plural)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| antecedent | antecedente | sustantivo al que se refiere el pronombre |
| collective noun | sustantivo colectivo | jury, team, class, audience |
| compound antecedent | antecedente compuesto | X and Y |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "After three days of discussion, the jury finally reached ____ verdict."
  opciones: ["its", "it's", "their's", "theirs"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "El jurado actúa como unidad → singular, posesivo 'its' (sin apóstrofo)."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The {A|museum} reopened {B|after} renovations, and {C|their} new wing {D|attracts} many visitors."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El antecedente 'museum' es singular y no persona → 'its new wing'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Error del libro (no copiar):** el ejemplo "The judge and executioner abhor his duties" (p. 38) ilustra bien el pronombre singular, pero el verbo debe ser **abhors** (sujeto singular: una sola persona).
- En inglés actual se acepta *they/their* singular para personas de género no especificado (*everyone… their*); en ítems TOEFL ITP lo más seguro es buscar la concordancia formal y que haya un único error claro.
