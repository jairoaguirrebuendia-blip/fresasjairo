# Modals: form, ability and permission (GRAM-235)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Units 79–80 (pp. 82–83), Unit 83 "Ability", p. 86, Unit 84 "Permission", p. 87.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Aplicar las reglas formales de los modales (base form, sin *-s*, nunca dos modales seguidos) y usar *can/could/be able to/be allowed to/may*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Forma (Units 79–80):**
- Modales: *can, could, may, might, must, ought, shall, should, will, would*. Siempre **primera palabra** del verb group.
- Seguidos de **forma base** (excepto *ought to*). Una sola forma: sin *-s*, sin *-ing*, sin *-ed*.
- Negación: *not* inmediatamente después (*cannot* en una palabra); preguntas: modal antes del sujeto.
- **Nunca dos modales juntos**: *He will can come* ✗ → *He will be able to come*. Sustitutos: *be able to, have to, be likely to, be allowed to*.

**Habilidad (Unit 83):**
- *can* (presente/futuro), *could* (habilidad general en el pasado).
- Logro en una ocasión concreta del pasado: **was/were able to** (no *could*); en negativo sí *couldn't*.
- *could have* + V3 = podía haberlo hecho pero no lo hizo (a veces crítica).
- *be able to* obligatorio tras otro modal o en forma *-ing*/participio/infinitivo.

**Permiso (Unit 84):** *can / can't*; pasado *could / couldn't*; *may* (formal); pedir: *Can I...? / Could I...?* (más cortés) */ May I...?*; *be allowed to* para hablar del permiso (no para pedirlo) y tras otro modal.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `modal + base form` · `ought to + base`
- `will / might / would + be able to / have to / be allowed to + base`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I must leave fairly soon.
- She ought to go straight back to England.
- After treatment he was able to return to work.
- Nobody else will be able to read it.
- You're not allowed to use calculators in exams.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She cans swim.* → ✅ *She can swim.*
- ❌ *He must to leave.* → ✅ *He must leave.*
- ❌ *You will can do it.* → ✅ *You will **be able to** do it.*
- ❌ *They could to finish.* → ✅ *They could finish.*
- ❌ *He could escape from the fire* (una ocasión) → ✅ *He **was able to** escape...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be able to | poder (capacidad) | sustituto de *can* |
| be allowed to | tener permiso | |
| be unable to | no poder | |
