# Present perfect continuous: duración y proceso (GRAM-412)

> **Fuente:** The English Tenses: Practical Grammar Guide — Williams, P., The Present — The Present Perfect Continuous, secciones 26–28, s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *have/has been + V-ing* para la duración de una acción que sigue (o acaba de terminar con efectos visibles) y distinguirlo del present perfect y del past continuous.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **26.1 Showing duration:** con *for*, *since* o *How long…?*; la acción empezó en el pasado y continúa.
- **26.2 Process of recent actions:** acción reciente que afecta al presente; importa el **proceso**, no la terminación (*I can't drive; I've been drinking*).
- **27. vs past continuous:** past continuous = terminó / anclado en el pasado; present perfect continuous = sigue o es reciente y con efectos ahora.
- **28. vs present perfect:** perfect continuous = proceso / periodos cortos / actividad repetida en curso; present perfect = **total** / largo plazo / acción ocasional (*He has lived here for decades* vs *He has only been living here for two months*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`have/has (+ not) + been + V-ing (+ for/since)` · `How long have you been + V-ing?`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He **has been sleeping since** yesterday.
- **How long have you been watching** TV for?
- He was thinking about joining the army. / He **has been thinking** about joining the army. (still)
- I have read about plants. / I **have been reading** about plants. (ongoing)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I am waiting here for two hours.* → ✅ *I have been waiting here for two hours.*
- ❌ *She has been study since 8 a.m.* → ✅ *She has been studying…*
- ❌ *I have been knowing her for years.* (estado) → ✅ *I have known her for years.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| How long…? | ¿cuánto tiempo…? | pregunta típica |
| all morning / all day | toda la mañana / todo el día | duración |
| lately / recently | últimamente / recientemente | proceso reciente |
