# Contraste: but, however, although (GRAM-611)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 35 "Meal-times", teacher's notes (s/p).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Unir ideas que contrastan con el conector adecuado y la puntuación/estructura correcta.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **but**: conjunción coordinante, une dos cláusulas en una oración (*…, but…*).
- **However**: adverbio conector; normalmente inicia una **nueva oración** (o va tras punto y coma) seguido de coma.
- **Although**: conjunción subordinante; introduce una cláusula dependiente que necesita una principal. No se combina con *but* en la misma oración.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Clause, but clause.`
- `Clause. However, clause.`
- `Although + clause, main clause.` / `Main clause although + clause.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Stephanie and Marius both eat something for breakfast. However, they don't eat something hot.
- Stephanie peels an apple before she eats it, but Marius doesn't.
- Stephanie eats bread with her main meal, but she doesn't put butter on her bread.
- Although Marius likes meat, he doesn't eat it every day.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Although he likes meat, but he doesn't eat it.* → ✅ quitar *but*
- ❌ *He likes meat, however he doesn't eat it.* (coma sola) → ✅ *He likes meat. However, he…* / *…meat; however, he…*
- ❌ *Although of the rain…* → ✅ *Although it rained…* / *Despite the rain…* (*although* + cláusula)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| but | pero | coordinante |
| however | sin embargo | adverbio conector |
| although | aunque | subordinante + cláusula |
| both | ambos | *They both eat…* |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: Worksheet 35, entrevistar a dos compañeros sobre hábitos de comida y escribir cinco oraciones comparativas.

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ the desert receives very little rain, many species of plants and animals live there."
  opciones: ["However", "But", "Although", "Despite"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Se necesita una conjunción subordinante seguida de cláusula (sujeto + verbo) → Although. 'Despite' exige sustantivo; 'However' y 'But' no subordinan."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Although} the experiment {B|was carefully planned}, {C|but} the results {D|were disappointing}."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'Although' y 'but' no se usan juntos para la misma relación: sobra 'but'."
```
