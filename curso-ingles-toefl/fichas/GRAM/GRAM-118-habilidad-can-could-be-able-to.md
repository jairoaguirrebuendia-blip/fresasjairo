# Habilidad: can, could, be able to (GRAM-118)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 12, §98, pp. 124–125; §99, pp. 125–126.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar habilidad en presente, pasado y futuro, y distinguir *could* (habilidad general) de *was able to* (logro concreto).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **can** (presente), **could** (pasado, habilidad general). *cannot* se escribe junto.
- **be able to** para formas que *can* no tiene: perfect (*hasn't been able to*), infinitivo (*to be able to*), -ing (*being able to*), futuro (*will be able to*).
- Pasado de **un logro concreto** → **was/were able to / managed to / succeeded in -ing**, no *could*. En negativas y preguntas valen ambos.
- **could have + V3** = tuvo la capacidad u oportunidad pero no lo hizo.
- **would**: situación irreal o petición cortés; **would like** (+ to-inf) = querer; **would rather + base / + cláusula en pasado** = preferir.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`can/could + V1` · `was able to / managed to + V1` · `will be able to + V1` · `would rather + V1 (than V1)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Nicola **could** play chess when she was six.
- He **hasn't been able to** work for some time.
- The injured man **was able to** walk to a phone box. NOT *The injured man could walk to a phone box.*
- He **could have walked** there, but he decided to wait.
- I'd rather walk than hang around for a bus. / The guide would rather we kept together.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Next year she can drive.* (habilidad futura) → ✅ *…she **will be able to** drive.*
- ❌ *After hours of effort, they could rescue the miners.* → ✅ *…they **were able to / managed to** rescue…*
- ❌ *I'd rather to stay.* → ✅ *I'd rather **stay**.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| manage to | lograr | logro concreto |
| succeed in + -ing | tener éxito en | preposición + gerund |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Despite the storm, the pilot ____ land the plane safely."
  opciones: ["could", "was able to", "can", "could have"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Logro concreto en una situación pasada → 'was able to' (no 'could')."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Since the accident, he ____ walk without assistance."
  opciones: ["can't", "couldn't", "hasn't been able to", "isn't able"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Con 'since' se necesita present perfect; 'can' no tiene perfect → 'hasn't been able to'."
```
