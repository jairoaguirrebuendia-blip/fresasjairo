# Present perfect vs past simple; stative verbs (GRAM-228)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 62 "Talking about the present", p. 65; Unit 63 "Talking about the past", p. 66.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir presente simple vs continuo, past simple vs present perfect, usar *used to / would* para hábitos pasados, y no poner verbos de estado en continuo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Presente (Unit 62):** *present simple* para lo general, verdades generales y hábitos; *present continuous* para lo que ocurre ahora o es temporal.
- **Verbos de estado** (no van en continuo): pensamiento (*believe, know, understand, want*), gusto (*like, love, prefer, hate*), apariencia (*seem, resemble*), posesión (*have, own, belong to, contain, include*), percepción (*hear, see, smell, taste*), existencia (*be, consist of, exist*), y *matter, mean, deserve*. Algunos sí con otro sentido: *We're having a party.*

**Pasado (Unit 63):**
- **Past simple**: evento en un momento pasado concreto; **necesita** referencia temporal (explícita o ya establecida).
- Hábitos pasados: *past simple*, *would* o *used to* (*would* no con verbos de estado).
- **Present perfect**: tiempo indefinido con efecto presente, o situación que empezó y continúa (*since/for*); *Have you ever...?*
- Futuro en el pasado: *would, was going to*, past continuous.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `past simple + yesterday / last week / in 1990 / ago`
- `have/has + V3 + since / for / ever / just / recently`
- `used to + base form` · `would + base form` (acciones repetidas)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Water boils at 100 degrees centigrade.
- The new term started last week.
- People used to believe that the world was flat.
- Have you ever stolen anything?
- He has been here since six o'clock.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✅ *I **saw** him yesterday.*
- ❌ *He lives here since 2010.* → ✅ *He **has lived** here since 2010.*
- ❌ *This box is containing books.* → ✅ *This box **contains** books.*
- ❌ *People use to believe...* (pasado) → ✅ *People **used to** believe...*
- ❌ *I am having a car.* → ✅ *I have a car.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| used to | solía | solo pasado |
| ever / never | alguna vez / nunca | present perfect |
| stative verb | verbo de estado | sin -ing |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The theory of continental drift {A|has been proposed} by Alfred Wegener {B|in} 1912, but {C|few} scientists {D|accepted} it at first."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Con fecha concreta en el pasado ('in 1912') se usa past simple: 'was proposed'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Since the invention of the printing press, books ____ much cheaper to produce."
  opciones: ["became", "have become", "become", "were becoming"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Since' + punto de inicio con situación que llega al presente → present perfect 'have become'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Seawater {A|is containing} {B|many} dissolved minerals, {C|including} salt {D|and} magnesium."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Contain' es verbo de estado (posesión): no va en continuo → 'contains'."
```
