# Past perfect con after y because (GRAM-613)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 40 "Work it out" (puzzles de lógica), s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el past perfect para la acción anterior a otra acción pasada, especialmente al explicar causas (*because*) o secuencias (*after, when*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Cuando se narra en pasado y se menciona algo que ocurrió **antes**, ese "pasado del pasado" va en **had + participio**. Es típico en explicaciones: *She knew because she had put sugar in it.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `subject + had + past participle`
- `After/When + subject + had + participle, past simple` · `past simple + because + had + participle`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He had done the journey on his hands.
- The manager of an important bank had just arrived at work one morning when one of his employees came into his office.
- The man was a night guard. He had fallen asleep and this proved that he was not doing his job.
- She had put sugar in the coffee before she complained to the waiter.
- They had let some air out of the tyres.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She had put sugar… before she had complained* (doble past perfect innecesario) → ✅ *…before she complained*
- ❌ *He had fell asleep.* → ✅ *He had **fallen** asleep.*
- ❌ *When I arrived, the film already started.* → ✅ *…the film **had** already started.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| had just + participle | acababa de | *had just arrived* |
| wear out | gastar (por uso) | *He didn't wear out his shoes* |
| complain | quejarse | |

```quiz
  opciones: ["already leave", "had already left", "have already left", "were already leave"]

  pregunta: "The scientist {A|realized} that she {B|has made} an error {C|after} she {D|checked} the data again."

  explicacion: "El error ocurrió antes de 'realized' (pasado) → 'had made', no present perfect."
```
