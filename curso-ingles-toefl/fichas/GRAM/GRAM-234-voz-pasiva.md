# The passive voice (GRAM-234)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 78 "The passive voice", p. 81 (ver también Unit 51, p. 54, y Unit 17 §6, p. 20).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar la pasiva en todos los tiempos y con modales, decidir cuándo usarla y con qué preposición introducir al agente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- La pasiva pone el foco en **quién/qué recibe la acción**: *be* (en el tiempo necesario) + **participio pasado**.
- Continua: *be + being + V3* (*Jobs are still being lost*).
- Con modales: *modal + be + V3*; pasado: *modal + have been + V3*.
- Infinitivo pasivo: *to be + V3*, *to have been + V3*.
- *get* + V3 = pasiva informal (*He got killed*).
- El agente se omite si no se sabe o no importa; si se menciona: **by** (agente) / **with** (instrumento).
- Solo verbos **transitivos** tienen pasiva (no *happen, occur, arrive, die*; tampoco *have* = poseer).
- Con dos objetos, cualquiera puede ser sujeto: *The secretary was given the key / The key was given to the secretary.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `S + be (any tense) + V3 (+ by agent)`
- `modal + be + V3` · `modal + have been + V3`
- `be + being + V3`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Dozens of trees were destroyed.
- It was being done without his knowledge.
- What can be done?
- The car was reported to have been stolen.
- A circle was drawn in the dirt with a stick.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The bridge was build in 1900.* → ✅ *...was **built**...* (participio)
- ❌ *The house is being paint.* → ✅ *...is being **painted**.*
- ❌ *The letter was written from my brother.* → ✅ *...**by** my brother.*
- ❌ *The accident was happened.* → ✅ *The accident **happened**.*
- ❌ *English speaks in many countries.* → ✅ *English **is spoken**...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| agent | agente | introducido por *by* |
| be said / believed to | se dice / se cree que | + to-inf |
| be brought up by | ser criado por | |

  pregunta: "Many new species {A|are} still {B|being discover} in the {C|deep} oceans {D|every} year."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Pasiva continua: 'being' + participio → 'being discovered'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The documents should ____ before the meeting starts."
  opciones: ["be signed", "been signed", "signed", "be sign"]

  explicacion: "Modal + be + participio: 'should be signed'."
