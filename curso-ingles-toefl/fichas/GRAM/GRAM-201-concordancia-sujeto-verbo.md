# Subject–verb agreement (GRAM-201)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Units 2, 13, 14, 15, 18 y 21 (pp. 5, 16, 17, 18, 21, 24).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Hacer concordar el verbo con el núcleo del sujeto (singular/plural), incluso con sustantivos incontables, indefinidos, colectivos y con *there is/are*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Un noun group singular lleva verbo singular; uno plural, verbo plural (Unit 2 §7; Unit 13 §6).
- Los **incontables** llevan verbo singular, aunque terminen en *-s* (*physics, athletics, measles, news*) (Unit 15 §3–4).
- Los **pronombres indefinidos** (*everyone, anybody, nothing...*) como sujeto llevan **siempre verbo singular**, aunque se refieran a varias personas (Unit 21 §3).
- **Colectivos** (*family, team, government, staff*): verbo singular o plural según se vea al grupo como unidad o como individuos (Unit 14 §4). *Police* es siempre plural.
- **There + be**: el verbo concuerda con el sustantivo que sigue; plural ante *a number of, a lot of, a few*; singular si el primer sustantivo es singular o incontable (Unit 18 §3–4).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `singular/uncount subject + singular verb` · `plural subject + plural verb`
- `Everyone / Nobody / Something + verb-s`
- `There is + singular/uncount noun` · `There are + plural noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- His son plays football for the school. / Her letters are always very short.
- Mathematics is too difficult for me.
- Everyone knows that.
- The police were informed immediately.
- There are many reasons for this. / There was a sofa and two chairs.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The address on the letters were wrong.* → ✅ *The address on the letters **was** wrong.* (el núcleo es *address*, no *letters*)
- ❌ *Physics are difficult.* → ✅ *Physics **is** difficult.*
- ❌ *Everybody have a ticket.* → ✅ *Everybody **has** a ticket.*
- ❌ *There is many reasons.* → ✅ *There **are** many reasons.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| collective noun | sustantivo colectivo | *family, team, staff* |
| news, physics, measles | noticias, física, sarampión | incontables con -s |
| a number of | varios | verbo plural |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The results of the new survey {A|on} consumer habits {B|shows} that {C|most} shoppers prefer {D|lower} prices."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El núcleo del sujeto es 'results' (plural); 'of the new survey' es solo un modificador. Debe ser 'show'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "There ____ a large number of applicants for the scholarship this year."
  opciones: ["is", "was", "were", "has been"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Con 'a number of' + plural el verbo va en plural; de las opciones, solo 'were' es plural."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Economics} {B|are} one of the {C|most popular} subjects {D|at} this university."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Economics' es incontable singular (materia de estudio terminada en -s): 'is'."
```
