# Present simple vs present continuous; state verbs (GRAM-103)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 9, §64, pp. 82–85; Chapter 8, §62, pp. 79–81.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir acción en curso/temporal (continuous) de hábito, hecho permanente o estado (simple), y evitar el continuous con verbos de estado.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Present continuous** (`am/is/are + -ing`): acción en desarrollo durante un periodo (aunque no sea en este instante); rutina **temporal**; con *always* = "demasiado a menudo".
- **Present simple**: estados (sentimientos, opiniones, relaciones), rutinas y hábitos **permanentes**, hechos generales, comentarios instantáneos, horarios futuros.
- En preguntas y negativas del present simple: `do/does + base form` (nunca *does he reads*).
- **State verbs** normalmente **no** van en continuous: *belong to, consist of, contain, depend on, deserve, know, matter, own, possess, prefer, seem, believe, understand, need, want…*
- Algunos verbos tienen sentido de acción y de estado: *have* (comer/poseer), *think* (decidir/creer), *taste, look, smell, weigh, measure, cost*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`S + am/is/are + V-ing` (en curso / temporal) · `S + V(-s)` (permanente / estado) · `do/does + not + base`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Hurry up. Your friends **are waiting** for you.
- Bob **works** in Avonmouth. He usually **drives** to work.
- My car's off the road. **I'm travelling** to work by bus this week.
- We are decorating the flat, but NOT *We are owning the flat*.
- We**'re having** lunch now. / We **have** a big kitchen.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *This book is belonging to my sister.* → ✅ *This book **belongs** to my sister.*
- ❌ *Does he reads…?* / *He does not reads* → ✅ *Does he read? / He does not read.*
- ❌ *Water is boiling at 100 °C.* → ✅ *Water **boils** at 100 °C.* (hecho general)
- ❌ *The box is containing old papers.* → ✅ *The box **contains**…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| at present / at the moment | actualmente / en este momento | marcadores de continuous |
| usually, often, every day | normalmente, a menudo, cada día | marcadores de simple |
| consist of | consistir en | verbo de estado |


## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- Uso activo/temporal de algunos verbos de estado: *I'm loving every minute of this holiday.* *Enjoy* es de acción: *I'm enjoying the party.*
- *Mean* (= significar) siempre es estado: *What does this word mean?*
