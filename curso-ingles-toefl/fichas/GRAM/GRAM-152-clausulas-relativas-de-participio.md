# Cláusulas relativas de participio (GRAM-152)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 35, §276–277, pp. 363–364.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reducir relativas a participios (activo/pasivo) e infinitivos, y elegir *-ing* o *-ed* según el sentido.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Participio activo (-ing)** = *who/which + verbo activo*: acción en curso (*people taking photos* = who are taking), estado (*equipment belonging to the club*), acción repetida (*people travelling into London every day*). No para una acción única completa (*NOT The gang stealing the jewels got away*).
- **Participio pasivo (-ed/V3)** = *who/which + be + V3*: *Applications sent in after 23rd March…*, *a body recovered from the river*. Continuo pasivo: *the subject being discussed*.
- Un participio solo puede ir antes del sustantivo (*a ringing telephone*), pero **no** toda la frase.
- **Infinitivo relativo** tras *first, second, next, last, only* y superlativos: *the first country to win*; pasivo: *the first British monarch to be filmed*.
- En el TOEFL suelen aparecer como frases entre comas o tras el sustantivo: *The first British TV commercial, broadcast in 1955, was for toothpaste.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`N + V-ing …` (= who/which + V activo) · `N + V3 …` (= who/which + be + V3) · `the first/last/only + N + to V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Those people **taking** photos over there come from Sweden.
- All the equipment **belonging** to the club is insured.
- Stones **thrown** at the train by vandals smashed two windows.
- The first British TV commercial, **broadcast** in 1955, was for toothpaste.
- William Pitt was the youngest person **to become** Prime Minister.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The bridge building in 1890 is still in use.* → ✅ *The bridge **built** in 1890…*
- ❌ *Students wanted to join must register.* → ✅ *Students **wanting** to join…*
- ❌ *The first woman flew across the Atlantic was…* → ✅ *The first woman **to fly** across…*
- ❌ *A cave was discovered in 1940 contains…* → ✅ *A cave **discovered** in 1940 contains…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| known as | conocido como | participio pasivo frecuente |
| located in | situado en | |
| consisting of | que consiste en | estado → -ing |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The Rosetta Stone, ____ in 1799, helped scholars decode Egyptian hieroglyphs."
  opciones: ["discovering", "was discovered", "discovered", "it was discovered"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Relativa reducida pasiva (= which was discovered) → participio 'discovered'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Animals {A|lived} in polar regions {B|often} have thick layers of fat {C|that} {D|protect} them from the cold."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Reducción activa de 'animals that live' → 'living'. 'lived' sería pasivo y sin sentido aquí."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Valentina Tereshkova was the first woman ____ into space."
  opciones: ["traveled", "traveling", "to travel", "who she traveled"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Tras 'the first + N' se usa infinitivo relativo: 'the first woman to travel'."
```
