# A few/few, a little/little, a bit of; fewer vs. less (GRAM-318)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 12 — Quantifiers (Part 5), Grammar Notes 4 — A Few, Few, A Little, Little, A Bit, p. 199.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir entre *a few/few* (contables) y *a little/little* (incontables), captar su matiz positivo/negativo y usar *fewer* vs *less*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*A few / few* + contables; *a little / little* + incontables. Con *a* el sentido es positivo ('poco pero suficiente'); sin *a* es negativo ('poco, insuficiente'). *Only a few/a little* pierde el sentido positivo. En comparaciones: *fewer* + contables, *less* + incontables; *more* con ambos. *A bit* necesita *of* antes de un sustantivo.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`a few / few / fewer + plural count noun | a little / little / less + non-count noun | a bit of + non-count noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Sayid has **a few** good friends.
- Hank has **few** friends. (a small number, but probably not enough)
- We have **a little** food at home. (probably enough)
- I have **fewer** problems than I used to. I earn **less** money, though.
- I had **a bit of** difficulty myself.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I have less problems now.* → ✅ *fewer problems*
- ❌ *I have a lot fewer stress.* → ✅ *a lot less stress*
- ❌ *She has a little friends.* → ✅ *a few friends*
- ❌ *I had a bit difficulty.* → ✅ *a bit of difficulty*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| a few | unos pocos (suficientes) | positivo |
| few | pocos (insuficientes) | negativo |
| fewer / less | menos | contable / incontable |
