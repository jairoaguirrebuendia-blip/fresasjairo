# Preposición + gerundio (GRAM-111)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 15, §131–132, pp. 161–166.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar siempre **-ing** después de una preposición (incluido *to* cuando es preposición) y reconocer el gerundio como sujeto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Tras **verbo + preposición** (*believe in, insist on, succeed in, object to, rely on*), **adjetivo + preposición** (*good at, interested in, capable of, responsible for, used to*) o **sustantivo + preposición** (*the idea of, success in*) → **gerund**, nunca to-infinitive ni cláusula.
- También tras *before, after, by, without, instead of, despite, in spite of, besides, as well as, on, in*.
- **to preposición:** *look forward to, object to, be/get used to, be accustomed to, be opposed to, in addition to, admit to, confess to* → **-ing**.
- Verbo + objeto + preposición + -ing: *accuse … of, prevent … from, congratulate … on, thank … for, blame … for*.
- Tras preposición no se usa participio pasivo solo: *after being approved*, no *after approved*.
- **Gerund como sujeto** es más normal que to-infinitive: *Keeping a copy of your letters is a good idea.* Con *it* se prefiere to-infinitive.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`preposición + V-ing` · `look forward to / be used to + V-ing` · `V-ing (sujeto) + V singular`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- My husband isn't very good **at cooking**.
- I hope **to see** you soon. / I look forward **to seeing** you soon.
- The article accuses the government **of concealing** important information.
- Please switch off the lights **before leaving**.
- The new drug was put on the market **after being approved** by the government.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She is interested in to study biology.* → ✅ *…interested in **studying**…*
- ❌ *We look forward to hear from you.* → ✅ *…to **hearing** from you.*
- ❌ *Instead of to land at Heathrow…* / *instead of we landed* → ✅ *Instead of **landing**…*
- ❌ *Despite he was tired…* → ✅ *Despite **being** tired…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be capable of | ser capaz de | + -ing |
| be responsible for | ser responsable de | + -ing |
| prevent sb from | impedir que alguien | + -ing |
| on + -ing | al (hacer) | *On turning the corner…* |

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- Algunos admiten ambos con igual sentido: *voted for joining / voted to join*. Otros cambian: *agree with* (opinar) vs *agree to* (decidir); *anxious to* (querer) vs *anxious about* (preocupado).
