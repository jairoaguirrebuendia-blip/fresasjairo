# Who(m)/which/that as objects; omitting the relative pronoun (GRAM-325)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 14 — Adjective Clauses: Introduction (Part 6), Grammar Notes 3 — Objects in Adjective Clauses, p. 233.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar pronombres relativos objeto (*whom, who, that, which*), saber cuándo omitirlos y no repetir el objeto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Como objeto, para personas: *whom* (formal, escrito), *who* o *that*; para cosas: *which* (algo más formal) o *that*. En conversación e inglés informal el pronombre objeto puede omitirse. El verbo de la cláusula concuerda con su propio sujeto, no con el objeto.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + whom/who/that/which/Ø + subject + verb (no object after the verb)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Mr. Pitkin, **whom I mentioned** yesterday, is my boss.
- The test **which I took** was difficult.
- Mrs. Gomez is the woman **I met**. (pronoun omitted)
- The Wangs are the people **that Sally knows**. NOT *…that Sally know.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *the people that Sally know* → ✅ *that Sally knows* (concordancia con *Sally*)
- ❌ *The book that I read it was long.* → ✅ *The book that I read was long.* (objeto repetido)
- ❌ *I have many friends whom mean a lot to me.* → ✅ *who mean* (*whom* solo como objeto)
- ❌ *the man whom lives next door* → ✅ *who lives*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whom | a quien | solo objeto; formal |
| omit | omitir | solo pronombres objeto en cláusulas identificativas |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The candidate ____ the committee selected has ten years of experience."
  opciones: ["whom", "whose", "which", "who he"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Relativo objeto para persona: *whom* (the committee selected him). *Which* es para cosas; *who he* repite el objeto."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The novel {A|that} I {B|borrowed} {C|it} from the library {D|was} fascinating."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "*That* ya funciona como objeto de *borrowed*; sobra *it*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The engineers ____ the company hired last year designed the new bridge."
  opciones: ["who they", "whom", "whose", "which"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Objeto de *hired*, referido a personas → *whom*."
```
