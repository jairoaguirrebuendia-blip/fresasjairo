# Subject–verb agreement II: compuestos, indefinidos, colectivos y cantidades (GRAM-508)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 10 reglas 3–4, 8, 15–16, 20–21, 26–27, pp. 63–67; Day 12 pp. 77–78; Day 13 pp. 79–80.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Decidir si el verbo va en singular o plural con sujetos compuestos, pronombres indefinidos, colectivos, cantidades y sustantivos terminados en -s.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **X and Y** (two different people/things) → plural. If they form one unit or one person → singular.
- **or / nor / either…or / neither…nor / not only…but also** → the verb agrees with the **nearer** subject.
- Always singular: *each, every, everyone, everybody, everything, someone, anybody, nobody, no one, either, neither, one*; also *each/every/many a + noun*.
- Always plural: *both, few, several, many*.
- *some, all, any, none, half, part* (+ *of*): depend on the noun that follows (noncount/mass → singular; plural noun → plural).
- **Collective nouns** (*team, class, family, jury, community*): singular when acting as a unit.
- Amounts of money, time, distance, weight as one quantity → singular (*Four weeks is…*).
- Nouns in -s with singular meaning: *news, mathematics, economics, physics, measles* → singular. *A pair of scissors* → singular.
- Company names and book titles → singular.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Neither A nor B + verb (agrees with B)` · `Each/Everyone + singular verb` · `Half of + noncount → is / + plural → are` · `quantity as a unit + is`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Right: Not only the students but also the teacher is learning.
- The manager and the owner were present in the meeting.
- Each arrives on time. · Everything is in order. · Both are to be blamed.
- Half of the children have eaten. · Half of the cake is left.
- Correct: A pair of scissors is all I need. · Four weeks is a long time to wait for you.
- Correct: Kindles, a company distributing books, has opened many branches in the Philippines.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Neither the manager nor the employees was aware.* → ✅ *…were aware.*
- ❌ *Economics are a popular major.* → ✅ *Economics is…*
- ❌ *Everyone need a ticket.* → ✅ *Everyone needs a ticket.*
- ❌ *Ten miles are too far to walk.* → ✅ *Ten miles is too far…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| neither… nor | ni… ni | concuerda con el más cercano |
| each / every | cada | singular |
| news, physics, measles | noticias, física, sarampión | singulares |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Neither the manager nor the employees ____ aware of the change in schedule."
  opciones: ["was", "is", "were", "has been"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Con 'neither… nor' el verbo concuerda con el sujeto más cercano: 'the employees' (plural) → 'were'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Economics {A|are} one of the {B|most popular} majors {C|at} many {D|universities}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Economics' como disciplina es singular → 'is'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Everyone in the two groups ____ to submit a final project."
  opciones: ["need", "needs", "are needing", "have needed"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Everyone' es siempre singular, aunque la frase 'in the two groups' sea plural → 'needs'."
```
