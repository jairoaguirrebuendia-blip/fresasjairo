# Sentence structure: sujeto, predicado y fragmentos (GRAM-500)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter I (Day 1), "Sentence / Part of Sentences / Sentence Fragment", pp. 12–14.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer si una oración está completa (sujeto + verbo conjugado + idea completa) y detectar fragmentos, el error más frecuente en la sección Structure.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- A **sentence** is a group of grammatically linked words that expresses a **complete thought**.
- Every sentence has a **subject** (who/what the sentence is about) and a **predicate** (what the subject does or is; it contains the finite verb).
- A **sentence fragment** looks like a sentence (capital letter + period) but is only a phrase or a **dependent clause**; it lacks a subject, a verb, or an independent clause.
- Fragments usually sit just before or after the independent clause they belong to; fix them by attaching them to that clause.
- Por función: *declarative* (.), *interrogative* (?), *imperative* (sujeto implícito *you*), *exclamatory* (!).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Subject + finite verb (+ complement)` = independent clause
`Subordinator + S + V` sola = **fragmento** → `Subordinator + S + V, + independent clause`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The lion | roared. (subject | predicate)
- The girl in the blue dress | arrived late.
- ❌ *When we got in the car. We rolled down the windows.* (fragmento)
- ✅ When we got in the car, we rolled down the windows.
- ✅ We rolled down the windows when we got in the car.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The river flowing through three countries.* → ✅ *The river flows through three countries.* (-ing solo no es verbo conjugado)
- ❌ *Because the bridge was closed.* (oración aislada) → ✅ *Because the bridge was closed, drivers took another route.*
- ❌ *The museum it opened in 1990.* → ✅ *The museum opened in 1990.* (sujeto duplicado)
- En Structure, si a la oración le falta el verbo principal, la opción correcta casi nunca es un -ing, un infinitivo ni "which + verbo".

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| subject | sujeto | quien realiza o "es" |
| predicate | predicado | contiene el verbo conjugado |
| sentence fragment | fragmento | oración incompleta |
| independent clause | cláusula independiente | puede ir sola |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The Amazon River ____ through several South American countries."
  opciones: ["flowing", "it flows", "flows", "which flows"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Falta el verbo conjugado. 'flowing' y 'which flows' dejan la oración sin verbo principal (fragmento); 'it flows' duplica el sujeto."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Because} the bridge {B|was closed} for repairs, {C|drivers} {D|taking} a longer route."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "La cláusula principal necesita verbo conjugado: 'drivers took'. 'taking' deja la oración sin verbo principal."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- El libro dice que un fragmento "carece de sujeto y predicado"; en realidad basta con que falte uno de los dos o que sea una cláusula dependiente sin cláusula principal.
- Redacción del libro con errores menores ("strong felling" por *feeling*); los ejemplos citados aquí son correctos.
