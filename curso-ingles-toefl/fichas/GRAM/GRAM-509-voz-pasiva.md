# Passive voice: be + past participle (GRAM-509)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 11 "Verbs: Voice — Active and Passive Voice", pp. 68–69; Day 14 "Complements of Action Verbs", p. 83.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar y reconocer la voz pasiva y saber cuándo el sujeto recibe la acción.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Active voice**: the subject does the action. **Passive voice**: the subject receives the action.
- Passive = a form of **be** (in the needed tense) + **past participle**; the doer can appear with *by*.
- Only **transitive** verbs (verbs with a direct object) can be passive; the object of the active becomes the subject of the passive.
- Use the passive when the action or the receiver is more important than the doer, or the doer is unknown. Prefer the active when it is more direct.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Object(→ subject) + be (am/is/are/was/were/has been/will be…) + past participle (+ by agent)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Passive: The play was written by Shakespeare. → Active: Shakespeare wrote the play.
- Passive: The telephone was invented by Alexander Graham Bell. → Active: Alexander Graham Bell invented the telephone.
- The concert was held in the park. (p. 27)
- Transitive verb: The old man embraced his long lost son. (p. 83)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The bridge was design by…* → ✅ *was designed* (participio)
- ❌ *The house painted by Larry.* (falta *be*) → ✅ *The house was painted by Larry.*
- ❌ *The accident was happened…* → ✅ *The accident happened…* (intransitivo: no hay pasiva)
- ❌ *Many manuscripts are preserving in…* → ✅ *are preserved*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| transitive / intransitive | transitivo / intransitivo | solo transitivos → pasiva |
| by + agent | por + agente | opcional |
| happen, occur, arise, exist | ocurrir, surgir, existir | intransitivos: nunca pasiva |

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** da como ejemplo de pasiva "The house painted by Larry." (p. 68; falta **was**); en la p. 83 presenta "The son was embraced tightly" como ejemplo de verbo intransitivo, cuando es la pasiva de un verbo transitivo; afirma "Never use the passive voice… when the active would be more natural" de forma demasiado tajante: en textos académicos (lecturas TOEFL) la pasiva es muy frecuente.
- El libro trata en la misma sección el modo subjuntivo (pp. 69–71: *If I were a Queen…*); varios de sus ejemplos son incorrectos ("He insists that he talk loud", "(If) he have been"), por eso no se extrajeron.
