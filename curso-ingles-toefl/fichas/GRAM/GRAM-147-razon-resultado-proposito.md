# Razón, resultado y propósito (GRAM-147)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 30, §247, p. 326; Chapter 31, §249–252, pp. 327–331.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar conjunciones de tiempo, causa, resultado y finalidad con la estructura correcta (cláusula vs sustantivo).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Una cláusula adverbial va al inicio (con coma) o al final; puede ser finita, participial (*while waiting*) o elíptica (*when on the road*).
- **Tiempo:** *when, while, as, before, after, since, until, by the time, once, as soon as* (*NOT by the postman comes*). *as* = cambio progresivo (*As we drove further north…*). *no sooner … than*, *hardly … when*.
- **Razón + cláusula:** *because, as, since, seeing (that), now (that)*. **Razón + sustantivo:** *because of, due to, in view of, on account of* (+ *the fact that* para cláusula).
- **Resultado:** *so* (conjunción, inicio de cláusula), *therefore, as a result, consequently* (adverbios); **so + adj/adv (that)**, **such (a) + N (that)**; no *very/too* en este patrón.
- **Propósito:** *to / in order to / so as to* + V1 (negativo *so as not to / in order not to*, nunca *not to* solo); **so that + cláusula** (con *can/could/will/would*), sobre todo si cambia el sujeto. *for + N* (*for some fresh air*); *for + -ing* = función general de un objeto.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`because + S + V` · `because of + N` · `so + adj + that + S + V` · `so that + S + can/could + V` · `in order (not) to + V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I made mistakes **because** I was tired. / The scheme was abandoned **because of** the cost.
- The ground is **so dry (that)** the plants are dying.
- The company borrowed money **(in order) to** finance their advertising.
- I wrote it in my diary **so as not to** forget. / **so that** I wouldn't forget.
- **No sooner had** Martin sat down **than** the phone rang.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *because of it was late* → ✅ *because it was late / because of the delay*
- ❌ *She saved money for buy a car.* → ✅ *…**to buy** / **so that she could buy** a car.*
- ❌ *It was very cold that the lake froze.* → ✅ *It was **so** cold that…*
- ❌ *I took notes not to forget.* → ✅ *…**so as not to** forget.*
- ❌ *Due to he was ill…* → ✅ *Because he was ill… / Due to his illness…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| due to / owing to | debido a | + sustantivo |
| consequently | en consecuencia | adverbio |
| now that | ahora que | razón |
| once | una vez que | tiempo |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Many flights were canceled ____ the heavy snowfall."
  opciones: ["because", "because of", "so that", "since"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sigue un sustantivo ('the heavy snowfall') → preposición 'because of'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Engineers added extra supports to the bridge ____ withstand strong earthquakes."
  opciones: ["so that it could", "in order that", "so as it", "for it"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Propósito con nuevo sujeto (it) → 'so that + sujeto + could + verbo'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The volcano erupted {A|so} violently {B|as} ash {C|fell} on cities hundreds of miles {D|away}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Patrón de resultado: 'so + adverbio + that': 'so violently that'."
```
