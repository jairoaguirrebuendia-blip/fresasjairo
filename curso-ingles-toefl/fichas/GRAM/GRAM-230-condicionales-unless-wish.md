# Conditionals, unless and wish (GRAM-230)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Units 66–68 (pp. 69–71).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Combinar correctamente los tiempos en los cuatro tipos de condicional, usar *unless* / *provided that*, la inversión sin *if* (*Had I known...*) y *wish / if only*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
| Tipo | Cláusula *if* | Cláusula principal | Uso |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | presente / present perfect | presente (o imperativo) | verdades generales, lo habitual |
| 1 | presente / present perfect | *will* + base | posible en el futuro |
| 2 | past simple / past continuous (*were*) | *would* + base | improbable o irreal |
| 3 | past perfect | *would have* + V3 | pasado que no ocurrió |
- **Nunca** *will*, *would* ni *would have* en la cláusula *if* (salvo *will* = "estar dispuesto").
- *If I were you...* para dar consejos.
- Se pueden usar modales en ambas cláusulas (*If he can't come, he will phone*); *should* en la cláusula *if* = más incierto.
- **unless** = *if ... not* (mismos tiempos que *if*); también *as long as, provided (that), providing, so long as, only if*.
- **Inversión formal** sin *if* con *had, should, were*: *Had I known, I would not have done it.*
- **wish / if only**: + past simple (deseo presente), + past perfect (lamento pasado), + *could* (capacidad), + *would* (voluntad de otro).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `If + past perfect, would have + V3` · `Had + S + V3, would have + V3`
- `unless + present, will + base`
- `I wish + S + past simple / past perfect`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- If the light comes on, the battery is OK.
- If I had enough money, I would buy the car.
- If he had realized that, he would have run away.
- You will fail your exams unless you work harder.
- Were it all true, it would still not excuse their actions.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *If I will see you tomorrow, I will give you the book.* → ✅ *If I **see** you tomorrow...*
- ❌ *If I would have seen him, I would have told him.* → ✅ *If I **had seen** him...*
- ❌ *If I was you...* (formal) → ✅ *If I **were** you...*
- ❌ *Unless you don't hurry, you'll miss it.* (doble negación) → ✅ *Unless you hurry...*
- ❌ *I wish I didn't marry him.* (pasado) → ✅ *I wish I **hadn't married** him.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| unless | a menos que | = if not |
| provided (that) | siempre que | |
| if only | ojalá | |
| as long as | mientras, con tal de que | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ the warning signs earlier, the engineers could have prevented the collapse."
  opciones: ["If they noticed", "Had they noticed", "They had noticed", "Would they notice"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Condicional tipo 3 con inversión formal: 'Had they noticed' = 'If they had noticed'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "If the Earth {A|would rotate} more slowly, our days {B|would be} {C|much} {D|longer}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "No se usa 'would' en la cláusula 'if': 'If the Earth rotated more slowly...'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Seeds will not germinate ____ they receive enough water and warmth."
  opciones: ["if", "unless", "provided", "because"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'unless' = 'if ... not': no germinan si no reciben agua. 'if' o 'provided' invertirían el sentido."
```
