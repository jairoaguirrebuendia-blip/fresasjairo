# Word choice: palabras confusas (GRAM-519)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., "List of Troublesome Words", pp. 167–172.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir pares de palabras parecidas que aparecen como error en Written Expression.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **already** (previously) vs **all ready** (everyone/everything ready).
- **almost** (adverb, nearly) vs **most** (superlative of *many/much*).
- **amount** (noncount quantity) vs **number** (countable units); **fewer** (countable) vs **less** (noncount).
- **raise** (regular, transitive: *raise something*) vs **rise** (irregular, intransitive: *rise–rose–risen*).
- **lay** (put/place something: *lay–laid–laid*) vs **lie** (rest/recline: *lie–lay–lain*).
- **precede** (go before) vs **proceed** (go forward, continue).
- **formally** (in a formal manner) vs **formerly** (previously).
- **passed** (past/pp of *pass*) vs **past** (time before now; adjective/preposition).
- **lend** (give temporarily) vs **borrow** (take temporarily); **bring** (toward the speaker) vs **take** (away).
- **their** (possessive) / **there** (place) / **they're** (they are).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`fewer + plural count noun` · `less + noncount noun` · `raise + object` · `rise (no object)` · `lay + object` · `lie (no object)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Already — means previously. / All ready — means everybody or everything is ready.
- Fewer — refers to quantities that can be counted individually. / Less — for quantities not counted individually.
- Raise — move to a higher place; regular verb. / Rise — get up; irregular verb.
- Precede — to go before in place, order, rank or time. / Proceed — to go or move forward.
- Formally — in a formal manner. / Formerly — previously.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Prices have raised.* → ✅ *Prices have risen.*
- ❌ *less cars* → ✅ *fewer cars* · ❌ *a large amount of people* → ✅ *a large number of people*
- ❌ *He laid down to rest.* → ✅ *He lay down to rest.*
- ❌ *Almost students passed.* → ✅ *Most students passed / Almost all students passed.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| rise – rose – risen | subir (algo sube solo) | intransitivo |
| raise – raised – raised | levantar, aumentar algo | transitivo |
| lie – lay – lain | yacer, estar tumbado | intransitivo |
| lay – laid – laid | poner, colocar | transitivo |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The bakery sold ____ cakes this week than last week."
  opciones: ["less", "fewer", "little", "lesser"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'cakes' es contable plural → 'fewer'. 'less' es para no contables."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The price of gasoline {A|has} {B|raised} {C|sharply} {D|since} January."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sin objeto directo, el precio 'sube' solo → verbo intransitivo 'rise': 'has risen'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Despite the rain, the ceremony will ____ as planned."
  opciones: ["precede", "proceed", "preceed", "proceeding"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'proceed' = continuar/seguir adelante. 'precede' = ir antes; 'preceed' no existe."
```
