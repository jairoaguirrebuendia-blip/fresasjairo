# Possibility, probability and modal perfects (GRAM-236)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 81 "Possibility", p. 84; Unit 82 "Probability and certainty", p. 85.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar grados de certeza en presente y pasado con *may/might/could, should, must, can't* y sus formas perfectas (*must have done*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- *can* = algo es posible en general (*Cooking can be a real pleasure*); *cannot / can't* = imposible.
- *could / might / may* = posible pero no seguro (*may* más formal). Negativo: *might not / may not*.
- Pasado posible: *could / may / might have* + V3. *could have* también = era posible, pero no ocurrió.
- *couldn't have* + V3 = imposible que ocurriera.
- *should / ought to* = probable; *should have / ought to have* + V3 = probablemente ocurrió, o se esperaba y no ocurrió.
- *must* = deducción casi segura (afirmativa); su negativo de deducción es **can't** (no *mustn't*).
- *must have* + V3 = casi seguro que ocurrió; *can't have* + V3 = seguro que no ocurrió.
- *will / won't* = certeza futura. Sin modal: *be bound to, be likely/sure/certain to*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `may / might / could + base` (presente/futuro posible)
- `must / can't + base` (deducción presente)
- `must / can't / may / might / could + have + V3` (deducción pasada)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He might not be in England at all.
- You may have noticed this advertisement.
- He didn't have a boat, so he couldn't have rowed away.
- He can't be very old - he's about 25, isn't he?
- We must have taken the wrong road.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *He must have went.* → ✅ *He must have **gone**.*
- ❌ *She mustn't be at home; the lights are off.* (deducción) → ✅ *She **can't** be at home.*
- ❌ *They might arrived.* → ✅ *They might **have arrived**.*
- ❌ *It must be rained last night.* → ✅ *It must **have rained** last night.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be bound to | ser seguro que | |
| must have + V3 | debe de haber | deducción pasada |
| can't have + V3 | no puede haber | |

  pregunta: "That ____ be the right answer; it contradicts all the data we collected."

  explicacion: "Deducción negativa segura → 'can't'. 'mustn't' significa prohibición, no deducción."
