# Verbs and objects (GRAM-224)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 51 "Verbs and objects", p. 54; Unit 52 "Verbs with two objects", p. 55; Unit 55 "Ergative verbs", p. 58.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Saber qué verbos llevan objeto (transitivos), cuáles no (intransitivos) y cómo ordenar dos objetos con *to/for*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Intransitivos** (sin objeto): existencia (*appear, die, happen, remain*), cuerpo (*faint, smile*), movimiento/posición (*arrive, come, fall, go, sit*). **No** se usan en pasiva.
- **Transitivos** (con objeto): *build, buy, enjoy, discuss, blame, warn...* Sí admiten pasiva (excepto *have*).
- Algunos cambian según el significado: *The hare runs* / *She runs a hotel*.
- **Dos objetos**: si el indirecto es corto (pronombre) va primero: *Dad gave me a car*. Si no, *objeto directo + to/for + indirecto*: *He handed his room key to the receptionist*.
  - con **to**: *give, lend, offer, pass, pay, promise, sell, send, show, teach, tell*;
  - con **for**: *book, buy, cook, find, keep, make, prepare, save*.
- **Ergativos** (*open, break, change, increase, improve, begin, stop*): transitivos e intransitivos; el objeto pasa a sujeto: *I broke the glass / The glass broke.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `S + V(intrans.)` · `S + V(trans.) + O`
- `S + V + IO + DO` · `S + V + DO + to/for + IO`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- An awful thing has happened.
- She reported the accident to the police.
- They bought a present for the teacher.
- Suddenly the door opened.
- The glass broke all over the floor.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The accident was happened.* → ✅ *The accident **happened**.* (intransitivo, sin pasiva)
- ❌ *Prices were increased by 5% last year* (si no hay agente) → ✅ *Prices **increased** by 5%.*
- ❌ *She explained me the problem.* → ✅ *She explained the problem **to me**.*
- ❌ *They discussed about the plan.* → ✅ *They discussed the plan.* (transitivo, sin preposición)
