# Cláusulas relativas especificativas (GRAM-149)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 35, §272–273, pp. 357–362.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar relativas que identifican o clasifican (sin comas) con el pronombre adecuado y sin pronombres sobrantes.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **who** = personas; **which** = cosas/ideas; **that** = ambas (en relativas sin comas). *whom* = objeto (formal).
- El pronombre relativo puede ser **sujeto** (*the man who lives on the corner*) u **objeto** (*the actor (who) we saw*).
- **Se puede omitir** el pronombre **solo si es objeto**; si es sujeto, es obligatorio (*That man who was sitting next to Angela…*).
- **Preposición:** al final (informal: *the man (who) I share a flat with*) o delante de **whom/which** (formal: *the person with whom…*); tras preposición **no** se usa *who* ni *that*.
- **No repetir** el sujeto/objeto con un pronombre personal: *NOT a man who he disappeared*, *NOT a body that they found it*.
- Con relativa especificativa el determinante suele ser *the*, no *my*.
- *that* preferido tras *everything, anything, all, superlativos*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`N + who/which/that + V…` (sujeto) · `N + (who/which/that) + S + V` (objeto) · `N + prep + whom/which + S + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The architect **who** designed these flats doesn't live here.
- It's the same actor (who/that) we saw at the theatre.
- The person **with whom** Mr Fletcher shared the flat had not paid his rent.
- a man who disappeared in January — NOT *a man who he disappeared in January*
- This is the worst summer I can remember.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The scientist which discovered…* → ✅ *The scientist **who** discovered…*
- ❌ *The book that I bought it…* → ✅ *The book that I bought…*
- ❌ *The topic in that she is interested* → ✅ *…**in which** she is interested*
- ❌ *Students live on campus pay less.* (falta sujeto relativo) → ✅ *Students **who** live on campus pay less.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whom | a quien | objeto, formal |
| in which / to whom | en el que / a quien | preposición + relativo |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The ancient Egyptians developed a calendar ____ was based on the flooding of the Nile."
  opciones: ["it", "who", "that", "what"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Relativa con antecedente 'calendar' (cosa) y pronombre sujeto → 'that' (o 'which')."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Marie Curie was a physicist {A|whom} work on radioactivity {B|earned} her {C|two} Nobel {D|Prizes}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Relación posesiva ('su trabajo') → 'whose work'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The documents {A|that} the researchers {B|found} {C|them} in the archive {D|were} centuries old."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El pronombre relativo 'that' ya es el objeto; sobra 'them'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
