# All, most, no, none (GRAM-215)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 27 "All, most, no, none", p. 30.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *all, most, no, none* con o sin *of*, y evitar *most the* / *none the* (error ID clásico; en español "la mayoría de los").

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- *all* y *most* + plural o incontable, para hablar en general: *All children should...*, *Most milk is...*
- Si hay determinante específico (*the, my, these*) o pronombre, se usa **most of / none of / all of**: *Most of my friends*, *None of them*.
- *all the* = *all of the* (mismo significado); pero **no** existe *most the* ni *none the*.
- *no* + singular, plural o incontable; *no* no es pronombre → *none (of)*.
- Si la cláusula ya es negativa: *any / any of*, no *no / none of*.
- *none of* + plural: verbo normalmente plural, pero también singular.
- *all* tras el sujeto o el objeto para énfasis: *The band all live together.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `most + plural/uncount noun` (general) · `most of + the/my/these + noun`
- `none of + pronoun / determiner + noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Most good drivers stop at zebra crossings.
- Most of my friends live in London.
- None of those farmers had ever driven a tractor.
- I hadn't eaten any of the biscuits.
- Thank you for all the help you gave me.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Most the students passed.* → ✅ *Most **of the** students passed.* / *Most students passed.*
- ❌ *Most of students...* → ✅ *Most students...* (sin determinante, sin *of*)
- ❌ *No of them came.* → ✅ ***None of** them came.*
- ❌ *I didn't eat none of them.* → ✅ *I didn't eat **any of** them.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| most (of) | la mayoría (de) | *of* solo ante determinante/pronombre |
| none (of) | ninguno (de) | pronombre |
| all of us | todos nosotros | pronombre objeto |
