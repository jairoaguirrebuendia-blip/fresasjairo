# Reducing adjective clauses with be (who is studying → studying) (GRAM-331)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 15 — Adjective Clauses and Phrases (Part 6), Grammar Notes 4 — Reducing Adjective Clauses to Adjective Phrases, p. 252.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reducir una cláusula adjetiva con *be* eliminando el relativo y el verbo *be*, manteniendo las comas si las tenía.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Una frase (*phrase*) no tiene sujeto ni verbo conjugado. Las cláusulas adjetivas con *be* se reducen borrando el pronombre relativo y *be*: queda un participio -ing, un participio pasado o una frase preposicional. Si la cláusula llevaba comas, la frase también. No se reduce cuando el relativo es *whose*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + (who/which/that + be) + V-ing / past participle / prepositional phrase`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Any student **studying abroad** must learn the language of the country. (from *who is studying abroad*)
- She's the student **from Nigeria**. (from *who's from Nigeria*)
- That's the man **in charge of personnel**.
- The Guggenheim Museum, **founded in 1939**, is very popular.
- NOT *We met Professor Mandel, classes are very popular.* (whose clause cannot be reduced)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The museum, was founded in 1939, is popular.* → ✅ *The museum, founded in 1939,…* (se borra también *be*)
- ❌ *The man who in charge of personnel* → ✅ *who is in charge* / *the man in charge*
- ❌ *Professor Mandel, classes are popular,* → ✅ *whose classes are popular* (no se reduce)
- ❌ *The documents were discovered in the attic are old.* → ✅ *The documents discovered in the attic are old.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| phrase vs. clause | frase vs. cláusula | la frase no tiene verbo conjugado |
