# Used to, would y be used to (GRAM-119)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 12, §100, p. 127.

## 🎯 Objetivo
No confundir **used to + base** (hábito pasado) con **be used to + -ing** (estar acostumbrado).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **used to + V1**: hábito o **estado** pasado que ya no ocurre. No tiene presente (*NOT I use to come here now*).
- Negativa/pregunta con **did**: *didn't use to*, *did you use to…?*
- **would + V1**: hábito pasado (acciones, **no estados**).
- **will + V1**: hábito típico presente (*Warm air will rise*).
- **be used to + sustantivo / V-ing** = estar acostumbrado (*to* es preposición). **get used to** = acostumbrarse.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`used to + V1` · `didn't use to + V1` · `be/get used to + N / V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I **used to come** here when I was a child.
- I **used to have** a bicycle, but I sold it.
- There **didn't use to be** so much crime.
- In those days people **would make** their own entertainment.
- We**'re used to living** in the country now.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *People used to traveling by horse.* → ✅ *People **used to travel** by horse.*
- ❌ *She is used to work at night.* → ✅ *She is used to **working** at night.*
- ❌ *Did you used to play?* → ✅ *Did you **use** to play?*
- ❌ *I would have a bicycle.* (estado) → ✅ *I **used to** have a bicycle.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| used to | solía | solo pasado |
| be accustomed to | estar acostumbrado | + -ing |
