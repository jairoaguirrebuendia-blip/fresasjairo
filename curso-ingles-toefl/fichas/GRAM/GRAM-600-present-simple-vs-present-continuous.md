# Present simple vs present continuous (GRAM-600)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 5 "But what are they doing now?", teacher's notes (s/p). Complemento: Worksheet 2 "Talk about routines".

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir entre present simple (estados permanentes, rutinas) y present continuous (acciones temporales, en curso ahora).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Present simple**: estados permanentes y hábitos/rutinas, a menudo con adverbios de frecuencia (*usually, often*).
- **Present continuous**: acción que ocurre **ahora** o es temporal (*now, at the moment*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `subject + verb(-s)` → *She works…*
- `subject + am/is/are + verb-ing` → *She's listening…*

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- What does Anna do? — She's a pilot. What's she doing now? — She's listening to music.
- What do you usually have for breakfast? (Worksheet 2)
- Carlos does his homework in the library. (Worksheet 2)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She is work in a bank every day.* → ✅ *She **works** in a bank every day.*
- ❌ *Look! It rains.* → ✅ *Look! It**'s raining**.*
- ❌ *He usually go…* → ✅ *He usually **goes**…* (-s de 3.ª persona)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| usually / often / always | normalmente / a menudo / siempre | → present simple |
| now / at the moment | ahora / en este momento | → present continuous |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Dr. Lee usually teaches biology, but this semester she ____ a course in chemistry."
  opciones: ["teach", "is teaching", "teaches always", "has teach"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'This semester' marca una situación temporal frente a la rutina (usually teaches) → present continuous."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Most bees {A|lives} in colonies {B|that} {C|are organized} {D|around} a single queen."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Sujeto plural 'Most bees' + hecho general → present simple 'live', sin -s."
```
