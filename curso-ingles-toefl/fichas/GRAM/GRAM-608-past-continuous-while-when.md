# Past continuous con while y when (GRAM-608)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 32 "Strange things happen…" y Progress check 36–40b "Sentence dominoes", s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Combinar una acción larga en progreso (past continuous) con una acción corta que la interrumpe (past simple).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Acción en progreso → **past continuous** (*was/were + -ing*), a menudo con **while**.
- Acción que interrumpe → **past simple**, a menudo con **when**.
- El libro la llama "past continuous for interrupted actions with while and when".

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `While + subject + was/were + -ing, subject + past simple`
- `subject + was/were + -ing + when + subject + past simple`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- While he was looking under the car, he felt a cold wind on his face.
- He was still looking under the car when a police car pulled up.
- I was looking out of the window… when I saw my friend.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *While he looked under the car, a police car was pulling up.* (invierte acción larga/corta) → ✅ *While he was looking…, a police car pulled up.*
- ❌ *When I was see my friend* → ✅ *when I saw my friend*
- ❌ *They was walking* → ✅ *They were walking*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| pull up | detenerse (un vehículo) | phrasal verb |
| to his surprise | para su sorpresa | conector narrativo (Worksheet 31b) |
| suddenly | de repente | conector narrativo |
