# Dependent prepositions and phrasal verbs (GRAM-226)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Units 47–50 (pp. 50–53).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Memorizar las preposiciones que "exigen" ciertos adjetivos, sustantivos y verbos, y usar los phrasal verbs con el objeto en la posición correcta.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Adjetivo + prep.** (Unit 47): *aware of, fond of, accustomed to, used to; afraid/proud/jealous of; good/bad at; pleased/satisfied with; similar/equal/related/married/dedicated to; angry about (algo) / with (alguien)*. *It was clever/kind of you*; *It's difficult for (persona)*.
- **Sustantivo + prep.** (Unit 48): *to*: *approach, attitude, reaction, reference, introduction*; *for*: *need, reason, respect, demand/desire, responsibility*; *on*: *effect, attack, tax, comment*; *with/between*: *connection, relationship, contact*; *in*: *increase, decrease, rise, fall, difficulty*. *of* para material, tema, medida (*a speed of 25 km an hour*).
- **Verbo + prep.** (Unit 49): *belong to, consist of, insist on, depend/rely on, listen to, refer to, apologize for, wait for, accuse sb of, result from (causa) / result in (efecto), agree with (persona) / on (tema)*.
- **Phrasal verbs** (Unit 50): verbo + partícula con nuevo significado. Separables: *clean up the mess / clean the mess up*; con **pronombre**, el objeto va **entre** verbo y partícula (*clean it up*, no *clean up it*). Con dos partículas: *look forward to, put up with, catch up with*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `adj/noun/verb + fixed preposition + noun / V-ing`
- `phrasal verb + it/them + particle` (separable)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- She had always been good at languages.
- Their need for money is growing fast.
- His illness had some connection with his diet.
- His failure resulted from lack of attention to details.
- It took ages to clean it up.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *depend of* → ✅ *depend **on***
- ❌ *consist in* (= consist of) → ✅ *consist **of***
- ❌ *an increase of wages* → ✅ *an increase **in** wages*
- ❌ *interested on / married with* → ✅ *interested **in** / married **to***
- ❌ *He cleaned up it.* → ✅ *He cleaned **it up**.*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The success of the experiment {A|depended} {B|of} the accuracy {C|of} the {D|measurements}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Se dice 'depend on', no 'depend of'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Scientists have observed a sharp {A|increase} {B|of} the number of {C|storms} {D|over} the past decade."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'increase' lleva 'in' para indicar lo que aumenta: 'an increase in the number of storms'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Heavy rainfall over several days eventually ____ severe flooding in the valley."
  opciones: ["resulted from", "resulted in", "resulted to", "resulted with"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'result in' introduce el efecto (las lluvias causaron la inundación); 'result from' introduce la causa."
```
