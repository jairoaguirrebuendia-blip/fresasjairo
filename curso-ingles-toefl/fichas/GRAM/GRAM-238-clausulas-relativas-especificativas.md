# Defining relative clauses (GRAM-238)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 92 "Defining relative clauses", p. 95.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar cláusulas relativas que identifican al sustantivo, eligiendo el pronombre correcto y sabiendo cuándo se puede omitir.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Identifican **cuál** persona/cosa; van justo después del sustantivo y necesitan una cláusula principal.
- Personas: *who / that* (objeto formal: *whom*). Cosas: *which / that*.
- El pronombre puede ser **sujeto** u **objeto** del verbo de la relativa.
- Se puede **omitir** si es **objeto** (*The car I wanted to buy*); **no** si es sujeto.
- El pronombre relativo **sustituye** al sustantivo: no se repite el pronombre personal (*the book which I read it* ✗).
- Con preposición: normalmente al final (*the house that we lived in*); delante solo de *whom* / *which* (formal): *to whom, to which*. Nunca *preposición + that / who*.
- *whose* = posesión (normalmente personas).
- *when* tras palabras de tiempo, *where* tras lugar, *why* tras *reason*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `noun + who/which/that + verb ...` (sujeto)
- `noun + (who/which/that) + subject + verb ...` (objeto, omisible)
- `noun + preposition + whom/which + S + V` · `noun + whose + noun + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The woman who lives next door is very friendly.
- The car I wanted to buy was not for sale.
- These are the people to whom Catherine was referring.
- A child whose mother had left him was crying loudly.
- He showed me the place where they work.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The woman lives next door is friendly.* → ✅ *The woman **who** lives next door...*
- ❌ *The book which I read it was long.* → ✅ *The book which I read was long.*
- ❌ *the people to who* / *to that* → ✅ *the people **to whom***
- ❌ *a scientist which discovered* → ✅ *a scientist **who** discovered*
- ❌ *the man who his car was stolen* → ✅ *the man **whose** car was stolen*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whom | a quien | objeto, formal |
| whose | cuyo | posesión |
| the reason why | la razón por la que | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Marie Curie was the first scientist ____ two Nobel Prizes in different fields."
  opciones: ["she won", "who won", "whom won", "which she won"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El relativo es sujeto de 'won' y se refiere a una persona → 'who won'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The village {A|where} my grandparents {B|were born} {C|it} is located {D|high} in the Andes."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El sujeto es 'The village (where...)'; 'it' lo repite innecesariamente."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "An orphan is a child {A|who} parents {B|have} died or {C|are} unable to {D|care} for him or her."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Posesión ('los padres del niño') → 'whose parents'."
```
