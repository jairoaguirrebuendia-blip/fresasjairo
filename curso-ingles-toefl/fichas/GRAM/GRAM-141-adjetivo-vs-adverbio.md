# Adjetivo vs adverbio (GRAM-141)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 25, §207, pp. 262–263; §209, pp. 266–267.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Decidir entre adjetivo y adverbio según lo que modifica: sustantivo/linking verb → adjetivo; verbo de acción, adjetivo u otro adverbio → adverbio.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Adjetivo modifica un **sustantivo**; adverbio de modo modifica un **verbo** (*quick snack / ate quickly*).
- **Linking verbs** (*be, seem, become, look, feel, smell, taste, sound, grow, appear*) + **adjetivo**: *The inspector was polite* / *She listened politely*. Algunos verbos son de enlace o de acción según el sentido: *looked nervous / looked nervously round the room*.
- Un adverbio también modifica adjetivos: *quietly confident*, *highly controversial*.
- Adjetivos en **-ly** (*friendly, lively, lovely, likely, costly, lonely*) no forman adverbio: *in a friendly manner*.
- Misma forma adjetivo/adverbio: *fast, hard, late, early, long, straight, daily, monthly*.
- Pares con distinto significado: *hard* (duro) / *hardly* (apenas); *late* (tarde) / *lately* (últimamente); *near* / *nearly* (casi); *high* / *highly* (muy); *free* / *freely*; *most* / *mostly*.
- **good** = adjetivo; **well** = adverbio (y adjetivo de salud). *well known, well organized*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`V acción + adv (-ly)` · `linking V + adj` · `adv + adj` (*extremely rare*)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Kate is **fluent** in Russian. She speaks Russian **fluently**.
- The speaker looked **nervous**. / He looked **nervously** round the room.
- Roger sings **well**, doesn't he? NOT *He sings good.*
- You've all worked **hard**. / I've got **hardly** any money.
- They greeted us **in a friendly manner**. NOT *friendlily*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The population grew rapid.* → ✅ *…grew **rapidly**.*
- ❌ *The soup tastes deliciously.* → ✅ *…tastes **delicious**.*
- ❌ *a high controversial theory* → ✅ *a **highly** controversial theory*
- ❌ *He works hardly every day.* (= mucho) → ✅ *He works **hard**…*
- ❌ *an extreme rare species* → ✅ *an **extremely** rare species*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| hardly | apenas | negativo |
| lately | últimamente | con present perfect |
| highly | sumamente | *highly unlikely* |
