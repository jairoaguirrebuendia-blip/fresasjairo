# Adverb clauses y subordinating conjunctions (GRAM-514)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter III (Day 22) "Two Kinds of Clauses / Adverbial clauses / Subordinate Conjunctions / Kinds of Adverbial Clause", pp. 116–121.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el subordinante adecuado al significado y construir la cláusula adverbial con sujeto y verbo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- An **independent clause** can stand alone; a **dependent (subordinate) clause** has S + V but cannot stand alone.
- An **adverb clause** works like an adverb (when, where, why, how, under what condition…) and begins with a **subordinating conjunction**.
- Meaning groups: **time** (*after, before, when, while, since, until, as soon as*), **place** (*where, wherever*), **manner** (*as, as if, as though*), **condition** (*if, unless, provided that*), **concession** (*although, though, even though, even if*), **cause** (*because, since, as*), **purpose** (*so that, in order that*), **result** (*so + adj + that, such + noun + that*), **comparison** (*than, as…as*).
- Subordinator + **clause** (S + V); preposition + **noun**: *because* vs *because of*, *although* vs *despite*.
- Clause first → comma: *When…, main clause*. Clause second → usually no comma.
- In comparisons, complete the clause to choose the pronoun: *faster than he (runs)*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Subordinator + S + V, + main clause` · `Main clause + subordinator + S + V` · `so + adj/adv + that` · `such (a) + noun + that`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I always wake up when the alarm clock rings.
- I read the book while I was waiting for you.
- Mr. Yani won the chess game although he sacrificed his queen.
- I was unable to visit you because my sister borrowed my car.
- I run faster than he (runs).

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Because of the museum was closed…* → ✅ *Because the museum was closed…*
- ❌ *Despite it rained…* → ✅ *Although it rained… / Despite the rain…*
- ❌ *so interesting lecture that* → ✅ *such an interesting lecture that / so interesting that*
- ❌ *Although…, but…* → ✅ elimina *but*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| although / even though | aunque | concesión + cláusula |
| unless | a menos que | = if… not |
| provided (that) | siempre que | condición |
| so that | para que | propósito (+ can/could/will/would) |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: "Exercise: Error Detection", pp. 121–123 (clave p. 175).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ the roads were icy, the bus arrived on time."
  opciones: ["Despite", "Because", "Although", "Unless"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Relación de contraste con una cláusula (S + V) → 'Although'. 'Despite' exige sustantivo; 'Because' y 'Unless' no expresan contraste."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Because of} the museum {B|was} closed, the tourists {C|visited} the park {D|instead}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Because of' va con sustantivo; ante una cláusula (the museum was closed) se usa 'Because'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The lecture was ____ interesting that nobody left early."
  opciones: ["such", "so", "too", "very"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Resultado con adjetivo solo: 'so + adjective + that'. 'such' necesita sustantivo (such an interesting lecture)."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** varios ejemplos de "Kinds of Adverbial Clause" (p. 119) son incorrectos: "The professor came in late than her usual schedule" (**later than**), "She studied very hard so that he will top the exam" (**so that she would top**), "The war is now inevitable so that we will have to evacuate" (mejor *…inevitable, so we will have to evacuate*), y el de condición está cortado ("provided1"). La lista de subordinantes (p. 118) incluye "except" y "whether", que no introducen cláusulas adverbiales típicas.
- La clave del Error Detection (p. 175) tiene fallos: ítem 4 da "he doesn't" (el relato está en pasado → *he didn't*) y el ítem 5 cita "Bacolod", que no aparece en el ejercicio (el error real es *my parents live* → *lived*).
