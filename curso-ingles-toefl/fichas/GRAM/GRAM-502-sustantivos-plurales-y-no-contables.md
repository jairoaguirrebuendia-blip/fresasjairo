# Nouns: plural forms y count/noncount nouns (GRAM-502)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter II (Day 2) "Different Kinds of Nouns / Fifteen rules… / Uncountable noun", pp. 19–25, 29; Day 17 "Indefinite adjective", p. 95.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar plurales regulares e irregulares y distinguir sustantivos contables de no contables con sus cuantificadores.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Most nouns add **-s**; nouns ending in s, ch, sh, x, z add **-es**; consonant + y → **-ies**; vowel + y → **-s**; many nouns in f/fe → **-ves**.
- Irregular plurals: vowel change (*foot–feet, tooth–teeth, woman–women*), *-en* (*child–children, ox–oxen*), no change (*sheep, aircraft, series, means*).
- Foreign (Latin/Greek) nouns often keep their plural: *crisis–crises, criterion–criteria, phenomenon–phenomena, bacterium–bacteria*. ¡Muy frecuentes en TOEFL!
- **Count nouns** have singular and plural (*a ball, three balls*). **Noncount (mass) nouns** have no plural and take a singular verb: *advice, information, equipment, furniture, luggage, scenery, jewelry*.
- Quantifiers: count → *many, several, few, a few*; noncount → *much, a great deal of, little*; both → *some, a lot of, enough*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`many/few/several + plural count noun` · `much/little/a great deal of + noncount noun` · `a piece of + noncount noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- city → cities · key → keys · knife → knives · tomato → tomatoes
- child → children · tooth → teeth · sheep → sheep
- crisis → crises · criterion → criteria · phenomenon → phenomena
- I have many books at home.
- I have much information on history.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *many informations / advices / equipments* → ✅ *much information / some advice / equipment*
- ❌ *These phenomenon are rare.* → ✅ *These phenomena are rare.* (y al revés: ❌ *a criteria* → ✅ *a criterion*)
- ❌ *less students* → ✅ *fewer students* (ver [[GRAM-519 Palabras confusas]])
- ❌ *one of the student* → ✅ *one of the students*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| criterion / criteria | criterio / criterios | singular / plural |
| phenomenon / phenomena | fenómeno(s) | |
| equipment, furniture, luggage | equipo, muebles, equipaje | no contables en inglés |
| a piece of advice | un consejo | contador para no contable |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: ejercicio de género, pp. 26–27 (no trata plurales).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Before the interview, my professor gave me several useful ____."
  opciones: ["advice", "advices", "pieces of advice", "piece of advices"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'advice' es no contable: no tiene plural. Con 'several' se necesita un contador plural: 'pieces of advice'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Many {A|phenomenon} in nature {B|are} still {C|poorly} {D|understood}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Tras 'many' va plural: 'phenomena' (plural irregular griego)."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** clasifica "stars" como sustantivo no contable (p. 20; es contable); da "runner up → runner ups" (p. 23; correcto: **runners-up**); presenta *patio, rodeo, cargo, motto* como "o precedida de vocal" (p. 23; *cargo* y *motto* terminan en consonante + o; *cargoes/cargos, mottoes/mottos* son ambos aceptados); en la tabla J pone *Statistics/Economics* como "singular" y *Mathematics/News* como "plural" (p. 24), cuando todos son formas en -s con significado singular; incluye "elite" y "staff" entre los no contables (p. 29; son colectivos).
- *Zeros/zeroes* y *mangos/mangoes*: ambas formas son válidas.
