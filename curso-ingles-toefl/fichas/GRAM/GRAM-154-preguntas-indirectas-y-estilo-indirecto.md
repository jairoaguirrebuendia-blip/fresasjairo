# Preguntas indirectas y estilo indirecto (GRAM-154)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., §23, pp. 27–28; §33, p. 37; §266, pp. 349–350; §268, p. 351; §269, pp. 353–354.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar inversión en preguntas directas y **orden de afirmación** en preguntas indirectas y reportadas.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Pregunta directa:** (wh-) + **auxiliar + sujeto** + verbo. Si hay varios auxiliares, solo el primero se invierte (*Could I have reserved…?*). Tiempos simples → *do/does/did*.
- **Pregunta indirecta / reportada** (tras *know, wonder, ask, tell me, find out, I'm not sure…*): **wh-word / if / whether + sujeto + verbo**, **sin inversión ni do**. Sin signo de interrogación si la principal es afirmativa.
- Si la *wh-word* es sujeto, el orden no cambia (*who left the bag there*).
- *whether or not* / *whether … or not*, pero no *if or not*.
- **say** sin objeto indirecto (*said (that)*, *said to me*); **tell** + persona (*told me that*); *tell a story/the truth/lies*.
- **Backshift:** tras verbo de reporte en pasado, el tiempo puede retroceder (*is → was, will → would*), sobre todo si ya no es verdad; tras verbo en presente, no cambia.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Wh + aux + S + V?` · `… + wh / if / whether + S + V` · `tell + persona + (that)` · `say + (that)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- We need to know what the rules are. NOT *We need to know what are the rules.*
- Could you tell me where Queen Street is, please?
- I asked Elaine when she had lunch. NOT *I asked Elaine when she did have lunch.*
- Celia told me she's fed up. / Celia said she's fed up. NOT *Celia said me she's fed up.*
- Will there be a band? → They asked if there would be a band.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Scientists do not know why do whales sing.* → ✅ *…why whales sing.*
- ❌ *Can you tell me where is the library?* → ✅ *…where the library is?*
- ❌ *He told that he was tired.* → ✅ *He **said** that… / He **told me** that…*
- ❌ *I wonder if or not…* → ✅ *I wonder whether or not…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whether | si (alternativa) | admite *or not* |
| inquire / wonder | preguntar / preguntarse | verbos de reporte |
