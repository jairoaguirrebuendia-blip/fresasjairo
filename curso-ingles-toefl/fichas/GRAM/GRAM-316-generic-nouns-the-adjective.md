# Generic nouns (five ways) and the + adjective (the rich, the poor) (GRAM-316)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 11 — Definite and Indefinite Articles (Part 5), Grammar Notes 5 — Generic Nouns + Articles, p. 183.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Hacer generalizaciones con las cinco formas genéricas y usar *the + adjective* con verbo plural.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Cinco formas genéricas: *a/an + count noun*, Ø + plural, *the + singular count noun*, *the + plural* (grupo), Ø + non-count. *The + adjective* (*the rich, the poor, the elderly*) representa a un grupo de personas: es plural, lleva verbo plural y nunca -s.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`A whale is… | Dogs are… | The mountain gorilla is… | Water is… | the + adjective + plural verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **A whale** is a large marine mammal that inhabits the world's oceans.
- **Dogs** are domestic mammals related to wolves and foxes.
- **The mountain gorilla** is considered critically threatened.
- **Water** is essential for survival of animals, humans, and plants.
- **The rich are** fortunate. They need to help **the poor**, who are not so fortunate.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The rich is fortunate.* → ✅ *The rich are…*
- ❌ *We must help the poors.* → ✅ *the poor* (el adjetivo no se pluraliza)
- ❌ *The water is essential for life.* (en general) → ✅ *Water is…*
- ❌ *Dolphin is a mammal.* → ✅ *A dolphin / The dolphin / Dolphins…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| the elderly | los ancianos | plural |
| the unemployed | los desempleados | plural |
| endangered | en peligro de extinción |  |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "During heat waves, the elderly ____ especially vulnerable."
  opciones: ["is", "are", "has", "was"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*The + adjective* designa un grupo de personas → verbo plural *are*."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "In many traditional societies, {A|the} young {B|is} expected {C|to show} respect {D|to} their elders."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*The young* = los jóvenes (plural) → *are expected*. *Their* también confirma el plural."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ is a mammal that spends its whole life in water."
  opciones: ["Dolphins", "A dolphin", "The dolphins", "Dolphin"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Genérico con verbo singular *is* → *a dolphin* (o *the dolphin*). *Dolphin* sin artículo es incorrecto con contable singular."
```
