# Pasiva con verbos de reporte y causativos (GRAM-126)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 13, §109–111, pp. 138–140.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Dominar las estructuras pasivas de textos académicos (*is believed to have…*) y el causativo (*have something done*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Patrón 1:** `It + be + V3 (said/thought/believed/known…) + that + cláusula`.
- **Patrón 2:** `Sujeto + be + V3 + to-infinitive` (simple, continuous *to be crossing*, perfect *to have behaved*, pasivo *to have been built*). También con *there*: *There is considered to be…*
- **It + passive + to-inf** solo con *agree, decide, propose*.
- Pasiva de *make* y verbos de percepción: **siempre to-infinitive** (*were made to lie down*). *let* → *be allowed to*.
- **Causativo:** `have/get + objeto + V3` = encargar que alguien lo haga. Activo: `have + obj + V1` / `get + obj + to V1`. También "sufrir algo": *We had a window broken in the storm.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`It is thought that S + V` = `S is thought to + V1 / to have + V3` · `have/get + obj + V3`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- It is thought that Stonehenge dates from about 1900 BC.
- Stonehenge **is thought to date** from about 1900 BC.
- Stonehenge **is thought to have been built** over a period of 500 years.
- The hostages **were made to lie** down.
- I **had my car serviced**. / I got the garage **to service** my car.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The pyramids are believed to built…* → ✅ *…are believed **to have been built**…*
- ❌ *It is said elephants to have good memories.* → ✅ *Elephants **are said to** have… / It is said **that** elephants have…*
- ❌ *She had her hair cutting.* → ✅ *She had her hair **cut**.*
- ❌ *The workers were made work.* → ✅ *…were made **to work**.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| is believed / is thought / is known | se cree / se piensa / se sabe | estilo académico |
| is estimated to | se estima que | + infinitive |
| have sth done | mandar hacer algo | causativo |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The Great Wall ____ over a period of more than two thousand years."
  opciones: ["is thought to build", "is thought to have been built", "thinks to have been built", "is thought that it built"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Patrón sujeto + is thought + infinitivo perfecto pasivo: la muralla fue construida en el pasado."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|It is} estimated {B|that} the universe {C|to be} about 13.8 billion years {D|old}."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Con 'It is estimated that' sigue una cláusula finita: 'the universe is'. El infinitivo va en el otro patrón: 'The universe is estimated to be…'."
```
