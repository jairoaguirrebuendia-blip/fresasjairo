# Subject + be said/thought + to (have) + verb; be regarded as (GRAM-302)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 7 — Passives: Part 2 (Part 3), Grammar Notes 3 & 4 — Opinion/Belief Passives + To Phrase, p. 110.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *X is said/thought/believed to (have)…* y distinguir *regarded as* y *considered (to be)*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Otra forma de reportar opiniones: el sujeto de la cláusula *that* pasa a sujeto principal y el verbo se vuelve infinitivo. Infinitivo simple (*to live*) = mismo tiempo; infinitivo perfecto (*to have visited*) = anterior. *Consider* admite infinitivo o solo frase nominal/adjetivo; *regard* va casi siempre con *as*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`subject + be + past participle + (by + agent) + to + base verb | to have + past participle; be regarded as + NP; be considered (to be) + NP/adj`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The culture **is assumed** (by scholars) **to date** from 5000 B.C.E.
- Bigfoot **is thought to live** in the Pacific Northwest.
- The Japanese **are thought to have visited** the New World before Columbus.
- Columbus **is regarded as** the discoverer of America.
- The defendant **is alleged to have committed** the crime.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *He is said to has written…* → ✅ *to have written* (tras *to* siempre forma base)
- ❌ *The Japanese are thought to visited…* → ✅ *to have visited* (acción anterior)
- ❌ *Columbus is regarded the discoverer.* → ✅ *is regarded as the discoverer*
- ❌ *The temple is believed to have built in 500 B.C.* → ✅ *to have been built* (el templo no construyó: pasiva)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be regarded as | ser considerado como | lleva *as* |
| be considered (to be) | ser considerado | *to be* opcional |
| be alleged to | presuntamente | lenguaje periodístico/jurídico |
