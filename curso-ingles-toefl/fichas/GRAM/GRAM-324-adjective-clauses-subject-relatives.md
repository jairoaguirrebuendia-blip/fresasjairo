# Adjective clauses: who/which/that as subjects; agreement; no double subject (GRAM-324)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 14 — Adjective Clauses: Introduction (Part 6), Grammar Notes 1–2 — Definition of Adjective Clauses; Subjects in Adjective Clauses, pp. 232–233.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar cláusulas adjetivas con pronombre relativo sujeto, con el verbo concordando con el antecedente y sin sujeto doble.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Una cláusula es un grupo de palabras con sujeto y verbo conjugado; la dependiente no puede ir sola. La cláusula adjetiva (relativa) modifica a un sustantivo o pronombre y suele ir justo después de él. Personas: *who/that* (sujeto); cosas: *which/that*. El verbo de la cláusula concuerda con el antecedente. No se repite el sujeto ni se usa *which* para personas.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun (person) + who/that + verb… | noun (thing) + which/that + verb… (verb agrees with the noun)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The house **that we bought** is in the suburbs.
- John is a man **who works** hard.
- There are many people **who have taken** this personality test.
- Type C individuals are people **who love** detail and data. NOT *…people who they love…*
- Ann is a person **who** spends a lot of time alone. NOT *…a person which spends…*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *people who they love detail* → ✅ *people who love detail* (sujeto doble)
- ❌ *a person which spends time alone* → ✅ *a person who spends…*
- ❌ *a personality test who is designed…* → ✅ *a test that/which is designed…*
- ❌ *Chuy is a person who prefer to be alone.* → ✅ *who prefers* (concordancia con *a person*)

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Marie Curie was the first person ____ two Nobel Prizes."
  opciones: ["who won", "who she won", "which won", "won she"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Pronombre relativo sujeto para persona: *who won*. *Who she won* tiene sujeto doble; *which* no se usa para personas."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Students {A|who} {B|they} attend every class {C|usually} {D|perform} better."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sujeto doble: *who* ya es el sujeto de la cláusula; sobra *they*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "This is one of the theories that ____ been tested many times."
  opciones: ["has", "have", "is", "was"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El antecedente de *that* es *theories* (plural), no *one* → *have been tested*."
```
