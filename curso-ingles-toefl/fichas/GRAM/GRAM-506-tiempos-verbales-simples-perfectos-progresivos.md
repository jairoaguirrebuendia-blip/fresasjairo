# Verb tenses: simple, perfect, progressive y consistencia (GRAM-506)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Days 6–8 "The Tenses of Verbs / Six tenses of the verb TO BE / Six Tenses in Progressive Forms", pp. 44–56.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el tiempo verbal según la relación temporal y mantener la coherencia de tiempos en la oración.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Simple present**: facts, general truths, habits. **Simple past**: completed past action (*yesterday, last night*).
- **Future**: *will/shall + base* or *be going to + base*.
- **Present perfect** (*have/has + pp*): action from the past continuing now, or finished at an unspecified time (*just, already, since, for*).
- **Past perfect** (*had + pp*): action completed **before another past action**.
- **Future perfect** (*will have + pp*): completed before a future time (*by next week*).
- **Progressive** = form of *be* + **-ing**: action in progress at that time (*am/is/are*, *was/were*, *will be*, *have been*, *had been*, *will have been* + -ing).
- **Tense consistency**: do not switch tenses without a reason.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`had + pp … when + simple past` · `was/were + -ing … when + simple past` · `by + future time, will have + pp` · `since/for + have/has (been) + pp/-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The sun rises in the east and sets in the west.
- He had left when I arrived.
- By next week, the court will have decided on the case.
- I was singing when you came.
- Joshua had been recording his songs all morning.
- ❌ *We went into the hall and there we eat our lunch.* → ✅ *We went into the hall and there we ate our lunch.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Since 2010 the city grew.* → ✅ *Since 2010 the city has grown.*
- ❌ *When they arrived, the fire has destroyed…* → ✅ *…had destroyed…*
- ❌ *He was study when I called.* → ✅ *He was studying…* (be + -ing)
- ❌ *She has finished it yesterday.* → ✅ *She finished it yesterday.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| since / for | desde / durante | → present perfect |
| by the time / by + fecha | para cuando | → perfect tenses |
| already / just | ya / acabar de | → perfect |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Since 2010, the population of the city ____ steadily."
  opciones: ["grows", "has grown", "is growing", "grew"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Since + punto en el pasado' con acción que continúa hasta ahora → present perfect 'has grown'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "When the firefighters {A|arrived}, the fire {B|has} already {C|destroyed} most of the {D|building}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Acción anterior a otra acción pasada (arrived) → past perfect 'had destroyed'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The technician ____ the machine when the power suddenly failed."
  opciones: ["repaired", "was repairing", "has repaired", "repairs"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Acción en progreso interrumpida por otra en pasado → past progressive 'was repairing'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** las tablas de conjugación ponen "he, she, it care / eat" (pp. 48–50; correcto **cares / eats**) y el pasado de EAT como "eat" (p. 50; correcto **ate**); las tablas progresivas omiten el verbo *be* ("I caring", "I have caring", pp. 55–56; correcto *I am caring, I have been caring*); clasifica "are researching" como presente simple (p. 44; es presente progresivo); "I have gone to the school" (p. 46) es dudoso como ejemplo de acción terminada (mejor *I have been to…*). La clave del ejercicio (p. 172) da "have eaten" en el ítem 4, cuando lo correcto es **had eaten** (acción anterior a otra pasada).
- *shall* con *I/we* es formal/británico; en TOEFL basta reconocerlo.
