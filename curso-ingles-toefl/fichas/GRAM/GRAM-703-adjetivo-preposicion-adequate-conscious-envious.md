# Adjective + dependent preposition: adequate, adjacent, conscious, envious (GRAM-703)

> **Fuente:** English Synonyms and Antonyms — Fernald, Part I, notas "Prepositions" de ADEQUATE, NECESSARY, ADJACENT, CONSCIOUS, ENVIOUS, ALIEN, ADDICTED, EAGER, INCONGRUOUS y PREVIOUS.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Memorizar la preposición fija de adjetivos frecuentes en textos académicos y detectar errores en Written Expression.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
| Adjetivo | Preposición | Ejemplo del texto |
|---|---|---|
| adequate | **to** (la demanda) / **for** (el propósito) | *Adequate to the demand; for the purpose.* |
| necessary | **to** (constituir algo) / **for** (un resultado, una persona) | *decision is necessary for command* |
| adjacent | **to** | *The farm was adjacent to the village.* |
| conscious | **of** | *man is conscious of the limitation of human power* |
| envious | **of** (persona o cosa) | *envious of his wealth or power* |
| alien | **to** (preferible a *from*) | *alien to my thought* |
| addicted | **to** | *Addicted to vice.* |
| eager | **for** (algo) / **in** (la acción) | *Eager for favor, honor; eager in pursuit.* |
| incongruous | **with** | *incongruous with the theme* |
| previous | **to** (= antes de) | *previous to the revolution* |

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`be + adjective + preposition + noun/-ing` → *be conscious of*, *be adjacent to*, *be addicted to + -ing*

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Adequate to the demand; for the purpose.
- The farm was adjacent to the village.
- On the stormy sea, man is conscious of the limitation of human power.
- Envious of (formerly at or against) a person.
- The illustrations were incongruous with the theme.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *adjacent with the river* → ✅ *adjacent **to** the river*.
- ❌ *conscious about the risk* → ✅ *conscious **of** the risk* (también *aware of*).
- ❌ *addicted to gamble* → ✅ *addicted to **gambling*** (*to* es preposición).
- ❌ *envious at his success* (arcaico) → ✅ *envious **of** his success*.
- ❌ *eager of success* → ✅ *eager **for** success* / *eager **to** succeed*.

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Previous to**: Fernald señala que en *made previous to my departure* lo estrictamente correcto sería *previously to*. Hoy *previous to / prior to* son aceptados como preposiciones compuestas formales; *previously to* suena anticuado. En estilo neutro, mejor *before*.
- *Adequate to the demand* hoy suena formal; lo habitual es *adequate for* o *adequate to meet the demand*.
