# Count and uncount nouns (GRAM-206)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 13 "Count nouns", p. 16; Unit 15 "Uncount nouns", p. 18.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir sustantivos contables e incontables, formar plurales correctamente y saber qué determinantes acepta cada tipo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Contables (Unit 13):**
- Tienen singular y plural; se usan con números.
- El **singular contable nunca va solo**: necesita determinante (*a, the, another, every...*).
- El plural va sin determinante cuando se habla en general (*The film is not suitable for children*).
- Plurales: *-s*; *-es* tras *-ss, -ch, -s, -sh, -x*; consonante + *-y* → *-ies*; vocal + *-y* → *-s*; irregulares (*child → children, foot → feet, mouse → mice*).

**Incontables (Unit 15):**
- Una sola forma, verbo singular, sin *a/an* ni números.
- Se cuantifican con *some* o con expresiones como *a piece of, a loaf of, a bit of*.
- Falsos amigos para hispanohablantes (incontables en inglés): *advice, baggage, equipment, furniture, homework, information, knowledge, luggage, machinery, money, news, traffic*.
- Algunos son contables al referirse a una porción o un caso concreto: *coffee / two coffees; victory / a convincing victory*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `a/the/every + singular count noun` · `(Ø/some/many) + plural count noun`
- `(Ø/some/much) + uncount noun + singular verb` · `a piece of + uncount noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He was eating another apple.
- Let me give you some advice.
- He gave me a very good piece of advice.
- Electricity is dangerous.
- We asked for two coffees. (count)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She gave me many informations.* → ✅ *She gave me **a lot of information**.*
- ❌ *an advice* → ✅ *a piece of advice / some advice*
- ❌ *I parked car over there.* → ✅ *I parked **the** car...* (singular contable sin determinante)
- ❌ *three furnitures* → ✅ *three pieces of furniture*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| equipment | equipo, material | incontable |
| a piece of advice | un consejo | |
| a loaf of bread | una barra de pan | |
| a bit of | un poco de | coloquial |

```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The laboratory {A|recently} purchased {B|several} new {C|equipments} for {D|its} research projects."

  explicacion: "'Equipment' es incontable: no tiene plural. Correcto: 'several new pieces of equipment'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Before the trip, the guide gave us ____ about the local customs."
  opciones: ["an useful advice", "some useful advice", "a useful advices", "many useful advice"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Advice' es incontable: va con 'some', sin 'a/an' ni plural, y no con 'many'."
```
