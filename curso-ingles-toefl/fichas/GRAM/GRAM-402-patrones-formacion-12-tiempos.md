# Patrones de formación de los 12 tiempos (GRAM-402)

> **Fuente:** The English Tenses: Practical Grammar Guide — Williams, P., Forming Tenses — 2. Tense Forms, 2.1–2.4, s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Construir (y detectar errores en) afirmativas, preguntas, negativas y preguntas negativas de los cuatro aspectos en pasado, presente y futuro.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Los 12 tiempos salen de 4 patrones aplicados a 3 tiempos (past / present / future):
- **Simple:** verbo en pasado/presente; futuro con *will* / *be going to* + bare infinitive. Preguntas y negativas con *do/does/did*; con *to be* no se usa *do* (*Are you happy?*).
- **Continuous (progressive):** *be* + present participle; futuro *will be* + V-ing.
- **Perfect:** *have* + past participle; futuro *will have* + past participle.
- **Perfect continuous:** *have been* + V-ing; futuro *will have been* + V-ing.
- Solo el primer auxiliar concuerda con el sujeto o marca el tiempo; *will*, *be*, *have*, *been* y los participios no cambian.
- **Preguntas negativas:** sin contracción, *not* va **después** del sujeto (*Are you not…?*, formal); contraída, toda la contracción va **antes** del sujeto (*Aren't you…?*, lo más común).
- **Will vs shall:** ante la duda, *will*. En preguntas, *shall* solo con *I/we* para ofrecer o sugerir (*Shall we dance?*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`(will) + be + V-ing` · `(will) + have + V3` · `(will) + have + been + V-ing` · `Aux + n't + subject…?` / `Aux + subject + not…?`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Are you not going to the party? / **Aren't you** going to the party?
- She **will have arrived** by this time tomorrow.
- **Haven't we tried** this already?
- We **will have been walking** for hours.
- **Shall I** open the window for you?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Don't you are happy?* → ✅ *Aren't you happy?* (con *be* no se usa *do*)
- ❌ *Do not you want to go?* → ✅ *Don't you want to go?* / *Do you not want to go?*
- ❌ *She will has arrived.* → ✅ *She will have arrived.* (tras *will*, *have* no cambia)
- ❌ *Shall you pass me that book?* → ✅ *Will you pass me that book?*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ going to the conference tonight? I thought you had registered."
  opciones: ["Aren't you", "Don't you", "You aren't", "Not are you"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Pregunta negativa contraída: la contracción completa va antes del sujeto. Con 'be' + going no se usa 'do'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "By next month, the engineers ____ the new bridge."
  opciones: ["will finish have", "will have finished", "will has finished", "have will finished"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Future perfect: will + have (invariable) + past participle."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Will} they not {B|have} {C|been} {D|work} on the project all week?"
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Perfect continuous: have + been + present participle → 'been working'."
```
