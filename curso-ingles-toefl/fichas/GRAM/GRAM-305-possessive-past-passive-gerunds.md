# Possessive + gerund; past gerund (having + pp); passive gerund (being/having been + pp) (GRAM-305)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 8 — Gerunds (Part 4), Grammar Notes 5–7 — Possessive Gerunds; Simple and Past Gerunds; Passive Gerunds, p. 129.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar posesivo antes del gerundio en registro formal y elegir entre gerundio simple, pasado y pasivo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
En escritura formal: posesivo + gerundio (*Pete's/His dominating…*); en conversación se acepta nombre u objeto (*him dominating*), nunca pronombre sujeto. Gerundio pasado *having + pp* = acción anterior al verbo principal. Gerundio pasivo: *being + pp* (presente), *having been + pp* (pasado).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`possessive (my/his/Pete's) + V-ing | having + past participle | being + past participle | having been + past participle`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Pete's dominating** every conversation bothers me.
- I don't like **him dominating** every conversation. (informal)
- **Having met** Jane in my first week of college helped me throughout my college career.
- She hates **being ignored**.
- She's still angry about **having been ignored**.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I was bothered by he coming in.* → ✅ *by his coming in* (nunca pronombre sujeto antes del gerundio)
- ❌ *She hates ignoring.* (si quiere decir que la ignoran) → ✅ *She hates being ignored.*
- ❌ *She was happy about having be invited.* → ✅ *having been invited*
- ❌ *Most people dislike being awaken.* → ✅ *being awakened* (participio correcto)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be bothered by | molestarse por | + possessive + V-ing |
| be annoyed at | irritarse por |  |
| appreciate | agradecer | + possessive + V-ing |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The scientist was proud of ____ for the international award."
  opciones: ["nominating", "having been nominated", "having nominated", "be nominated"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "La científica recibió la nominación (pasiva) antes de sentirse orgullosa → gerundio pasado pasivo *having been nominated*."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|We} sincerely appreciated {B|they} {C|helping} us {D|during} the move."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Antes de un gerundio no va pronombre sujeto; en registro formal se usa el posesivo: *their helping*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Most people dislike ____ by loud noises at night."
  opciones: ["waking", "being awakened", "to awaken", "having awaken"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El sujeto recibe la acción → gerundio pasivo *being awakened*. *Having awaken* tiene participio incorrecto."
```
