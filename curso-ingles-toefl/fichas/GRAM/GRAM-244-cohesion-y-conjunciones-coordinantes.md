# Cohesion and coordinating conjunctions (GRAM-244)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 102 "Cohesion", p. 105.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Conectar ideas con pronombres, determinantes y conjunciones coordinantes (*and, but, or, nor, so, yet, then*), manteniendo el paralelismo cuando se omite el sujeto repetido.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Referencia hacia atrás**: pronombres personales y posesivos (*she, it, them, mine*), determinantes específicos (*the, his*), demostrativos (*this, that, these, those*) y determinantes generales (*another, each, every, both, either, neither*).
- **Conjunciones coordinantes**: *and, but, or, nor, so, yet, then*. Unen cláusulas del mismo nivel.
- Si las cláusulas tienen el **mismo sujeto**, se puede omitir en la segunda → los verbos quedan **paralelos** (*born in Budapest and raised in Manhattan*).
- Muchas conjunciones subordinantes (*when, so long as*) y adverbios (*therefore*) pueden unir oraciones separadas.
- Palabras que resumen lo dicho: *That's a good question.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Clause 1 + and/but/or/so/yet + Clause 2`
- `S + V1 + and + V2` (mismo sujeto, verbos paralelos)
- `Sentence 1. Therefore, + Sentence 2.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Mary came in. She was a good-looking woman.
- Five officials were sacked. Another four were arrested.
- He was only a boy then, yet he was not afraid.
- She was born in Budapest and raised in Manhattan.
- When she saw Morris she went pale, then blushed.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She was born in Budapest and raising in Manhattan.* → ✅ *...and **raised** in Manhattan.* (paralelismo)
- ❌ *He didn't yell or screaming.* → ✅ *He didn't yell or **scream**.*
- ❌ *The plan was cheap, but it effective.* → ✅ *..., but it **was** effective.* (falta verbo)
- ❌ Referencia ambigua: *Anna told Mary that she...* → aclarar con el nombre.

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| yet | sin embargo, pero | conjunción |
| nor | ni | invierte: *nor did he* |
| therefore | por lo tanto | adverbio conector |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The ancient Egyptians {A|studied} the stars, {B|developed} a calendar, and {C|building} enormous {D|monuments}."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Serie de verbos con el mismo sujeto: deben ser paralelos (pasado simple): 'built'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The team worked for months on the design, ____ the final product still had several defects."
  opciones: ["yet", "because", "so that", "unless"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Contraste entre dos cláusulas coordinadas → 'yet' (= but). 'because', 'so that' y 'unless' no expresan contraste."
```
