# It + be + past participle + that clause (it is said/believed that…) (GRAM-301)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 7 — Passives: Part 2 (Part 3), Grammar Notes 2 & 4 — Opinion/Belief Passives; Passives in Academic Discourse, pp. 109–110.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reportar opiniones y creencias de forma impersonal con *It is believed/said/thought that…*, típico de textos académicos.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Para reportar ideas sin comprometer al autor se usa *it* + *be* + participio de un verbo de opinión + cláusula *that*. Solo funciona con verbos que admiten *that* (allege, assume, believe, claim, report, say, think). El *by phrase* es opcional. *That* puede omitirse en inglés informal.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`It + be + past participle (said/believed/thought/claimed/assumed/alleged/reported) + (by + agent) + (that) + clause`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- It **is assumed that** this culture is very old.
- It **is believed that** Atlantis really existed.
- It **is claimed** (by some scholars) **that** Shakespeare didn't write all his plays.
- It **is thought that** the Vikings explored the New World long ago.
- NOT *It is regarded that Shakespeare didn't write all of his plays.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Is believed that Atlantis existed.* → ✅ *It is believed that…* (el sujeto *it* es obligatorio; en español 'se cree que' no lo lleva)
- ❌ *It is regarded that…* → ✅ *It is believed that…* / *X is regarded as…* (*regard* no admite *that*)
- ❌ *It believed that…* → ✅ *It is believed that…* (falta *be*: es pasiva)
- ❌ *It is said what the people came from Asia.* → ✅ *It is said (that)…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| allege | alegar, afirmar sin probar | muy usado en noticias judiciales |
| assume | suponer | It is assumed that… |
| claim | afirmar, sostener | It is claimed that… |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ that the ancient city was destroyed by an earthquake."
  opciones: ["It is believed", "Believed", "It believes", "Is believed"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Estructura impersonal *It + is + believed + that*. Sin *it* falta el sujeto; *It believes* es activa y no tiene sentido."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|It is} {B|regarded} {C|that} the Vikings {D|reached} North America before Columbus."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*Regard* no se usa con *that*; debe ser *thought/believed*. (*Regard* va con *as*: The Vikings are regarded as…)."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "In many early reports, ____ that the disease was spread by contaminated water."
  opciones: ["it was claimed", "was claimed", "it claimed by doctors", "claimed it was"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Pasiva impersonal completa: *it + was + claimed + that*. Las demás omiten el sujeto, usan voz activa o alteran el orden."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
Según el libro, empezar con *It + be + participio + that* crea la mayor distancia entre autor e idea; por eso es formal y frecuente en artículos y noticias.
