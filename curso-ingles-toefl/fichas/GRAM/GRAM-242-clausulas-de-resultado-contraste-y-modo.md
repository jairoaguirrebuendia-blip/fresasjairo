# Result, contrast and manner clauses (GRAM-242)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 98 "Result clauses", p. 101; Unit 99 "Contrast clauses", p. 102; Unit 100 "Manner clauses", p. 103.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar resultado (*so...that, such...that, too...to, enough to, therefore*), contraste (*although, despite, in spite of*) y modo (*as, as if, the way*) con la estructura correcta.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Resultado (Unit 98):** siempre **después** de la principal.
- *so* / *so that* + resultado; **so + adj/adv + that**; **such (a/an) + (adj) + noun + that** (*that* omisible).
- *too + adj/adv + to-inf.* (no sucede); *adj/adv + enough + to-inf.* (sí sucede); *enough* va **antes** del sustantivo.
- *and as a result / and so / and therefore*; *As a result,* / *Therefore,* al inicio de otra oración.

**Contraste (Unit 99):**
- *although / though / even though* + **cláusula** (se puede reforzar con *still, nevertheless*).
- *despite / in spite of* + **noun group** o **-ing**; con cláusula: *despite the fact that / in spite of the fact that*.
- *in spite **of*** pero *despite* **sin *of***.
- Con mismo sujeto se omiten sujeto y *be*: *Although poor, ...*

**Modo (Unit 100):** *as* (como), *just/exactly as*; *as if / as though* (+ pasado / *were* si es irreal); *the way (that), in a way that*; *like* (informal).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `so + adj/adv + (that) + clause` · `such + a + adj + noun + (that) + clause`
- `too + adj + to-inf` · `adj + enough + to-inf`
- `although + S + V` · `despite / in spite of + noun / V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He dressed so quickly that he put his boots on the wrong feet.
- She got such a shock that she dropped the bag.
- He was too proud to apologise.
- Despite the difference in their ages they were close friends.
- You talk about him as if he were dead.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *despite of the rain* → ✅ *despite the rain / in spite of the rain*
- ❌ *Although the rain, we played.* → ✅ ***Despite** the rain, ...* / *Although **it rained**, ...*
- ❌ *It was so a good film that...* → ✅ *It was **such** a good film that...*
- ❌ *Although he was tired, but he worked.* → ✅ *Although he was tired, he worked.* (conector doble)
- ❌ *She was enough old to vote.* → ✅ *She was old enough to vote.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| despite / in spite of | a pesar de | + sustantivo / -ing |
| even though | aunque | + cláusula, enfático |
| as a result | como resultado | conector |
| as if / as though | como si | modo irreal |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ its small size, the hummingbird can fly long distances during migration."
  opciones: ["Although", "Despite", "Even though", "In spite"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sigue un noun group ('its small size') → preposición 'Despite'. 'Although/Even though' necesitan cláusula; 'In spite' requiere 'of'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The lecture was {A|such} {B|complicated} {C|that} many students {D|could not} follow it."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Ante adjetivo solo se usa 'so': 'so complicated that'. 'such' va con sustantivo ('such a complicated lecture')."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Although} the medicine was expensive, {B|but} many patients {C|bought} it {D|anyway}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Although' ya une las cláusulas; 'but' es un conector redundante."
```
