# Defining relative clauses (GRAM-605)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 24 "True and false definitions" (hoja del alumno y teacher's notes, s/p). Relacionado: Worksheet 25 "Stuff and things".

## 🎯 Objetivo
Definir personas, cosas y lugares con cláusulas relativas especificativas, eligiendo el relativo correcto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **who** → personas (*someone who…, a person who…*)
- **that** → cosas (también personas) (*something that…*)
- **where** → lugares (*a place where…*)
- Cuando el relativo es **objeto**, el pronombre sobrante no se repite: *something that you rest your head on* (no *…on it*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `noun + who/that + verb…` (relativo sujeto)
- `noun + that + subject + verb (+ preposition)` (relativo objeto)
- `place + where + subject + verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- A vandal is someone who damages buildings or objects.
- A plumber is a person who repairs water pipes.
- A pillow is something that you rest your head on in bed.
- Flip-flops are things that you wear on your feet at the beach.
- A cot is a place where a baby sleeps.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *a person which repairs…* → ✅ *a person **who** repairs…*
- ❌ *something that you rest your head on **it*** → ✅ *…rest your head on* (sin pronombre repetido)
- ❌ *a place where a baby sleeps **there*** → ✅ *a place where a baby sleeps*
- ❌ *a place which a baby sleeps* → ✅ *a place **where** a baby sleeps* / *a place **in which**…*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "A seismologist is a scientist ____ earthquakes."
  opciones: ["who studies", "which studies", "studies", "who study them"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Persona → who; el verbo concuerda con 'a scientist' (singular) → studies."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The village {A|where} the poet {B|was born} {C|it} has become {D|a popular} tourist site."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El sujeto es 'The village (where the poet was born)'; 'it' repite el sujeto y sobra."
```
