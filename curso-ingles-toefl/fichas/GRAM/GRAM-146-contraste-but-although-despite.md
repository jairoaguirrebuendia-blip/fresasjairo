# Contraste: but, although, despite (GRAM-146)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 30, §246, pp. 325–326.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el conector de contraste según lo que sigue: cláusula, sustantivo/gerundio, o una nueva oración.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Conjunciones + cláusula:** *but, yet* (coordinantes); **although, though, even though** (subordinantes; *even though* más enfático; no *even although*); **whereas / while** (diferencia, no sorpresa).
- **Preposiciones + sustantivo / gerundio:** **in spite of, despite** (no *despite of*). No pueden ir con cláusula, salvo *despite/in spite of the fact that*.
- **Adverbios de enlace (nueva oración o tras punto y coma):** **however, nevertheless, even so, all the same, still, on the other hand**.
- *though* (adverbio) al final: *It was closed, though.*
- *on the contrary* = lo opuesto es verdad (no = *on the other hand*).
- Patrón enfático: *Strange though it may seem…*, *Much as I like Tom…*
- No combinar *although* y *but* en la misma oración.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Although + S + V, S + V` · `Despite / In spite of + N / V-ing, S + V` · `S + V. However, S + V.`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Although** the Great Fire destroyed much of London, only six people died.
- **In spite of/Despite** the widespread destruction, only six people died.
- The family always enjoy themselves **despite having** no money. NOT *despite of having*
- The Great Fire destroyed much of London. **However**, only six people lost their lives.
- I'm right-handed **whereas** my brother is left-handed.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Despite it was raining, they played.* → ✅ ***Although** it was raining… / **Despite** the rain…*
- ❌ *Although she was tired, but she continued.* → ✅ *Although she was tired, she continued.*
- ❌ *in spite the cost* → ✅ *in spite **of** the cost*
- ❌ *He studied hard, however he failed.* (comma splice) → ✅ *He studied hard; however, he failed.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| whereas | mientras que | contraste de diferencia |
| nevertheless | sin embargo, no obstante | adverbio formal |
| despite the fact that | a pesar de que | + cláusula |
