# Sustantivos plurales, pair nouns y group nouns (GRAM-102)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 18, §154–157, pp. 194–197.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer sustantivos que "parecen" plurales pero son singulares (y viceversa) para no fallar la concordancia.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Siempre plurales (verbo plural):** belongings, clothes, congratulations, earnings, goods, outskirts, premises, remains, riches, surroundings, thanks, troops.
- **Forma en -s pero verbo singular:** *news*; materias (*mathematics, statistics, physics, politics, economics*); deportes y juegos (*athletics, gymnastics, billiards*); enfermedades (*measles, mumps*).
- **Misma forma singular y plural:** barracks, crossroads, headquarters, means, series, species, works.
- **Pair nouns** (trousers, glasses, scissors, jeans, pyjamas…): plural, sin *a* ni números; se cuentan con **a pair of** (que es singular).
- **Group nouns** (family, team, government, committee, staff…): en inglés británico admiten singular o plural; en **inglés americano** (el del TOEFL) normalmente **singular**. Mantén la coherencia: singular → *it/its/which*; plural → *they/their/who*.
- **police, people, cattle, livestock, poultry** → siempre **plural**.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`The news is…` · `Economics is…` · `These trousers are… / This pair of trousers is…` · `The police are…`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The news **isn't** very good, I'm afraid.
- These trousers **need** cleaning. / This pair of trousers **needs** cleaning.
- The government wants to improve **its** image. / The government want to improve **their** image.
- The police **are** questioning a man. / Some cattle **have** got out into the road.
- Each town has **its own** mayor.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Economics **are** a difficult subject.* → ✅ *Economics **is**…*
- ❌ *The police **is** investigating.* → ✅ *The police **are** investigating.*
- ❌ *The committee has made **their** decision.* → ✅ *…**its** decision.* (no mezclar singular con *their*)
- ❌ *a scissor* → ✅ *a pair of scissors*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| species / series / means | especie(s) / serie(s) / medio(s) | igual en singular y plural |
| premises | local, instalaciones | siempre plural |
| outskirts | afueras | siempre plural |
| livestock | ganado | plural, sin -s |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Statistics {A|are} a branch of mathematics that {B|deals} with {C|the collection} and analysis of {D|numerical data}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Statistics' como materia de estudio es singular → 'is'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The committee has finally announced ____ decision on the new budget."
  opciones: ["its", "their", "it's", "theirs"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "El verbo 'has' marca al group noun como singular, así que el posesivo debe ser 'its' (no 'their'). 'it's' = it is."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- *These statistics are complicated* (= estas cifras) sí es plural. *His politics are left-wing* (= sus opiniones) también.
- *The United States* normalmente lleva singular.
