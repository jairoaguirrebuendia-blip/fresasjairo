# Where and when in adjective clauses (GRAM-327)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 14 — Adjective Clauses: Introduction (Part 6), Grammar Notes 5–6 — Where; When and That in Adjective Clauses, p. 234.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *where* para lugares y *when/that* para tiempos, y saber cuándo *where* no es posible.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*Where* reemplaza a *there* y modifica un sustantivo de lugar; equivale a *which/that + preposición* (y entonces *which/that* puede omitirse). Solo se usa *where* si la idea puede reformularse con *there*; si el lugar es sujeto u objeto de la cláusula, usa *that/which*. *When/that* modifican sustantivos de tiempo y pueden omitirse en registro informal.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`place noun + where + subject + verb = place noun + (which/that) + subject + verb + preposition | time noun + when/that + clause`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- This is the restaurant. + We ate there. = This is the restaurant **where we ate**.
- This is the building **where she works**. = This is the building **(which) she works in**.
- Chihuahua is the town **where I was born**.
- Stockholm is a city **that has** beautiful scenery. NOT *…a city where has beautiful scenery.*
- I can't think of a time **when I wasn't** happy.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *a city where has beautiful scenery* → ✅ *a city that has…* (*where* no puede ser sujeto)
- ❌ *the town where I was born there* → ✅ *the town where I was born*
- ❌ *the building where she works in* → ✅ *where she works / which she works in*
- ❌ *a time where he wasn't helpful* → ✅ *a time when…*

|---|---|---|

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Detroit is the city ____ the American automobile industry first grew rapidly."
  opciones: ["which", "where", "when", "whose"]
  respuesta: B

- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Silicon Valley {A|is} a region {B|where} {C|has} attracted {D|thousands} of technology companies."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El relativo es el sujeto de *has attracted*; *where* no puede ser sujeto → *that/which*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "1969 was the year ____ humans first walked on the moon."
  opciones: ["where", "which", "when", "whom"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Sustantivo de tiempo + cláusula completa → *when* (*which* exigiría preposición: *in which*)."
```
