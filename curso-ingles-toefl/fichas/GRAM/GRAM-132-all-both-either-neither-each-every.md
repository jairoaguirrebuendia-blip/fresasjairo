# All, both, either, neither, each, every (GRAM-132)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 21, §178, pp. 222–226.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar correctamente los cuantificadores de "todo/parte" y sus patrones con *of*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **most + N** (general) vs **most of + the/these/my + N** (específico). Nunca *the most of*.
- **all / half**: *of* opcional antes de determinante, obligatorio antes de pronombre (*all of them*).
- **none of** = ninguno (negativo). *NOT All of the rabbits didn't survive*; *not all* = no todos.
- **whole** = adjetivo: *the whole tape* (no *the all tape*).
- **Para dos:** *both* (los dos), *either* (uno u otro), *neither* (ninguno de los dos). *either/neither + singular*; *either/neither of + plural*. No *the both*.
- **every / each + singular + verbo singular**. *every* = todos (3+, grupo); *each* = uno por uno (2+). *each of*, *each* tras el sujeto, *£5 each*; *every* no admite *of*. *almost every*, no *almost each*. *every other week* = cada dos semanas.
- No verbo negativo tras *every/each*: *None of the doors were locked*.
- **part of** solo + singular (*some of the students*, no *part of the students*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`most + N` / `most of the + N` · `both (of the) + N pl` · `either/neither + N sing` · `each/every + N sing + V sing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Most people want a quiet life. / Most of the people here are strangers to me.
- Most of the pubs around here serve food. NOT *the most of the pubs*
- If you're ambidextrous, you can write with **either** hand.
- **Every** shop was open. / We went into **each** shop in turn.
- **None** of the rabbits survived. NOT *All of the rabbits didn't survive.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The most of the students passed.* → ✅ ***Most of** the students…*
- ❌ *Each of the members have a vote.* → ✅ *…**has** a vote.*
- ❌ *Every students must register.* → ✅ ***Every student** must…*
- ❌ *Both of candidates…* → ✅ ***Both (of the)** candidates…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| the majority of | la mayoría de | = most of |
| every other | cada dos | *every other weekend* |
| the whole of | la totalidad de | whole como sustantivo |
