# Coordinating and correlative conjunctions (GRAM-513)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 1 "Compound Sentence", p. 15; Day 21 "Conjunctions / Types of Conjunctions", pp. 112–114.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar coordinantes y parejas correlativas completas y correctas, uniendo elementos del mismo tipo gramatical.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Coordinating conjunctions** (*for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so*) join words, phrases or clauses with the **same grammatical construction**.
- **Correlative conjunctions** work in fixed pairs: *both…and*, *either…or*, *neither…nor*, *not only…but (also)*, *whether…or*.
- Never mix the pairs (*neither…or*, *both…or*, *either…nor*).
- The elements after each half of the pair must be **parallel** (noun–noun, adjective–adjective, verb–verb).
- Subject agreement with *either/neither/not only*: the verb agrees with the nearer subject (ver GRAM-508).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`both X and Y` · `either X or Y` · `neither X nor Y` · `not only X but also Y` · `whether X or Y` (X e Y del mismo tipo)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Julia and Petra are pretty girls.
- Neither we nor they want it.
- I will visit you, whether by boat or by plane.
- Xyli is not only beautiful, but also brilliant.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *either take the train nor rent a car* → ✅ *either… or*
- ❌ *both the professor or the students* → ✅ *both… and*
- ❌ *not only intelligent but also she works hard* → ✅ *not only intelligent but also hardworking* (paralelismo)
- ❌ *Neither… nor… don't* → ✅ *neither… nor* ya es negativo: no añadir *not*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| both… and | tanto… como | plural si une sujetos |
| neither… nor | ni… ni | sin otra negación |
| not only… but also | no solo… sino también | |
| whether… or | si… o | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The researchers wanted to know ____ the drug was effective or merely safe."
  opciones: ["neither", "whether", "both", "either"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Pareja 'whether… or' introduciendo una alternativa tras 'know'. 'either' no va tras 'know' para introducir una cláusula."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Visitors can {A|either} take the train {B|nor} rent a car {C|to reach} the {D|village}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'either' se combina con 'or', no con 'nor'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The scientist was both admired ____ criticized for her theory."
  opciones: ["or", "nor", "and", "but"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "La pareja es 'both… and'."
```
