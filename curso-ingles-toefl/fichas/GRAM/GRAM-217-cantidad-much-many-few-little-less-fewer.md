# Quantity: much, many, few, little, less, fewer (GRAM-217)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 29 "Quantity 1", p. 32.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Emparejar cada cuantificador con el tipo de sustantivo correcto (contable plural vs incontable) y distinguir *few / a few*, *little / a little*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
| Incontable | Plural contable |
|---|---|
| much (mucho) | many (muchos) |
| little / a little | few / a few |
| less (menos) | fewer (menos) |
| more | more |
- *much* se usa sobre todo en **negativas y preguntas**; en afirmativas se prefiere *a lot of, lots of, plenty of* (válidos con ambos tipos). Sí se dice *so much / too much* en afirmativas.
- *few / little* (sin *a*) enfatizan que es **poco** (sentido negativo); *a few / a little* solo indican cantidad pequeña (sentido positivo).
- *too much / too many* = más de lo razonable; *very many* para énfasis.
- *less* + plural es informal; en inglés formal (TOEFL) **fewer** + plural.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `much / little / less + uncount noun`
- `many / few / fewer + plural count noun`
- `a lot of / plenty of + uncount or plural`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- We've made little progress.
- He wrote many novels.
- He did not speak much English.
- The poor have less access to education.
- There are fewer trees here.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *less cars* (formal) → ✅ ***fewer** cars*
- ❌ *many information* → ✅ *much / a lot of information*
- ❌ *much people* → ✅ ***many** people*
- ❌ *a few money* → ✅ ***a little** money*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| plenty of | de sobra, mucho | ambos tipos |
| few vs a few | pocos (casi ninguno) vs algunos | matiz |
| too many / too much | demasiados / demasiado | |
