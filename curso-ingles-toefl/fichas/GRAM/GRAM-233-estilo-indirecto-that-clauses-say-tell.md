# Reported speech: that-clauses, say vs tell (GRAM-233)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 74 "Reporting the past", p. 77; Unit 76 "Reporting: 'that'-clauses", p. 79; Unit 77 "Other report structures", p. 80.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Construir oraciones con verbo de reporte + cláusula nominal (*that*-clause, *to*-infinitivo, *if/wh*-clause), distinguir *say* y *tell* y ajustar los tiempos.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Estructura: **reporting clause** (*say, tell, ask, explain...*) + **reported clause** (*that*-clause, *to*-inf., *if*-clause o *wh*-clause).
- Tiempos: si el verbo de reporte está en pasado, la cláusula suele ir en pasado; puede quedar en presente si sigue siendo verdad o es un consejo vigente. Presente de reporte para lo dicho ahora o habitual; futuro para predecir.
- *that* puede omitirse, **excepto** tras *answer, argue, explain, reply*.
- **say** + (*to* + oyente) + *that*: nunca con el oyente como objeto directo (*I said them* ✗).
- **tell** + **oyente** obligatorio + *that*: *He told me that...* (nunca *tell that* ni *tell to them*). Igual: *convince, inform, notify, persuade, reassure, remind*.
- *explain, mention, suggest, admit, complain, announce* + **to** + oyente.
- Órdenes/peticiones/consejos: verbo + objeto + *(not) to*-infinitivo (*advise, ask, order, tell, warn, beg, persuade*).
- Verbos de pensamiento + *that*: *believe, consider, feel, know, think...*; + *to*: *intend, plan, want*; ambos: *agree, decide, expect, hope, prefer...*
- Pasiva: *She was told that...*; *It was said that...* (no con *answer, complain, insist, promise, reply, warn*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `say (to sb) + that-clause` · `tell + sb + that-clause`
- `tell / ask / advise + sb + (not) to-inf`
- `explain / suggest + to sb + that`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He told me that he was a farmer.
- He answered that the price would be three pounds.
- He complained to me that you were rude.
- He ordered me to fetch the books.
- Doctors advised him not to play for three weeks.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She said me that...* → ✅ *She **told** me that...* / *She said **to me** that...*
- ❌ *He told that he was tired.* → ✅ *He **said** that... / He told **us** that...*
- ❌ *She explained me the rules.* → ✅ *She explained the rules **to me**.*
- ❌ *He told me don't wait.* → ✅ *He told me **not to wait**.*
- ❌ *He suggested me to go.* → ✅ *He suggested **that I go** / suggested going.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| reporting verb | verbo de reporte | say, tell, claim |
| claim / deny / insist | afirmar / negar / insistir | + that |
| remind sb to | recordar a alguien que | |

  pregunta: "The professor reminded the students ____ their essays by Friday."
  opciones: ["that submit", "to submit", "submitting", "for submit"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'remind' + objeto + to-infinitivo: 'reminded the students to submit'."
