# Past continuous y past perfect (GRAM-105)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 9, §66, pp. 89–91; §68, pp. 92–94.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Ordenar acciones en el pasado: acción de fondo (past continuous), acción completa (past simple) y acción anterior a otra pasada (past perfect).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Past continuous** (`was/were + -ing`): acción en desarrollo en un momento o periodo pasado; acción de **fondo** interrumpida por otra en past simple (*when*). Dos acciones simultáneas → ambas en continuous (*while*).
- Acciones sucesivas (una tras otra) → ambas en **past simple**.
- **Past perfect** (`had + past participle`): acción **anterior** a un momento pasado (*by then, by midnight, before…*).
- Una sola acción pasada → past simple, **no** past perfect.
- *When + had done* = terminó una cosa y luego hizo otra. *When the boss arrived, the meeting **began*** (después) ≠ *…**had begun*** (antes).
- **Past perfect continuous** (`had been + -ing`): actividad durante un periodo hasta un momento pasado (duración, causa visible).
- Con *hardly/no sooner* → past perfect (ver [[GRAM-155 Inversión]]).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`S + was/were + V-ing … when + S + V2` · `By + tiempo pasado, S + had + V3` · `S + had been + V-ing (for…)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I **was walking** home **when** I **saw** the UFO.
- Tim **was washing** his hair **while** I **was cleaning up** the kitchen.
- We ran onto the platform, but the train **had just gone**.
- This lamp is a new one. I **bought** it last week. NOT *I had bought it last week.*
- My hands were wet. I **had been washing** the floor.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *By 1900, the city has grown to a million people.* → ✅ *By 1900, the city **had grown**…*
- ❌ *While she cooked, the phone was ringing.* → ✅ *While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**.*
- ❌ *When Miranda wrote the letter, she went out to post it.* (ambiguo) → ✅ *When Miranda **had written** the letter, she went out…*
- ❌ *My grandmother was loving this house.* → ✅ *…**loved**…* (estado → simple)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| by the time / by then | para cuando / para entonces | suele pedir past perfect |
| while / as | mientras | acción de fondo (continuous) |
| previously | previamente | marcador de past perfect |


## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- Con *before/until* el past perfect es opcional: *We didn't want to stop until we finished/had finished the job.*
- *When I saw Debbie, she was playing golf* (en medio) ≠ *she'd been playing golf* (ya había terminado).
