# Past perfect continuous y contrastes (GRAM-406)

> **Fuente:** The English Tenses: Practical Grammar Guide — Williams, P., The Past — The Past Perfect Continuous, secciones 12–14, s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *had been + V-ing* para la **duración** de una actividad hasta un punto del pasado, y distinguirlo del past continuous y del past perfect.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **12.1 Duration:** actividad continua o repetida en curso hasta otro evento pasado que la termina o interrumpe; suele ir con *for* / *since*.
- **12.2 Completed past processes:** proceso terminado poco antes de un punto pasado cuyo resultado se nota (pelo mojado, cansancio); enfatiza la actividad; puede expresar irritación.
- **13. vs past continuous:** el past continuous con duración indica **simultaneidad** (*for an hour while she spoke*); el past perfect continuous indica que la actividad **empezó antes** y llevaba tiempo.
- **14. vs past perfect:** past perfect = **resultado/completitud** (*for twelve people*); past perfect continuous = **proceso/duración** (*for two hours*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`had been + V-ing (+ for/since …) + when/before + past simple`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I **had been reading for hours** when I fell asleep.
- Her hair was wet because she **had been swimming**.
- I **had been washing** the car **for an hour** when she phoned.
- I had cooked dinner for twelve people. / I **had been cooking** dinner for two hours.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I was washing the car for an hour when she phoned.* → ✅ *I had been washing the car for an hour when she phoned.*
- ❌ *They had been work all day.* → ✅ *They had been working all day.*
- ❌ *She had been knowing him for years.* (estado) → ✅ *She had known him for years.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| for + periodo | durante | *for an hour, for months* |
| since + punto | desde | *since the night before* |
