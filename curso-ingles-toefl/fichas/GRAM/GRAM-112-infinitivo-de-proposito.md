# Infinitivo de propósito (GRAM-112)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 14, §118–119, p. 147; §126(6), p. 157.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar finalidad con `to + base` (no *for + -ing* ni *for to*), y usar el infinitivo como sujeto o complemento.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Propósito:** to-infinitive (*to do some shopping*). Puede ir al inicio: *To get a good seat, you need to arrive early.*
- Si el infinitivo tiene sujeto propio → **for + sujeto + to-infinitive**: *For plants to grow properly, you have to water them.*
- **Resultado (literario):** *came home to find her house on fire*; con *only* = mala noticia tras buena.
- **Comentario:** *To be frank, …* / *to tell you the truth*.
- **Como sujeto:** posible pero poco usual; se prefiere *it* + to-infinitive: *It takes ages to defrost this fridge.* Como complemento tras *be*: *The important thing is not to panic.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`(In order) to + V1` · `for + sujeto + to + V1` · `It + be/takes + … + to + V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Laura has gone to town **to do** some shopping.
- I'm writing **to enquire** about activity holidays.
- **To get** a good seat, you need to arrive early.
- There are telephones **for drivers to call** for help if they break down.
- Melanie's ambition is **to go** to Australia.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *She went to the library for study.* → ✅ *…**to study**.*
- ❌ *Bees visit flowers for to collect nectar.* → ✅ *…**to collect** nectar.*
- ❌ *For get a good seat, arrive early.* → ✅ ***To get** a good seat…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| in order to / so as to | para / con el fin de | más formal; ver §252 |
| only to | para luego (mala noticia) | *only to lose them again* |
