# Verb + noun/pronoun + infinitive (tell/advise/persuade someone to…) (GRAM-308)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 9 — Infinitives (Part 4), Grammar Notes 3 — Verbs + Nouns + Infinitives, p. 145.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el patrón verbo + objeto + infinitivo y evitar el calco del español 'querer que + subjuntivo'.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Algunos verbos exigen objeto antes del infinitivo: advise, allow, convince, encourage, force, invite, order, persuade, require, tell, urge, warn. Otros admiten infinitivo con o sin objeto: ask, choose, expect, get, need, pay, want, would like.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`advise/tell/warn/persuade/require + object + to + base verb | want/expect/ask/need + (object) + to + base verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I warned **Stan to make** the payments. NOT *I warned to make the payments.*
- We told **Nancy to come** early. NOT *We told to come early.*
- We expected **to finish** on time. / We expected **Asha to finish** on time.
- I would like **her to drive** me to your house.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *We told to come early.* → ✅ *We told Nancy to come early.* (falta objeto)
- ❌ *I want that you come.* → ✅ *I want you to come.* (calco de 'quiero que vengas')
- ❌ *They persuaded him that he apply.* → ✅ *persuaded him to apply*
- ❌ *The doctor advised him studying less.* → ✅ *advised him to study less*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| urge | instar, exhortar | + object + to |
| require | exigir | The law requires drivers to… |
| convince | convencer | + object + to |
