# Certeza y posibilidad: must, can't, may, might, could (GRAM-117)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 12, §95, pp. 120–121; §97, pp. 122–124.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Expresar deducciones sobre el presente y el pasado con modales simples y perfectos.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **must** = seguro que sí (lógicamente). **can't** = imposible. Son opuestos.
- **will** = predicción segura sobre algo que no vemos (*It'll be the milkman*).
- **may / might / could** = posible (may un poco más fuerte). **can** NO se usa para posibilidad concreta; solo para posibilidad general (*Smoking can damage your health* = a veces).
- **may not / might not** (quizá no) ≠ **can't** (imposible).
- **Pasado:** *must have + V3* (seguro que sí), *can't/couldn't have + V3* (imposible), *may/might/could have + V3* (quizá).
- *must have done* (certeza pasada) ≠ *had to* (necesidad pasada). *could have done* también = oportunidad no aprovechada.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`must / can't / may / might / could + V1` (presente) · `+ have + V3` (pasado) · `+ be + V-ing` (en curso)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- You got up at four o'clock! Well, you **must be** tired.
- This **can't be** Roland's textbook. He doesn't do physics.
- This glass is cracked. Someone **must have dropped** it.
- Miranda **may have missed** the train.
- You **can't/couldn't have seen** Bob this morning. He's in Uganda.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The dinosaurs must die out suddenly.* (deducción pasada) → ✅ *…**must have died** out…*
- ❌ *He mustn't be at home; the lights are off.* (deducción) → ✅ *He **can't** be at home.*
- ❌ *She may has forgotten.* → ✅ *She may **have** forgotten.*
- ❌ *The timetable can be in this drawer.* (posibilidad concreta) → ✅ *…**could/may** be…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be bound to / be sure to | seguro que | certeza sobre el futuro |
| might as well | más vale (no hay mejor opción) | *I might as well throw it away* |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The tools found at the site are so sophisticated that their makers ____ a highly developed culture."
  opciones: ["must have had", "must had", "can't have had", "should have"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Deducción segura sobre el pasado → 'must have + participio'. 'can't have had' daría el sentido contrario."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The comet {A|that} astronomers observed last night {B|can't be} the same one {C|that} {D|appear} every 76 years, because its orbit is different."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "El antecedente 'one' es singular: 'that appears'. 'can't be' es correcto para imposibilidad lógica."
```
