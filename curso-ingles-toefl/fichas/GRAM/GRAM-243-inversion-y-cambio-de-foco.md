# Inversion and changing sentence focus (GRAM-243)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 101 "Changing sentence focus", p. 104.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer la inversión tras adverbiales de lugar y negativos al inicio de la oración, y las estructuras de énfasis (*It was X who/that...*, *What we want is...*, *The problem is that...*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Normalmente el sujeto va primero; para enfatizar otra parte, se coloca al **inicio** (*In 1956 they went to Australia*).
- Tras **adverbiales de lugar** al inicio, el verbo puede ir antes del sujeto (*In came a girl*), **pero no si el sujeto es pronombre** (*in she came*).
- Tras **adverbiales negativos** al inicio (*On no account, Never, Seldom...*) se invierte: **auxiliar + sujeto** (*On no account must they be let in*).
- Pregunta reportada al inicio: *How he managed I can't imagine.*
- *The fact / problem / thing / truth / answer is (that)...*
- **Cleft sentences**: *It + be + elemento enfatizado + who/that...* (*It was Ted who broke the news*); *What we want is money.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Adverbial of place + V + noun subject` (*In came a girl*)
- `Negative adverbial + aux + S + V` (*Never have I seen...*)
- `It + be + focus + who/that + clause` · `What + S + V + is + focus`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Next to the coffee machine stood a pile of cups. (Unit 38)
- On no account must they be let in.
- The door opened and in she came.
- It was Ted who broke the news to me.
- What we want is money.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *On no account they must be let in.* → ✅ *On no account **must they** be let in.*
- ❌ *Never I have seen...* → ✅ *Never **have I** seen...*
- ❌ *In came she.* → ✅ *In **she came**.* (sujeto pronombre)
- ❌ *It was Ted which broke the news.* → ✅ *It was Ted **who** / **that**...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| on no account | bajo ningún concepto | + inversión |
| the fact is that | el hecho es que | |
| it is ... that | es ... que | cleft |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Never before ____ such a large crowd gathered in the city square."
  opciones: ["had", "it had", "there", "was it"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Adverbial negativo al inicio ('Never before') → inversión auxiliar + sujeto: 'Never before had such a large crowd gathered'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|It was} the Wright brothers {B|which} {C|made} the first {D|powered} flight in 1903."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "En una cleft sentence sobre personas se usa 'who' o 'that': 'It was the Wright brothers who made...'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Seldom ____ such heavy snowfall in this part of the country."
  opciones: ["we see", "do we see", "we do see", "seeing we"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Adverbio negativo ('Seldom') al inicio → inversión con auxiliar: 'Seldom do we see'."
```
