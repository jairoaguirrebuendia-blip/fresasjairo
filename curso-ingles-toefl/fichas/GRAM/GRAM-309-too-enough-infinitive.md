# Adjective/noun + infinitive; too/enough + infinitive (GRAM-309)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 9 — Infinitives (Part 4), Grammar Notes 4–6 — Adjectives + Infinitives; Nouns + Infinitives; Too and Enough + Infinitives, p. 146.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Colocar correctamente *too* y *enough* con infinitivos y usar adjetivos/sustantivos + infinitivo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Adjetivos de sentimiento sobre personas + infinitivo (afraid, glad, likely, proud, reluctant, willing…). Sustantivo + infinitivo da información sobre el sustantivo (*a good place to spend*). *Too + adj/adv + infinitive* implica resultado negativo. *Enough* va DESPUÉS de adjetivo/adverbio y ANTES de sustantivo (formal: también después). *For + noun* indica quién actúa.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`too + adjective/adverb + (for + noun) + to V | adjective/adverb + enough + to V | enough + noun + to V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- George is afraid **to make** mistakes.
- Cozumel is a good place **to spend** a vacation.
- We were **too tired to do** any work today. (So we didn't work.)
- Ken is **strong enough to lift** 80 kilos.
- There's not **enough money for Jane to pay** for the repairs.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *He is enough strong to lift it.* → ✅ *strong enough* (enough va después del adjetivo)
- ❌ *We were too tired for doing any work.* → ✅ *too tired to do*
- ❌ *She was so tired to work.* → ✅ *too tired to work* (*so…that*, no *so…to*)
- ❌ *He was too much tired to work.* → ✅ *too tired*

|---|---|---|

| likely to | probable que | It is likely to rain |

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The instructions were ____ for most participants to follow without help."

```
