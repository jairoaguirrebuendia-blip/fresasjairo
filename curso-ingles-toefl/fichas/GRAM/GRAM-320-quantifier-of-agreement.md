# Quantifier + of + the/determiner; verb agreement with most of / many of (GRAM-320)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 12 — Quantifiers (Part 5), Grammar Notes 7 — Quantifiers in Phrases with Of, p. 201.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *quantifier + of + the* solo para grupos específicos y concordar el verbo con el sustantivo que sigue a *of*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*Quantifier + of + the* (u otro determinante) cuando se habla de un grupo específico; sin *of* para hablar en general (*Most people…*). Con *most of, many of*, etc., el verbo concuerda con el sustantivo después de *of*. No se usa *quantifier + of* ante un sustantivo singular contable. Las reducciones orales *lotta, mosta* no se escriben.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`most/many/some/a few + of + the/my/these + plural or non-count noun | most + noun (general) | most of the + non-count → singular verb; most of the + plural → plural verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Many of the students** in my class got good grades. (a specific group)
- **Most people** don't understand the Theory of Relativity. (people in general)
- **Most of the information** we heard **was** useful.
- **Most of the students were** paying attention.
- **A few of the students** did well on the exam. NOT *A few of the student…*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Most of people can study in quiet places.* → ✅ *Most people…* (general, sin *of the*)
- ❌ *Most of the information were useful.* → ✅ *was*
- ❌ *A few of the student did well.* → ✅ *A few of the students…*
- ❌ *We have a lotta work.* (escrito) → ✅ *a lot of work*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| most of the + noun | la mayoría de los/las | grupo específico |
| most + noun | la mayoría de | general |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ can concentrate more easily in a quiet environment."
  opciones: ["Most of people", "Most people", "The most people", "Most of the people's"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Generalización sobre la gente en general → *Most people* (sin *of the*)."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Most of} the {B|furniture} in the old house {C|were} {D|made} by hand."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El verbo concuerda con *furniture* (incontable, singular) → *was made*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Half of the students in the class ____ from Asia."
  opciones: ["comes", "come", "is coming", "has come"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Concordancia con el sustantivo tras *of*: *students* (plural) → *come*."
```
