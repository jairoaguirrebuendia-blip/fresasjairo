# Should, shouldn't, ought to (GRAM-607)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 29 "What's the matter?", teacher's notes (s/p).

## 🎯 Objetivo
Dar y reconocer consejos con *should/shouldn't* y *ought to/oughtn't to*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*should* + verbo base expresa lo recomendable; *shouldn't*, lo no recomendable. *ought to* es equivalente (el libro dice que los alumnos pueden usar *ought to / oughtn't to*). Nota: *ought* sí lleva **to**; *should* no.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `subject + should/shouldn't + base verb`
- `subject + ought to / oughtn't to + base verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- What's the matter? — I've got backache. — Oh dear, you should lie down and you shouldn't lift anything heavy.
- He should lie down and he shouldn't lift anything heavy. — Has he got backache?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *You should to rest.* → ✅ *You should rest.*
- ❌ *You ought rest.* → ✅ *You ought **to** rest.*
- ❌ *He shoulds…* → ✅ *He should…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| What's the matter? | ¿qué te pasa? | pregunta por un problema |
| I've got backache / a headache | me duele la espalda / la cabeza | |
| lie down | acostarse | phrasal verb |

## 📝 Práctica
- Ref. libro: Worksheet 29, tarjetas de dolencias con ✓ (lo que debe hacer) y ✗ (lo que no).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Doctors {A|say} that people {B|with} high blood pressure {C|should} {D|to reduce} the salt in their diet."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Should + verbo base: 'should reduce'."
- tipo: listening
  pregunta: "Woman: I've had this terrible headache all afternoon. Man: Maybe you ought to stop staring at that screen for a while. What does the man suggest?"
  opciones: ["The woman should buy a new screen.", "The woman should take a break from the computer.", "The woman should see a doctor immediately.", "The woman should work all afternoon."]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'You ought to stop staring at that screen' = consejo de dejar de mirar la pantalla, o sea, tomar un descanso del computador."
```
