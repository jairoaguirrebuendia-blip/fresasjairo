# Relative clauses: who/whom/whose/which/that (GRAM-515)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Chapter III (Day 23) "Adjective Clauses / Restrictive and Nonrestrictive Clause", pp. 124–126.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el pronombre relativo correcto y puntuar las cláusulas restrictivas y no restrictivas.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- An **adjective (relative) clause** modifies a noun (its **antecedent**) and is introduced by a relative pronoun.
- **who** = person, subject of the clause; **whom** = person, object of the clause; **whose** = possession (people or things); **which** = things; **that** = people or things (restrictive only).
- **Restrictive** clause: needed to identify the noun → **no commas**.
- **Nonrestrictive** clause: extra information → **commas**, and use *who/which* (not *that*).
- The relative pronoun replaces a noun/pronoun: do **not** repeat it (*the book that I read ~~it~~*).
- Prueba who/whom: si en la cláusula cabe *he* → *who*; si cabe *him* → *whom*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + who/that + verb…` · `noun + whom/that + S + V…` · `noun + whose + noun + V…` · `Noun, which/who…, verb…`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The manager who received the award was my high school teacher.
- I will buy you those toys that you requested yesterday.
- I saw the girl whom I met at the office.
- Correct: I met the lady whom I saw carrying your radio.
- It is exciting to visit museums that display Greek and Roman arts. (restrictive)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The novel that I borrowed it…* → ✅ *The novel that I borrowed…*
- ❌ *Mexico City, that is huge, …* → ✅ *Mexico City, which is huge, …*
- ❌ *the scientist which discovered* → ✅ *the scientist who discovered*
- ❌ *a writer who's books* → ✅ *a writer whose books*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| restrictive / nonrestrictive | especificativa / explicativa | sin comas / con comas |
| whose | cuyo/a | personas y cosas |
| antecedent | antecedente | sustantivo modificado |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The scientist ____ discovery changed modern medicine received the Nobel Prize."
  opciones: ["who", "whom", "whose", "which"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Relación de posesión (el descubrimiento del científico) → 'whose + noun'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The novel {A|that} I borrowed {B|it} from the library {C|is} {D|overdue}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'that' ya funciona como objeto de 'borrowed'; 'it' repite el objeto y sobra."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Mexico City, ____ is one of the largest cities in the world, sits on an ancient lakebed."
  opciones: ["that", "which", "who", "where"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Cláusula no restrictiva (entre comas) sobre una cosa → 'which'. 'that' no se usa entre comas."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** llama a *whom* "the correct form for the adjective case" (p. 125; es el caso **objetivo**); el ejemplo no restrictivo "Ms.Maritess, The author who wrote an article on DRUGS, visited our school." (p. 126) tiene puntuación y mayúsculas erróneas (mejor *Ms. Maritess, who wrote an article on drugs, visited our school.*).
- En inglés informal se usa *who* por *whom*; en TOEFL se espera *whom* como objeto.
