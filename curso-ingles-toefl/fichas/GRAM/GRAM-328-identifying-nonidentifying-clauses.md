# Identifying vs. nonidentifying clauses; that vs. which; which referring to a whole idea (GRAM-328)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 14 — Adjective Clauses: Introduction (Part 6), Grammar Notes 7–8 — Identifying and Nonidentifying Adjective Clauses; Special Use of Which, p. 235.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir cláusulas identificativas (sin comas) y no identificativas (con comas), y no usar *that* tras coma.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
La identificativa (esencial) es necesaria para saber de quién/qué se habla: sin comas y el pronombre objeto puede omitirse. La no identificativa (no esencial) aporta información extra: va entre comas, el pronombre nunca se omite y no se usa *that* (usa *which/who*). *Which* puede referirse a toda una idea previa (*…, which is why…*); en escritura formal es mejor anteponer un sustantivo (*a quality which…*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + who/that… (no commas) | Proper noun / unique noun + , who/which … , | clause + , which + verb (whole idea)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- People **who embrace risk and change** are classified as Type A people. (identifying)
- Maryam, **who is undoubtedly a Type A person**, is the student body president. (nonidentifying)
- Tarik, **who** just took the personality inventory, is originally from Lebanon. NOT *Tarik, just took…*
- The Myers-Briggs test, **which** I took a long time ago, has proved to be accurate. NOT *…, that I took…*
- Paris is beautiful and exciting, **which** is why I like going there.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The Myers-Briggs test, that I took…* → ✅ *, which I took…* (nunca *that* tras coma)
- ❌ *Tarik, just took the test, is from Lebanon.* → ✅ *Tarik, who just took…* (no se omite)
- ❌ *Boris is very detail-oriented, that is why we should hire him.* → ✅ *, which is why…*
- ❌ *The man, who lives next door, is talkative.* (si hay varios hombres) → ✅ sin comas: identificativa

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| essential / identifying | especificativa | sin comas |
| nonessential / nonidentifying | explicativa | con comas |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The Amazon River, ____ flows through several countries, carries more water than any other river."
  opciones: ["that", "which", "it", "what"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Cláusula no identificativa (entre comas) sobre una cosa → *which*. *That* no se usa tras coma; *it* crea una oración mal unida."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Mount Everest, {A|that} is located {B|in} the Himalayas, {C|is} the highest mountain {D|on} Earth."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Entre comas (no identificativa) se usa *which*, no *that*."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The train was delayed for three hours, ____ caused many passengers to miss their connections."
  opciones: ["that", "what", "which", "it"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "*Which* puede referirse a toda la idea anterior (el retraso). *It* crearía un *comma splice*."
```
