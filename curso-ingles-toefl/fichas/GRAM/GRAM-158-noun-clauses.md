# Noun clauses (GRAM-158)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 33, §260–262, pp. 341–345.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar cláusulas sustantivas y elegir *that*, *what/how/why…* o *whether* según la función.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Una noun clause empieza con **that** (afirmación), **wh-word** (pregunta wh-) o **if/whether** (pregunta sí/no), con **orden de afirmación** (*NOT what time was it*).
- **Funciones:** objeto (*I noticed that the door was open*), complemento (*The idea is that we take it in turns*), **sujeto** (*That he could be mistaken didn't seem possible*), tras *it* vacío (*It didn't seem possible that…*), tras preposición, adjetivo (*I'm glad (that)…*) o sustantivo (*the fact that, the news that, the idea that*).
- **that** puede omitirse como objeto, pero **no cuando la cláusula es sujeto** (*NOT Everyone got back safely was a great relief*), y normalmente tampoco tras sustantivo.
- Como sujeto o tras preposición: **whether**, no *if*.
- **Tras preposición** no va *that*-clause: *about Nicola's illness* / *about Nicola being ill* / *told me (that) Nicola was ill*.
- *what* = *the thing that* (sin antecedente).
- Negación con *think/believe*: *I don't think we've got time*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`That + S + V + V principal` · `It + be + adj + that + S + V` · `prep + wh/whether + S + V` · `the fact that + S + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- We didn't know what time it was. NOT *We didn't know what time was it.*
- **That** everyone got back safely was a great relief.
- **Whether** I'll be able to come depends on a number of things.
- The government is looking into **what** needs to be done.
- You can't deny **the fact that** you received the message.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The earth is round was proved long ago.* → ✅ ***That** the earth is round was proved…*
- ❌ *What does the data show is surprising.* → ✅ *What the data show(s) is surprising.*
- ❌ *If life exists on Mars is unknown.* → ✅ ***Whether** life exists on Mars is unknown.*
- ❌ *They talked about that he resigned.* → ✅ *…about **his resignation** / about **why** he resigned.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| the fact that | el hecho de que | + cláusula completa |
| whether or not | si … o no | |
| it is clear/evident that | está claro que | it vacío |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ dinosaurs became extinct remains a subject of scientific debate."
  opciones: ["Why", "Because", "That why", "The reason"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Se necesita una noun clause como sujeto: 'Why dinosaurs became extinct' + 'remains'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Scientists} have long debated {B|if or not} the universe {C|will continue} {D|expanding} forever."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'if or not' no es correcto; se usa 'whether or not' (o 'whether … or not')."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The discovery ____ penicillin kills bacteria changed modern medicine."
  opciones: ["what", "that", "which", "of"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Tras un sustantivo como 'discovery' se usa una that-clause apositiva: 'the discovery that…'."
```
