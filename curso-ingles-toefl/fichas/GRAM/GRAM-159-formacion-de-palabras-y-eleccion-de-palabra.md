# Formación de palabras y elección de palabra (GRAM-159)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 36, §284–287, pp. 369–372; Chapter 11, §89, p. 112.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Detectar la **forma incorrecta** de una palabra (sustantivo por adjetivo, etc.) y colocaciones erróneas con *do/make*, dos errores muy típicos del *Written Expression*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Sustantivos abstractos:** *-ment* (development), *-tion/-sion* (production, decision), *-ance/-ence* (performance, existence), *-ty/-ity* (security), *-ness* (happiness).
- **Personas:** *-er/-or* (owner, editor), *-ist* (tourist), *-ant/-ent* (inhabitant, student), *-ian* (musician), *-ee* (employee, sentido pasivo).
- **Verbos:** *-ize* (modernize), *-en* (widen, shorten).
- **Adjetivos:** *-al* (national), *-ic* (artistic), *-ive* (effective), *-ful/-less* (careful/careless), *-ous* (dangerous), *-y* (healthy), *-able/-ible* (washable = can be washed).
- **Adverbios:** adjetivo + *-ly*.
- Comprueba la **función** de la palabra en la oración: tras artículo/posesivo → sustantivo; antes de sustantivo → adjetivo; modificando verbo/adjetivo → adverbio.
- **make** = producir/crear: *make a mistake, make progress, make an effort, make money, make arrangements, make a noise*. **do** = actividad/tarea: *do homework, do business, do a course, do your best, do harm, do good, do a favour, do an exam*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`the/a/his + SUSTANTIVO` · `ADJETIVO + N` · `V + ADVERBIO` · `make + resultado / do + actividad`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- They agreed. → their **agreement**
- decide → **decision**, permit → **permission**, silent → **silence**
- The company is the biggest **employer** in the town. It has two thousand **employees**.
- This sweater is **washable**. (= It can be washed.)
- **make** an effort / **make** progress / **do** your best / **do** harm

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *the develop of agriculture* → ✅ *the **development** of agriculture*
- ❌ *a danger chemical* → ✅ *a **dangerous** chemical*
- ❌ *They made their homework.* → ✅ *They **did** their homework.*
- ❌ *The team did a big progress.* → ✅ *…**made** great progress.*
- ❌ *economy growth* → ✅ ***economic** growth*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| make arrangements | hacer preparativos | make = producir/organizar |
| do business | hacer negocios | |
| -less | sin | *powerless, worthless* |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The {A|invent} of the printing press {B|made} it possible to {C|produce} books {D|quickly}."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Tras 'The' y antes de 'of' se necesita un sustantivo: 'invention'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Researchers have {A|done} significant {B|progress} in {C|understanding} how the {D|brain} stores memories."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "La colocación correcta es 'make progress': 'have made significant progress'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Solar energy is becoming increasingly ____ as technology improves."
  opciones: ["economy", "economize", "economical", "economically"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Tras el linking verb 'becoming' y el adverbio 'increasingly' se necesita un adjetivo: 'economical'."
```
