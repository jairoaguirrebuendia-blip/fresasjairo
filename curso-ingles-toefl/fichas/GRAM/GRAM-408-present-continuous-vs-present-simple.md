# Present continuous vs present simple (GRAM-408)

> **Fuente:** The English Tenses: Practical Grammar Guide — Williams, P., The Present — The Present Continuous, secciones 18–19, s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Decidir entre *am/is/are + V-ing* y present simple con la pregunta clave del libro: **¿es temporal?**

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **18.1 Temporary actions:** acción empezada y no terminada, en un periodo que **incluye ahora** (no necesariamente en este instante: *We are learning to speak English*).
- **18.2 Processes of change:** con *increase, decrease, become, develop, expand, get, grow* (*Unemployment is rising*). Pregunta: ¿el proceso puede terminar? Sí → continuo; regla general → simple.
- **18.3 Repeated actions:** con *always, forever, constantly* el continuo expresa **irritación**; el simple es neutro.
- **18.4 Temporary states:** coloquial (*I'm loving this burger*); el libro recomienda a los estudiantes **evitarlo**.
- **19.** Simple = atemporal; continuo = temporal (actual).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`am/is/are (+ not) + V-ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I read books. / I **am reading** a book.
- That dog runs very fast. / That dog **is running** very fast. (on this occasion)
- Howard **is getting** fat. / Howard **gets** fat when he eats chocolate.
- They **are constantly arguing**. / They constantly argue.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Look! The baby sleeps.* → ✅ *Look! The baby is sleeping.*
- ❌ *Prices rise sharply this month.* → ✅ *Prices are rising sharply this month.* (proceso temporal)
- ❌ *I am understanding the question.* → ✅ *I understand the question.* (estado)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| at the moment / now / currently | en este momento | disparadores del continuo |
| constantly / forever | constantemente | + V-ing = queja |
| rise, grow, get, become | subir, crecer, volverse | verbos de cambio |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "According to the latest report, unemployment in the region ____ steadily at the moment."
  opciones: ["rises", "is rising", "rose", "has been rise"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Proceso de cambio temporal en curso ('at the moment') → present continuous 'is rising'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Right now my brother {A|reads} a novel {B|in} his room, so {C|please} don't {D|disturb} him."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "'Right now' indica acción en curso → 'is reading'."
```
