# Possessives and possessive pronouns (GRAM-212)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 22 "Possession", p. 25.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir posesivos (*my, its, their*), pronombres posesivos (*mine, hers, theirs*), el genitivo *'s* y la construcción con *of*; evitar la confusión *its / it's*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Possessives** (determinantes): *my, your, his, her, its, our, their* + sustantivo. Números y adjetivos van después: *Their two small children...*
- **Possessive pronouns** (sin sustantivo): *mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs*. **No existe** pronombre posesivo *its*.
- *its* (posesivo) **sin apóstrofo**; *it's* = *it is / it has*.
- *'s* con singulares y plurales irregulares (*my sister's boots, children's parties*); solo apóstrofo con plurales en *-s* (*his parents' problem*). Con dos nombres unidos por *and*: *Sue and Tim's car*.
- *'s* con expresiones de tiempo: *yesterday's newspaper, four weeks' holiday*.
- *of* + posesión, sobre todo con cosas: *the pages of her magazine*; doble posesivo: *a friend of mine*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `possessive + (number) + (adjective) + noun`
- `noun + 's` · `plural-s + '` · `a friend of + mine/John's`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- They took off their shoes.
- It was his fault, not theirs.
- It is not his parents' problem.
- They have four weeks' holiday per year.
- He was an old friend of mine.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The tree lost it's leaves.* → ✅ *The tree lost **its** leaves.*
- ❌ *The parrot and it's cage* → ✅ *its cage*
- ❌ *a friend of me* → ✅ *a friend of **mine***
- ❌ *Is that coffee your or mine?* → ✅ ***yours** or mine*
- ❌ *The animals protect its young.* → ✅ *...**their** young.* (concordancia)

- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Many birds {A|build} {B|its} nests {C|high} in trees {D|to protect} their eggs."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El antecedente es 'many birds' (plural): 'their nests'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The decision was not the manager's; it was entirely ____."
  opciones: ["their", "theirs", "their's", "them"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sin sustantivo detrás se usa el pronombre posesivo 'theirs'; 'their's' no existe."
