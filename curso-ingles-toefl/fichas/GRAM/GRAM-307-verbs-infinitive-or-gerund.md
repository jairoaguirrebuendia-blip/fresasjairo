# Verbs followed by infinitives, gerunds, or both (stop/remember/forget) (GRAM-307)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 9 — Infinitives (Part 4), Grammar Notes 2 — Verbs + Infinitives and Gerunds, p. 145.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir infinitivo o gerundio según el verbo y reconocer los verbos que cambian de significado.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Cuatro patrones: (1) solo infinitivo: appear, decide, expect, hope, manage, need, pretend, seem, want, would like; (2) solo gerundio: avoid, be worth, can't help, consider, enjoy, feel like, have trouble, keep, mind, miss, spend (time); (3) ambos sin cambio: begin, can't stand, continue, hate, like, love, prefer, start; (4) ambos con cambio de significado: forget, go on, quit, regret, remember, stop, try.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`manage/decide/hope + to V | avoid/enjoy/consider + V-ing | stop to V (para hacer) ≠ stop V-ing (dejar de)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- They managed **to find** new jobs.
- We considered **hiring** him.
- They began **to encourage** her. / They began **encouraging** her.
- I stopped **to buy** a soda. ≠ I stopped **buying** sodas.
- Maria didn't remember **to call**. (She didn't call.) ≠ Alicia doesn't remember **calling**. (She called.)

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *They managed finding new jobs.* → ✅ *managed to find*
- ❌ *We considered to hire him.* → ✅ *considered hiring*
- ❌ *It's definitely worth to see.* → ✅ *worth seeing*
- ❌ *I'd enjoy to work with him.* → ✅ *I'd enjoy working with him.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be worth + V-ing | valer la pena |  |
| have trouble / a hard time + V-ing | tener dificultad para |  |
| regret to V / regret V-ing | lamentar (informar) / arrepentirse de | cambio de significado |
