# Present simple passive (GRAM-610)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheet 34 "What do you know about the world?" (quiz de datos del mundo), s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar y reconocer la pasiva en presente simple, muy común en textos de datos y ciencia (y en el TOEFL).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
La pasiva pone en primer plano lo que **recibe** la acción. Se forma con **is/are + participio pasado**; el agente, si importa, va con **by**. El verbo *be* concuerda con el sujeto pasivo.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`subject + am/is/are + past participle (+ by + agent)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- 70% of the Earth's surface is covered by water.
- Seals are caught and eaten by polar bears.
- Spanish/English/Mandarin Chinese is spoken as a first language by the largest number of people in the world.
- An earthquake is measured on the Richter scale.
- Houses are heated by hot water from under the ground in Iceland.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Seals are catch by…* → ✅ *Seals are **caught**…* (participio)
- ❌ *Spanish is speak…* → ✅ *Spanish is **spoken**…*
- ❌ *Diamonds is used…* → ✅ *Diamonds **are** used…* (concordancia con sujeto plural)
- ❌ *The Earth covers by water.* → ✅ *The Earth **is covered** by water.* (falta *be*)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be covered by | estar cubierto de | |
| be measured on | medirse en | |
| be used for -ing | usarse para | *Diamonds are used for drilling* |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Most of the world's coffee ____ in Brazil, Vietnam, and Colombia."
  opciones: ["grows", "is grown", "are grown", "growing"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El café es cultivado (pasiva) y 'most of the coffee' (incontable) concuerda en singular → is grown."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Glass {A|is made} from sand, which {B|is heated} until it {C|becomes} liquid and then {D|shape}."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Paralelismo y pasiva: 'is heated… and then shaped'. Falta el participio 'shaped'."
```
