# Infinitives as subjects/objects/complements; It + be + adj + infinitive (GRAM-306)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 9 — Infinitives (Part 4), Grammar Notes 1 — Form and Function of Infinitives, p. 144.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el infinitivo (*to* + base) en funciones nominales, colocar bien *not* y preferir *It is + adj + to…*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
El infinitivo es *to + base* y puede ser sujeto, objeto o complemento del sujeto. Negación: *not* antes de *to*. Como sujeto es formal; es más común *It + be + adjective + infinitive*, con *for + noun/pronoun* para indicar quién hace la acción. Elipsis: se puede repetir solo *to* (*he didn't want to*). *To* del infinitivo va con base; *to* preposición con -ing.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`It + be + adjective + (for + noun/pronoun) + to + base verb | not + to + base verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **To finish** what you started is advisable.
- A teacher's job is **to create** a desire to learn.
- It's advisable **not to get** behind in your homework.
- It's important **for a student to take** good notes in class.
- Steve knew he had to go to work, but he didn't want **to**.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I plan to working hard.* → ✅ *I plan to work hard.* (el libro lo marca con NOT)
- ❌ *Is important to take notes.* → ✅ *It is important…* (falta sujeto *it*)
- ❌ *The teacher asked us don't be late.* → ✅ *asked us not to be late*
- ❌ *It's important that a student to take notes.* → ✅ *for a student to take*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| advisable | aconsejable | It's advisable to… |
| ellipsis | elipsis | didn't want to (sin repetir el verbo) |

```quiz
- tipo: structure

- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The main goal of the program {A|is} {B|to helping} students {C|develop} strong {D|study} habits."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Complemento del sujeto con infinitivo: *to help*. Aquí *to* es parte del infinitivo, no preposición."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The doctor advised him ____ salty foods."
  opciones: ["not eat", "to not eating", "not to eat", "no eating"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "*Advise + objeto + infinitivo*; la negación va antes de *to*: *not to eat*."
```
