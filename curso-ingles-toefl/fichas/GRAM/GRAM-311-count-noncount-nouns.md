# Count vs. non-count nouns (categories, a/an, singular verb) (GRAM-311)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 10 — Count and Non-Count Nouns (Part 5), Grammar Notes 1–3 — Definition of Nouns; Types of Common Nouns; Categories of Non-Count Nouns, pp. 164–165.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir sustantivos contables e incontables y aplicar sus reglas de artículo, plural y concordancia.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Los contables se cuentan por separado, tienen singular y plural y admiten *a/an*. Los incontables no tienen plural en su sentido básico ni llevan *a/an*; suelen ir con *the, some, any* y con verbo y pronombre en singular. Categorías: abstracciones (honesty), enfermedades (cancer), campos de estudio/idiomas (physics), comida/bebida (bread), fenómenos naturales (electricity), partículas (sand, salt) y otros (equipment, furniture, money, news, traffic).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`a/an + singular count noun | Ø / some / any / the + non-count noun + singular verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- There are eight **planets** in our solar system.
- You should avoid **cholesterol**. NOT *You should avoid a cholesterol.*
- Sally said she didn't need **any advice**. NOT *…an advice.*
- **Rice feeds** millions. **It feeds** millions.
- Do you have enough **money** in your account?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *He gave me an advice.* → ✅ *some advice / a piece of advice*
- ❌ *equipments, furnitures, informations* → ✅ *equipment, furniture, information* (sin plural)
- ❌ *It takes a work to prepare a meal.* → ✅ *It takes work…*
- ❌ *Many people needs to lose weight.* → ✅ *need* (people = plural)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| equipment | equipo | incontable |
| furniture | muebles | incontable; 'a piece of furniture' |
| traffic | tráfico | incontable: NOT *a traffic* |

```quiz
  pregunta: "The tour guide {A|gave} us {B|some} useful {C|informations} {D|about} the city."

- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Electricity ____ through copper wires very efficiently."

  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Sustantivo incontable como sujeto → verbo en singular: *travels*."
```
