# Patrones de comparación (GRAM-143)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 26, §221–222, pp. 283–285.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Construir comparaciones completas y correctas, incluido el patrón *the + comparativo…, the + comparativo*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Igualdad:** *as + adj/adv + as*. Negativa: *not as/so … as*. Con sustantivo: *as comfortable a hotel as*, *as much coffee as*, *as many points as*.
- **Superioridad/inferioridad:** comparativo + **than** (nunca *as*): *softer than*, *less expensive than*.
- Otras: *the same as*, *like*, *similar to*, *different from*, *superior/preferable to* (sin *than*).
- Tras *as/than* + pronombre: *than us / than we did*.
- **Superlativo** + *in/of/ever/relativa*: *the tallest building in the world*, *of all*, *the most marvellous painting I've ever seen*, *one of the tallest*.
- **Modificadores:** *much/far/a lot/a bit/slightly + comparativo* (no *very nicer*); *by far the best*; *twice/three times as … as*.
- **Aumento continuo:** *taller and taller, more and more crowded*.
- **Paralelo:** *The + comparativo (+ S + V), the + comparativo (+ S + V)*: *The further you travel, the more you pay.*
- La comparación debe ser **lógica y paralela** (comparar cosas equivalentes).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`as + adj + as` · `adj-er / more adj + than` · `the + comparativo…, the + comparativo…` · `much + comparativo`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Some motels are **as comfortable as** hotels.
- Gold is softer **than** copper. NOT *Gold is softer as copper.*
- Motels are **similar to** hotels. Motels are not very **different from** hotels.
- This new sofa is **much nicer** than the old one. NOT *very nicer*
- **The longer** the journey (is), **the more expensive** the ticket (is).

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *more efficient as* → ✅ *more efficient **than***
- ❌ *as tall than* → ✅ *as tall **as***
- ❌ *The more you practice, you become better.* → ✅ *The more you practice, **the better** you become.*
- ❌ *very more expensive* → ✅ ***much** more expensive*
- ❌ *different than* (en examen) → ✅ *different **from***
- ❌ *The climate of Peru is warmer than Canada.* → ✅ *…than **that of** Canada.* (comparación lógica)

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ a star is, the shorter its life tends to be."
  opciones: ["More massive", "The more massive", "The most massive", "As massive"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Patrón paralelo 'The + comparativo…, the + comparativo…'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Sound travels {A|much} {B|faster} in water {C|as} it does {D|in} air."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Tras un comparativo se usa 'than': 'faster in water than it does in air'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The brain of a dolphin is larger than ____."
  opciones: ["a human", "that of a human", "human", "of a human"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Hay que comparar cerebro con cerebro: 'that of a human' (= the brain of a human)."
```
