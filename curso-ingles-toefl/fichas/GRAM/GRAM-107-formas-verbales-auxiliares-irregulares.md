# Forma del verbo tras cada auxiliar; verbos irregulares (GRAM-107)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., §58–60, pp. 75–78; §299–301, pp. 382–388; §8 y §11, pp. 8–11.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Detectar formas verbales mal construidas: cada auxiliar exige una forma concreta del verbo siguiente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Formas del verbo: base (*steal*), s-form (*steals*), pasado (*stole*), ing-form (*stealing*), participio (*stolen*).
- Orden de auxiliares: **modal → have → be (continuous) → be (passive)**.
- Cada auxiliar determina la forma de la palabra siguiente:
  - **modal + base**: *will steal, should have worked*
  - **have + past participle**: *has gone, has been taking*
  - **be + -ing** (continuous): *was looking*
  - **be + past participle** (pasiva): *were informed, had been stolen*
- En tiempos simples, negativas y preguntas usan **do + base**.
- Irregulares: distinguir **pasado** (*wrote*) de **participio** (*written*): *I've written*, no *I've wrote*. Los prefijos mantienen la irregularidad (*rewritten, undid*).
- **raise** (transitivo, lleva objeto) vs **rise** (intransitivo). **lay/laid/laid** (poner algo, con objeto) vs **lie/lay/lain** (estar tumbado, sin objeto).
- Participios especiales solo antes de sustantivo: *a sunken ship, molten metal, a proven fact*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`modal + V1` · `have + V3` · `be + V-ing` · `be + V3 (pasiva)` · `do/does/did + V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Someone **will steal** them. / This **has gone** on too long.
- His clothes **had been stolen**. / I **must have been dreaming**.
- I wrote the letter yesterday. / I've **written** the letter.
- The oil companies will **raise** their prices. / The price of oil will **rise**.
- The woman **laid** a blanket on the ground. / The woman **lay** in the sunshine.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The data has been **analyze**.* → ✅ *…has been **analyzed**.*
- ❌ *The river has **rose** sharply.* → ✅ *…has **risen** sharply.*
- ❌ *Prices **raised** last year.* → ✅ *Prices **rose** last year.*
- ❌ *She should **has** called.* → ✅ *She should **have** called.*
- ❌ *Did it **opened**?* → ✅ *Did it **open**?*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| rise – rose – risen | subir (sin objeto) | intransitivo |
| raise – raised – raised | levantar, subir algo | transitivo |
| lie – lay – lain | yacer, estar tumbado | intransitivo |
| lay – laid – laid | poner, colocar | transitivo |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Over the past decade, the cost of solar panels {A|has fell} {B|dramatically}, making them {C|affordable} for {D|many} households."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Tras 'has' va el participio: 'fallen' (fall – fell – fallen)."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Many birds {A|lay} their eggs in spring, when food {B|becomes} more {C|plentiful} and temperatures {D|raise}."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Sin objeto se usa 'rise' (intransitivo): 'temperatures rise'. 'lay their eggs' sí es correcto (lleva objeto)."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The ancient manuscript must ____ by monks in the twelfth century."
  opciones: ["have copied", "be copying", "have been copied", "had been copied"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "modal + have + been + participio: deducción sobre el pasado en pasiva (el manuscrito fue copiado)."
```
