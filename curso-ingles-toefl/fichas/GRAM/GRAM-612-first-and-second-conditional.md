# First and second conditional (GRAM-612)

> **Fuente:** *Reward Pre-intermediate Resource Pack* — Macmillan, 1999, Worksheets 37a/37b "What will happen if…?", Worksheet 39 "Dream on" y Progress check 36–40a "What would you do?", s/p.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Distinguir condiciones probables (first conditional) de situaciones imaginarias o poco probables (second conditional) y combinar bien los tiempos.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **First conditional**: situación probable y su resultado → *If + present simple, will + base*.
- **Second conditional**: situación imaginaria o improbable → *If + past simple, would + base*. El pasado no indica tiempo pasado, sino irrealidad.
- Nunca *will/would* dentro de la cláusula *if*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `If + present simple, will/won't + base verb`
- `If + past simple, would/wouldn't + base verb` (también *could* en la cláusula if)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- If you visit my country in winter, we'll go shopping.
- If you write to me, I'll be surprised.
- If you could be somebody else, who would you like to be?
- If Bruno was an animal he'd be an octopus.
- If you found a silver bracelet in the street, would you keep it?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *If you will visit…, we'll go…* → ✅ *If you **visit**…, we'll go…*
- ❌ *If I would have more time, I would travel.* → ✅ *If I **had** more time…*
- ❌ *If you found a bracelet, will you keep it?* → ✅ *…, **would** you keep it?* (no mezclar tipos)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| it depends | depende | el libro pide responder solo yes/no |
| imaginary / unlikely situations | situaciones imaginarias / improbables | → second conditional |

- Ref. libro: Worksheets 37a/37b (juego de mesa con tarjetas de resultado); Worksheet 39 (completar frases sobre uno mismo); Progress check 36–40a (tarjetas de situaciones).

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "If the Earth had no atmosphere, temperatures ____ dramatically between day and night."
  opciones: ["will change", "would change", "changed", "are changing"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Situación imaginaria (If + past simple 'had') → would + base."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "If the price of oil {A|will rise} again, {B|many} airlines {C|will} {D|increase} their fares."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "En la cláusula if del first conditional va present simple: 'rises'."
```
