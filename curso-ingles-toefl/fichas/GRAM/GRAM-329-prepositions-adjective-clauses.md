# Preposition + whom/which (to whom, for which) (GRAM-329)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 15 — Adjective Clauses and Phrases (Part 6), Grammar Notes 1 — Adjective Clauses with Prepositions, p. 251.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Colocar la preposición al inicio (formal) o al final (informal) de la cláusula adjetiva y elegir *whom/which*, nunca *that*, tras preposición.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
*Who(m), that, which, whose* pueden ser objeto de preposición. Preposición al inicio = formal (solo con *whom* o *which*, nunca *that* ni *who*); al final = informal. Si se omite el pronombre, la preposición va al final. *Whose* no se omite.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + preposition + whom/which + subject + verb (formal) | noun + (who/that/which/Ø) + subject + verb + preposition (informal)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Bill is the man **to whom** I spoke.
- Dr. Gomez is the professor **who(m) I spoke to**.
- It's the firm **for which** he works. NOT *It's the firm for that he works.*
- She's the friend **I was referring to**.
- She's the friend **whose car** I ran into. NOT *She's the friend car I ran into.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *the firm for that he works* → ✅ *for which he works*
- ❌ *the man to who I spoke* → ✅ *to whom I spoke*
- ❌ *the man to whom I spoke to* → ✅ *to whom I spoke* (preposición duplicada)
- ❌ *the company for which he works for* → ✅ *for which he works / which he works for*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| refer to | referirse a | the book to which he referred |
| depend on | depender de | the factors on which it depends |
