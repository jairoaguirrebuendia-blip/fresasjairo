# Impersonal 'it' and 'there' (GRAM-209)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 17 "Impersonal subject 'it'", p. 20; Unit 18 "Impersonal subject 'there'", p. 21.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el sujeto "vacío" *it* o *there*, que el español omite ("Llueve", "Hay dos hombres"), y no dejar la oración sin sujeto en el TOEFL.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**It** (Unit 17): introduce información nueva sobre
- hora y fecha (*It is nearly one o'clock*), clima (*It's still raining*, *It's getting dark*),
- opiniones: *it + be + adjetivo/sustantivo + -ing / to-infinitivo / that-clause*,
- reacciones: *It surprised him to realize...*,
- creencias generales con verbos de reporte en pasiva: *It is believed that...*

**There** (Unit 18): *there + be + noun group* para existencia, sucesos y cantidades.
- El verbo concuerda con el sustantivo que sigue (plural con *a number of, a lot of, a few*).
- Con modales: *There could be a problem. There must have been some mistake.*
- Con *appear/seem + to be*: *There seems to have been some carelessness.*

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `It + be + adj + to-infinitive / that-clause`
- `It + is + said/believed/thought + that...`
- `There + be (agrees with noun) + noun group`
- `There + modal + be / have been`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- It was a pleasure to be there.
- It's strange that it hasn't been noticed before.
- Nowadays it is believed that the size is unimportant.
- There have been two telephone calls.
- There appears to be a vast amount of confusion on this point.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Is important to study.* → ✅ ***It** is important to study.*
- ❌ *Have many reasons for this.* / *It has many reasons...* (calco de "hay") → ✅ ***There are** many reasons for this.*
- ❌ *There is two men in the room.* → ✅ *There **are** two men...*
- ❌ *Is believed that...* → ✅ ***It** is believed that...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| it is said / believed / thought that | se dice / cree / piensa que | estilo periodístico |
| there seems to be | parece haber | |
| it's getting + adj | se está poniendo... | cambio de clima |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ many species of birds that migrate thousands of kilometers each year."
  opciones: ["It has", "There are", "They are", "Have"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Para expresar existencia ('hay') se usa 'There are' + sustantivo plural. 'It has' y 'Have' son calcos del español."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ that the ancient city was destroyed by an earthquake."
  opciones: ["Is believed", "It is believed", "Believed", "There is believed"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Creencia general con verbo de reporte en pasiva: 'It is believed that...'. El inglés exige sujeto."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|There} {B|was} a lot of {C|people} waiting {D|outside} the stadium."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Con 'a lot of' + plural ('people') el verbo es plural: 'There were'."
```
