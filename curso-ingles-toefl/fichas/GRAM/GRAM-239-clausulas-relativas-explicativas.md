# Non-defining relative clauses (GRAM-239)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 93 "Non-defining clauses", p. 96.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar cláusulas relativas explicativas (entre comas) con *who / which / whom / whose*, *which* referido a toda la oración, y *some of whom / many of which*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Añaden **información extra**, no necesaria para identificar; van entre comas.
- Siempre con pronombre relativo (no se omite); personas: *who* (formal objeto *whom*); cosas: *which*.
- **No** se usa *that* en las explicativas.
- *which* puede referirse a **toda la cláusula principal**: *I never met Brando again, which was a pity.*
- Cuantificador + *of whom / of which*: *many of whom, some of which, none of whom, one of which*.
- *when / where* tras expresiones de tiempo o lugar: *in 1957, when I was still a baby*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `Noun, who/which + verb ..., main verb`
- `..., which + verb` (comenta toda la oración)
- `..., some/many/none of whom/which + verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Professor Marvin, who was always early, was there already.
- Heath Robinson, who died in 1944, was a graphic artist and cartoonist.
- She was a little tense, which was understandable.
- He talked about several very interesting people, some of whom he was still in contact with.
- This happened in 1957, when I was still a baby.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Paris, that is the capital of France, ...* → ✅ *Paris, **which** is the capital...*
- ❌ *many of them / some of who* (en relativa) → ✅ *many **of whom***
- ❌ *He failed the test, what surprised everyone.* → ✅ *..., **which** surprised everyone.*
- ❌ *The Amazon, it is the largest rainforest, ...* → ✅ *The Amazon, which is...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| some of whom | algunos de los cuales | personas |
| many of which | muchos de los cuales | cosas |
| which (= lo cual) | lo cual | toda la oración |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The conference attracted over two hundred researchers, ____ came from Asia."
  opciones: ["many of them", "many of whom", "many of who", "which many"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Cuantificador + 'of whom' en una relativa explicativa sobre personas. 'many of them' crearía dos cláusulas independientes unidas solo por coma."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The Nile, {A|that} flows {B|northward} through eleven countries, is {C|one of} the longest rivers {D|in} the world."
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "En relativas explicativas (entre comas) no se usa 'that': 'The Nile, which flows...'."
```
