# Changing adjective clauses without be to -ing phrases (who lives → living) (GRAM-332)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 15 — Adjective Clauses and Phrases (Part 6), Grammar Notes 5 — Changing Adjective Clauses without a Be Verb, p. 252.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Convertir una cláusula adjetiva sin *be* en frase con -ing cuando el relativo es sujeto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Si la cláusula no tiene *be*, a menudo puede cambiarse a frase adjetiva: se borra el relativo y el verbo pasa a -ing. Solo es posible cuando *who/which/that* es el sujeto de la cláusula.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`noun + (who/which/that + verb) → noun + V-ing …`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Frank Okawilo, **currently studying** at Harvard University, is going to graduate this year.
- The tenants **currently living** in that apartment building will have to move soon.
- I read a book **describing** World War II. (from *which describes*)
- We saw a movie **taking place** in Pakistan.
- Bob's the colleague **living** downtown.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Researchers study the ocean floor have found new species.* → ✅ *Researchers studying the ocean floor…*
- ❌ *I read a book describes World War II.* → ✅ *a book describing / which describes…*
- ❌ *The tenants live in that building will move.* → ✅ *The tenants living in that building…*
- ❌ *The book I reading…* → ✅ *The book I am reading* (si el relativo es objeto no se puede reducir así)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| currently | actualmente | frecuente en frases -ing |
| take place | tener lugar | a movie taking place in… |

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The report, ____ the effects of pollution on children, was published last week."
  opciones: ["describes", "describing", "described it", "which describing"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Frase adjetiva reducida de *which describes* → *describing*. *Which describing* mezcla relativo y frase reducida."
```
