# Consejo y obligación: should, ought to, had better (GRAM-116)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 12, §93, pp. 118–119; §96, p. 121.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *should/ought to* (consejo), *should have done* (crítica del pasado), *had better* y *be supposed to*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **should / ought to + V1**: lo correcto o lo mejor; más suave que *must*.
- **should/ought to have + V3**: lo correcto **no** se hizo (≠ *had to*, que sí se hizo).
- **had better + V1** (negativa *had better not*): lo mejor en una situación concreta; más fuerte que *should*. Nunca *have better*.
- **be supposed to**: lo esperado por norma, acuerdo o lo que la gente dice.
- **should/ought to** para **probabilidad** de que todo salga bien: *They should have our letter by now.* (no para cosas que salen mal).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`should / ought to + V1` · `should have + V3` · `had better (not) + V1` · `be supposed to + V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- They should build/ought to build more hospitals.
- I ought to have left a tip. (…but I didn't leave one.) / I had to leave a tip. (…so I did.)
- You're ill. You **had better see** a doctor. NOT *You have better see a doctor.*
- Too much sugar is supposed to be bad for you.
- The train should be on time. but NOT *The train should be late.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *You ought study more.* → ✅ *You **ought to** study more.*
- ❌ *You had better to leave.* → ✅ *You had better **leave**.*
- ❌ *He should have call earlier.* → ✅ *…should have **called**…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be supposed to | se supone que | norma/expectativa |
| had better | más vale que | situación concreta |
