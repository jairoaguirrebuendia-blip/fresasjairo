# Future forms (GRAM-229)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 64 "'Will' and 'going to'", p. 67; Unit 65 "Present tenses for future", p. 68.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir *will, going to*, presente simple/continuo, future continuous y future perfect, y **no usar *will* en cláusulas de tiempo y condicionales**.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **will**: predicciones basadas en creencias/opiniones; decisiones del momento (*I think I'll go to bed*); promesas y ofrecimientos.
- **going to**: predicción con evidencia presente (*It's going to rain*); intención ya decidida. No se suele decir *going to go* → *I'm going*.
- **Present simple**: horarios y calendario (*My last train leaves at 11.30*).
- **Present continuous**: planes y arreglos personales (*I'm meeting Bill next week*).
- **Future continuous**: algo que ocurrirá por arreglo (*I'll be waiting for you outside*); **future perfect**: terminado antes de un punto futuro.
- En cláusulas **if**, de **tiempo** (*when, until, after, before, as soon as*) y relativas definitorias se usa **presente** (o present perfect) con sentido futuro, **no *will***.
- *hope/expect/intend/want + to-infinitive* para un futuro incierto.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `will + base` · `be going to + base`
- `when / if / until + present simple, ... will + base`
- `will be + V-ing` · `will have + V3`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The weather tomorrow will be warm and sunny.
- They're going to have a party.
- If he comes, I'll let you know.
- We won't start until everyone has arrived.
- Give my love to any friends you meet.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *When he will arrive, I'll call you.* → ✅ *When he **arrives**, I'll call you.*
- ❌ *If it will rain, we'll stay home.* → ✅ *If it **rains**...*
- ❌ *I'm going to go to the cinema.* (posible, pero poco natural) → ✅ *I'm going to the cinema.*
- ❌ *By 2030 the project will finish.* (antes de un punto) → ✅ *...will have finished.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| by the time / by + year | para cuando / para | future perfect |
| be about to | estar a punto de | |
| until | hasta que | + presente |
