# Wish y subjuntivo (GRAM-123)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 29, §241, pp. 320–322; §242, p. 322.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el pasado "irreal" tras *wish/if only/it's time/would rather* y el subjuntivo (forma base) tras verbos y adjetivos de necesidad.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **wish + would**: deseo de cambio futuro / queja (*I wish you wouldn't smoke*).
- **wish + past / could**: deseo sobre el presente (*NOT I wish I would have…*).
- **wish + past perfect / could have**: deseo sobre el pasado (*NOT I wish you would have told me*).
- **if only** = *I wish*, más enfático.
- Pasado irreal también tras *suppose, imagine, would rather, as if/as though, it's time*.
- **were** (subjuntivo) en lugar de *was*: *If I were you…*
- **Subjuntivo = forma base** (sin -s, sin -ed) en una *that*-clause tras **demand, insist, propose, recommend, request, suggest, ask** y **essential, important, necessary, advisable, desirable**. Negativo: *that he **not** be*. Muy usado en inglés americano (TOEFL).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`wish + S + past / past perfect` · `It's time + S + past` · `recommend/insist/It is essential + that + S + V1 (base)`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I wish I **had** more spare time.
- I wish I **had never bought** this toaster.
- It's time I **got** my hair cut.
- The committee recommended that the scheme **go** ahead.
- It is important that an exact record **be** kept.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The doctor insisted that he stops smoking.* → ✅ *…that he **stop** smoking.*
- ❌ *It is essential that every sample is labeled.* (AmE formal) → ✅ *…every sample **be** labeled.*
- ❌ *I wish I would have studied more.* → ✅ *I wish I **had studied** more.*
- ❌ *I wish I can ski.* → ✅ *I wish I **could** ski.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| insist that | insistir en que | + subjuntivo |
| it is imperative that | es imprescindible que | + subjuntivo |
| as if / as though | como si | pasado irreal |
