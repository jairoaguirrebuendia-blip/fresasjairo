# Verb + object + from + -ing: prevent, prohibit, hinder (GRAM-701)

> **Fuente:** English Synonyms and Antonyms — Fernald, Part I, notas "Prepositions" de PREVENT, PROHIBIT, HINDER, ABSOLVE, ABSTINENCE, COMPEL y ALLOW, PDF pp. 230, 234, 165, 13, 14, 96, 35.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar **from + -ing** tras los verbos de impedir/prohibir y no confundirlos con los verbos que llevan **to + infinitivo**.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Verbos de "impedir/apartar" → `verb + object + from + -ing`: **prevent, prohibit, hinder, stop, keep, discourage, ban**.
  - *He was prevented by illness from joining the expedition.*
  - Fernald: *prohibit* antes llevaba infinitivo (como *forbid*), pero la construcción con **from** + forma en -ing **ha sustituido por completo** a la antigua.
- Sustantivos y verbos de "abstenerse/liberar" también llevan **from**: *abstinence from, absolve from, cease from* (este último arcaico).
- Verbos de "obligar/permitir" → `verb + object + to + infinitive`: **compel, force, allow, permit, enable**. Fernald prefiere *compelled to desert* a *compelled to desertion*.
- *allow of* = forma antigua; en el uso recomendado ya por Fernald, simplemente **allow**.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`prevent/prohibit/stop/keep + O + from + V-ing` · `forbid + O + to + V` · `compel/allow/permit/enable + O + to + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He was prevented by illness from joining the expedition.
- A person is prohibited by law from doing a certain act.
- Hinder one in his progress; from acting promptly; by opposition.
- The negative side of virtue is abstinence from vice.
- The soldiers were compelled to desertion: preferably with the infinitive, compelled to desert.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The law prohibits drivers to use phones* → ✅ *prohibits drivers **from using** phones*.
- ❌ *prevent the disease to spread* → ✅ *prevent the disease **from spreading***.
- ❌ *from use* → ✅ *from **using*** (tras preposición, gerundio).
- ❌ *The rule allows of exceptions* (arcaico) → ✅ *allows exceptions*.
- ❌ *compelled them from leaving* → ✅ *compelled them **to leave***.

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Verbo | Patrón | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| prevent | O + from + -ing | prevent fish from escaping |
| prohibit | O + from + -ing | prohibited from voting |
| stop / keep | O + from + -ing | keep children from falling |
| hinder | O + from + -ing | hindered from advancing |
| forbid | O + to + V | forbid them to enter |
| compel / force | O + to + V | compelled to retreat |
| allow / permit / enable | O + to + V | enabled scientists to measure |

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- *Cease from anger* (PDF p. 86) es arcaico; hoy *cease + -ing / cease + noun* sin preposición.
- *Absolved from (rarely of)*: hoy se usan ambos (*absolved of all blame* es frecuente).
