# Participle clauses after nouns (GRAM-240)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 94 "Participle clauses", p. 97; Unit 95 "Adding to a noun group", p. 98.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer y formar cláusulas reducidas tras un sustantivo (*-ing* activo, *-ed* pasivo), aposiciones y sustantivo + *that*-clause / *to*-infinitivo. Es uno de los patrones más evaluados en Structure.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **-ing clause** tras sustantivo = lo que la persona/cosa **hace** (activo), en un momento o en general: *the box lying on the table* (= which was lying).
- **-ed clause** (participio pasado) = la persona/cosa **recibe** la acción (pasivo): *the idea suggested by Tim* (= which was suggested). Participios irregulares: *written, bought*.
- Tras verbos de percepción + objeto: *we saw Amy walking down the path*.
- Adjetivo + complemento tras sustantivo: *the weapons likely to be used*, *the facilities available here*.
- **Aposición**: segundo noun group entre comas.
- Sustantivos + **that**-clause: *advice, belief, claim, conclusion, decision, evidence, fact, feeling, hope, idea, news, possibility, promise, warning*; algunos + *to*-inf.: *decision, promise, agreement, order, wish*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `noun + V-ing ...` (= who/which + active verb)
- `noun + V3 (+ by ...)` (= who/which + be + V3)
- `noun, appositive noun group, ...` · `the fact / belief / news + that + clause`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The young girl sitting opposite him was his daughter.
- Problems facing parents should be discussed.
- The man injured in the accident was taken to hospital.
- A story written by a young girl won the competition.
- He couldn't believe the news that his house had just burned down.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The man injuring in the accident...* → ✅ *The man **injured**...* (sentido pasivo)
- ❌ *A story wrote by a girl...* → ✅ *A story **written**...*
- ❌ *The students attend the meeting were...* (verbo conjugado sin relativo) → ✅ *The students **attending** the meeting were...*
- ❌ *The girl sits opposite him is his daughter.* → ✅ *The girl **sitting** opposite him...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| participle clause | cláusula de participio | reducida |
| appositive | aposición | entre comas |
| the fact that | el hecho de que | + clause |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Most of the paintings ____ in the exhibition were donated by private collectors."
  opciones: ["displaying", "displayed", "were displayed", "they displayed"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Las pinturas reciben la acción → participio pasado 'displayed' (= which were displayed). 'were displayed' dejaría dos verbos conjugados."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Animals {A|living} in the desert {B|have developed} many ways of {C|conserve} water {D|in} their bodies."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Tras la preposición 'of' se usa gerundio: 'of conserving'. 'living' (cláusula de participio activa) es correcto."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Ernest Hemingway, ____, won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1954."
  opciones: ["he was an American writer", "an American writer", "was an American writer", "who an American writer"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Aposición: un noun group entre comas que explica el sujeto, sin verbo."
```
