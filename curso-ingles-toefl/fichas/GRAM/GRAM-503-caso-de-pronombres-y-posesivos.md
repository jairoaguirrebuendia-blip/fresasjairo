# Pronoun case y posesivos sin apóstrofo (GRAM-503)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 2 "Rules in forming possession" pp. 27–28; Days 3–4 pp. 31, 36–37; Day 16 "Cases of Nouns and Pronouns" pp. 87–92; Day 22 "Common mistakes" pp. 120–121.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir la forma correcta del pronombre según su función y escribir posesivos correctamente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Nominative (subject) case**: *I, you, he, she, it, we, they, who* — subject of a verb (and, in formal English, after *be*).
- **Objective case**: *me, you, him, her, it, us, them, whom* — object of a verb or of a preposition.
- **Possessive case**: *my/mine, your/yours, his, her/hers, its, our/ours, their/theirs, whose* — **never with an apostrophe**.
- *its* = possessive; *it's* = *it is / it has*. *whose* = possessive; *who's* = *who is*.
- In comparisons with *than / as…as*, complete the clause mentally to choose the case: *faster than he (runs)*.
- Nouns: singular + **'s** (*girl's*), plural in -s + **'** (*girls'*), irregular plural + **'s** (*women's*). Indefinite pronouns take 's: *everyone's, someone's*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`subject pronoun + verb` · `verb/preposition + object pronoun` · `possessive (no ') + noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- The Commission on Election failed to publish its findings.
- It's a lovely guitar. (it's = it is)
- Correct: Whose paper is this? — Incorrect: Who's paper is this?
- I run faster than he (runs). · You can run as fast as we (can run).
- Girl's / Girls' · Women's · Everyone's opinion

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The company raised it's prices.* → ✅ *its prices*
- ❌ *between you and I* → ✅ *between you and me* (objeto de preposición)
- ❌ *Him and his brother founded the firm.* → ✅ *He and his brother…*
- ❌ *their's / your's / our's* → ✅ *theirs / yours / ours*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| its / it's | su (de cosa) / es, ha | error ID clásico |
| whose / who's | cuyo / quién es | |
| nominative / objective case | caso sujeto / objeto | |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The professor asked Maria and ____ to present the results to the class."
  opciones: ["I", "me", "myself", "mine"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'Maria and ___' es objeto del verbo 'asked' → caso objetivo 'me'. 'myself' solo se usa si el sujeto es 'I'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The company {A|announced} that {B|it's} profits {C|had risen} {D|sharply}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Posesivo de 'it' = 'its' (sin apóstrofo). 'it's' significa 'it is / it has'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Her sister is much taller than ____."
  opciones: ["she is", "her is", "she does", "hers is"]
  respuesta: A
  explicacion: "Completa la cláusula: 'than she is (tall)'. 'she does' no corresponde a un verbo 'be'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** la tabla de la p. 31 pone "me" como nominativo plural de 1.ª persona (es **we**); la lista de posesivos de la p. 36 incluye "It's" (es **its**); la tabla de la p. 89 coloca "whom" como plural junto a "her"; la regla B de la p. 90 dice que se añade solo apóstrofo a sustantivos que terminan en "y" (falso: *baby's*, como el propio libro muestra). La clave de respuestas (p. 172) da "Them" y "me" en ítems donde el registro formal exige *they* / *I* ("It was they…", "shorter than … I").
- *It's me* es normal en conversación; en TOEFL formal se prefiere *It is I* solo si aparece como opción en Structure.
- *Sanchez'* / *Sanchez's*: ambas formas se aceptan.
