# Personal pronouns: case and agreement (GRAM-208)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 16 "Personal pronouns", p. 19; Unit 19 "Demonstrative pronouns", p. 22.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir el pronombre de sujeto u objeto correcto y hacerlo concordar en número con su antecedente.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Sujeto**: *I, you, he, she, it, we, they*. **Objeto**: *me, you, him, her, it, us, them* (objeto directo/indirecto y **después de preposición**).
- Tras *be* en inglés moderno se usa el objeto: *It's me.*
- *he/she* se refieren a personas; casi nunca a cosas (→ *it*).
- Un pronombre singular remite a un noun group singular y uno plural a un plural. Se admite plural (*they/them*) para referirse a indefinidos (*anybody*) y a colectivos.
- *you* y *they* pueden referirse a la gente en general.
- *one / ones* sustituyen a un sustantivo ya mencionado: *My car is the blue one.* (Unit 19)

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `verb / preposition + object pronoun`
- `singular antecedent → it/he/she/its` · `plural antecedent → they/them/their`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- John took the book and opened it.
- We were all sitting in a cafe with him.
- There was only John, Baz, and me in the room.
- If anybody comes, tell them I'm not in.
- Are the new curtains longer than the old ones?

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *between you and I* → ✅ *between you and **me*** (objeto tras preposición)
- ❌ *The company changed their logo; it...* (mezcla) → ✅ mantener el mismo número: *The company changed **its** logo.*
- ❌ *The birds built its nest.* → ✅ *...built **their** nests.*
- ❌ *My mother she works...* (sujeto duplicado) → ✅ *My mother works...*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| subject pronoun | pronombre sujeto | *I, he, they* |
| object pronoun | pronombre objeto | *me, him, them* |
| one / ones | uno(s) | sustituye a un sustantivo contable |
