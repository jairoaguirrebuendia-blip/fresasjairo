# Tercer condicional, mixtos e inversión (GRAM-121)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 32, §257(6), pp. 337–338; §258, p. 338.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar el condicional irreal del pasado, mezclar tiempos y reconocer la inversión formal sin *if*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Type 3:** `if + past perfect, would/could/might have + V3` — pasado imaginario (no ocurrió). En la cláusula principal **no** past simple ni past perfect.
- *would have* en la cláusula *if* es incorrecto (solo habla muy informal).
- **Mixtos:** condición presente → resultado pasado (*If Tom was more ambitious, he would have found…*) o condición pasada → resultado presente (*If you hadn't woken me up…, I wouldn't feel so tired now*).
- **Formal:** *if … should* (poco probable), *were* (subjuntivo), *were to*.
- **Inversión** (se elimina *if*): **Should** anyone ring…, **Were** the picture genuine…, **Had** you taken a taxi… Negativo: *Had the guests **not** complained…* (sin contracción).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`If + had + V3, would have + V3` · `Had + S + V3, would have + V3` · `Were + S + (to V1), would…` · `Should + S + V1, …`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- If you had taken a taxi, you would have got here in time.
- The man would have died if the ambulance hadn't arrived so quickly.
- If you hadn't woken me up in the middle of the night, I wouldn't feel so tired now.
- **Had** you taken a taxi, you would have got here on time.
- **Should** we not succeed, the consequences would be disastrous.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *If you had taken a taxi, you got here in time.* → ✅ *…you **would have got** here…*
- ❌ *If she would have known, she would have come.* → ✅ *If she **had known**…*
- ❌ *Had they not to delay…* / *Hadn't they delayed…* → ✅ ***Had they not delayed**…*
- ❌ *Were the plan succeed…* → ✅ ***Were the plan to succeed**…* / ***Should the plan succeed**…*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ the warning signs earlier, the engineers could have prevented the collapse."
  opciones: ["If they recognized", "Had they recognized", "They had recognized", "Were they recognized"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Type 3 con inversión: 'Had + sujeto + participio' = If they had recognized."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "If the settlers {A|had brought} more supplies, {B|fewer} of them {C|would die} during {D|the first} winter."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Resultado irreal en el pasado (Type 3) → 'would have died'."
```
