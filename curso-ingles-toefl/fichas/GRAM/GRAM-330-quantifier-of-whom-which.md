# Quantifier + of whom/which/whose; noun + of which (GRAM-330)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 15 — Adjective Clauses and Phrases (Part 6), Grammar Notes 2–3 — Adjective Clauses with Quantifiers; Adjective Clauses with Noun + of which, pp. 251–252.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *most of which, all of whom, an example of which* (no *of them*) para unir ideas sin crear un *comma splice*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Patrón formal y escrito: cuantificador (all, most, many, some, a few, both, none, neither…) + *of* + *whom/which/whose*; refiere a personas o cosas. Patrón *noun + of which* (an example of which) solo para cosas. Si la cláusula está dentro de la principal, va entre comas; si va después, la precede una coma.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`…, quantifier + of + whom (people) / which (things) / whose + noun … | …, noun + of which + verb …`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He has made many friends at the university, **all of whom** he likes.
- His university has many beautiful buildings, **most of which** are old.
- My Swahili professor has written two books, **neither of which** is available yet in this country.
- New York City has many impressive tourist attractions, **an example of which** is the Empire State Building.
- …many students living in my dormitory, **a few of whom** are from other countries. NOT *…a few of which…*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *…many buildings, most of them are old.* → ✅ *most of which are old* (con *them* son dos oraciones unidas por coma)
- ❌ *…students, a few of which are from abroad.* → ✅ *a few of whom* (personas)
- ❌ *…friends, some of who live nearby* → ✅ *some of whom* (tras *of* va objeto)
- ❌ *…two books, neither of which are available* → ✅ *is available* (formal)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| most of which | la mayoría de los cuales | cosas |
| all of whom | todos los cuales | personas |
| an example of which | un ejemplo de lo cual | solo cosas |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The festival attracted thousands of visitors, many of ____ came from abroad."
  opciones: ["them", "whom", "who", "which"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Cuantificador + *of* + relativo de persona → *many of whom*. Con *them* se forma un *comma splice*; *which* es para cosas."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The museum owns {A|several} paintings by Van Gogh, {B|two} of {C|them} {D|are} currently on loan."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Para unir las dos ideas con coma se necesita relativo: *two of which are…*"
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The researcher published four articles, ____ appeared in major journals."
  opciones: ["all of them", "all of which", "all which", "that all"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*All of which* introduce la cláusula relativa. *All of them* deja dos oraciones independientes unidas solo por coma."
```
