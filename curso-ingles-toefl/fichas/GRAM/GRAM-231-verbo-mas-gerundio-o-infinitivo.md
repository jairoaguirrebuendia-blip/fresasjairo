# Verb + -ing or to-infinitive (GRAM-231)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 69 "Verbs with '-ing' clauses", p. 72; Unit 71 "Verb + 'to'- or '-ing'", p. 74.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Saber qué verbos van seguidos de *-ing*, cuáles de *to*-infinitivo, y cuáles cambian de significado (*remember, forget, stop, try, go on, used to*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Verbo + -ing** (Unit 69):
- decir/pensar: *admit, consider, deny, imagine, mention, recall, suggest*;
- gusto: *adore, detest, dislike, dread, enjoy, fancy, mind, resent*;
- otros: *avoid, delay, finish, involve, keep, miss, postpone, practise, resist, risk*;
- phrasal verbs: *carry on, end up, give up, keep on, put off*; frases: *can't help, can't stand, feel like*.
- Pasiva: *being* + V3 (*They enjoy being praised*); pasado: *having* + V3 (*denied having seen*).
- Verbo + objeto + *-ing*: *catch, find, imagine, leave, prevent/stop (from), watch*.

**Ambas formas, mismo sentido** (Unit 71): *attempt, begin, continue, hate, love, prefer, start* (en continuo → *to*: *is beginning to export*).
**Cambia el sentido:**
- *remember/forget + -ing* = recuerdo de algo ya hecho; *+ to* = algo por hacer.
- *regret + -ing* = lamentar lo hecho; *regret to say/inform* = lamentar lo que se dice ahora.
- *try to* = esforzarse; *try -ing* = probar como experimento.
- *go on -ing* = continuar; *go on to* = pasar a hacer otra cosa.
- *be used to -ing* = estar acostumbrado; *used to + base* = solía.
- *need to* (sujeto activo) / *need -ing* (sentido pasivo: *It needs cutting*).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `avoid / enjoy / finish / suggest + V-ing`
- `be used to + V-ing` · `used to + base`
- `remember + to-inf` (futuro) / `remember + V-ing` (pasado)

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- He denied taking drugs.
- I suggested meeting her for a coffee.
- I can't help worrying.
- I must remember to send a gift for her child.
- We are used to working together.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *He suggested to meet her.* → ✅ *He suggested **meeting** her.*
- ❌ *They avoided to answer.* → ✅ *They avoided **answering**.*
- ❌ *I'm used to work at night.* → ✅ *I'm used to **working** at night.*
- ❌ *She enjoys to read.* → ✅ *She enjoys **reading**.*
- ❌ *He denied to have seen him.* → ✅ *He denied **having seen** him.*
