# Time prepositions and adverbials: at/in/on, for/since/during, ago, already/still/yet (GRAM-223)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 40 "Adverbials of time", p. 43; Unit 42 "Adverbials of duration", p. 45.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar la preposición de tiempo correcta y los marcadores *for, since, during, ago, by, already, still, yet, no longer*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **at**: horas (*at eight o'clock*), festividades (*at Christmas*), comidas, periodos concretos (*at night, at the weekend*).
- **in**: estaciones, años, siglos, meses, partes del día (*in the morning*); y "dentro de" un plazo futuro (*in the next few days*).
- **on**: días, días especiales, fechas (*on Monday, on my birthday, on June 21st*).
- Sin preposición con *yesterday, today, tomorrow, last/next + week...*
- **for** + periodo (cualquier tiempo verbal). **during** = dentro de un periodo, **no** para duración (*during three weeks* ✗).
- **since** + punto de inicio, con present perfect / past perfect.
- **ago** después del periodo, con **past simple** (nunca con present perfect).
- **by** = no más tarde de; *from...to/until*, *between...and*.
- *already* antes del verbo principal (tras *be*); *still* = continúa; *yet* al final de negativas/preguntas; *any longer / any more* al final de negativas; *no longer* (formal) antes del verbo.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `for + period` · `since + point in time` (+ present perfect)
- `period + ago` (+ past simple)
- `haven't + V3 + yet` · `no longer + verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Marilyn has lived in Paris since 1984.
- I saw him twice during the holidays.
- John's wife died five years ago.
- Can we get this finished by tomorrow?
- He no longer wanted to buy it.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I went there during three weeks.* → ✅ *...**for** three weeks.*
- ❌ *We have gone to Spain two years ago.* → ✅ *We **went** to Spain two years ago.*
- ❌ *She has lived here since ten years.* → ✅ *...**for** ten years.*
- ❌ *in Monday / on 1990* → ✅ *on Monday / in 1990*
- ❌ *ago two years* → ✅ *two years ago*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| by + time | para (a más tardar) | |
| until / till | hasta | |
| no longer | ya no | formal |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The bridge {A|has been} closed to traffic {B|since} {C|more than} {D|two months}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Con un periodo de tiempo ('more than two months') se usa 'for', no 'since'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The first {A|successful} vaccine {B|was developed} more than two centuries {C|before ago} by Edward Jenner, {D|an English} doctor."
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'ago' va solo, después del periodo: 'more than two centuries ago'. 'before ago' es incorrecto."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The library will be closed ____ the holidays and will reopen in January."
  opciones: ["since", "during", "ago", "for"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'during' + un periodo nombrado ('the holidays') indica dentro de qué periodo ocurre algo. 'for' necesita una duración ('for two weeks')."
```
