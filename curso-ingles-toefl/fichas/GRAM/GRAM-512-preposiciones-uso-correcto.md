# Prepositions: in/on/at, between/among, in/into, different from (GRAM-512)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 15 "Prepositions", pp. 84–86; Day 20 "Preposition / Prepositional Phrases / The Correct Use of Prepositions", pp. 107–111; "List of Troublesome Words" n.º 32, p. 171.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Elegir la preposición correcta en los contrastes y combinaciones fijas más evaluados.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- A preposition goes before a noun/pronoun (its **object**) and shows its relation to another word; *preposition + object* = **prepositional phrase** (works as adjective or adverb).
- Place: **at** + specific address; **on** + street/avenue; **in** + city, province, country, continent.
- **between** two; **among** three or more.
- **in** = location/motion inside; **into** = movement from outside to inside.
- **beside** = next to; **besides** = in addition to.
- **different from** (forma preferida en inglés formal); **differ from** = be unlike; **differ with** = disagree with someone.
- **agree with** a person; **agree to** a proposal.
- **in regard to / with regard to** (no *regards*).
- Compound prepositions take a noun, not a clause: *because of, in spite of, instead of, according to, in addition to*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`at + number + street` · `on + street` · `in + city/country` · `between A and B` · `among + group (3+)` · `preposition + noun / -ing`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- My classmate lives on Ayala Avenue. · My classmate lives in Canada.
- My classmate lives at 111 Ayala Avenue, Pasig City.
- The swimmer jumped into the pool. · She is swimming in the pool.
- Correct: Can Angelo sit beside you?
- Correct: Her testimony in court was different from what she told me.
- Cebu differs from Manila in many ways.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *different than* → ✅ *different from* (respuesta esperada en TOEFL)
- ❌ *in regards to* → ✅ *in regard to*
- ❌ *She lives in Elm Street.* → ✅ *on Elm Street*
- ❌ *because the rain* → ✅ *because of the rain*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| in spite of / despite | a pesar de | + sustantivo / -ing |
| according to | según | |
| in accordance with | de acuerdo con | (no *to*) |
| instead of | en lugar de | + sustantivo / -ing |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The prize money was shared equally ____ all the members of the team."
  opciones: ["between", "among", "beside", "into"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'all the members' es un grupo de más de dos → 'among'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The climate of the coast is {A|quite} different {B|than} the climate {C|of} the {D|interior}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "En inglés formal se usa 'different from'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Dr. Lee has lived ____ Elm Street for ten years."
  opciones: ["in", "at", "on", "into"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "Nombre de calle sin número → 'on'. Con número exacto sería 'at 25 Elm Street'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** el ejemplo "correcto" "The book about costs $99" (p. 109) tiene el orden mal (**The book costs about $99**); declara incorrectos "around $99" y "over one hundred apples" (pp. 109, 111), que son aceptables en inglés actual (solo es una preferencia de estilo); marca "We agree with his suggestion" como incorrecto (p. 110), aunque *agree with* + idea también es común; la lista de compuestas trae "in accordance to" (p. 85; correcto **in accordance with**) y "in apposition with"; erratas en listas: "expect", "un" (p. 108), "though" por *through* (p. 107); un ejemplo "incorrecto" sin verbo: "Micoy and Ramen the money among them" (p. 110).
- En el uso real *between* también se usa con más de dos elementos individuales (*a treaty between three countries*); para TOEFL, aplica la regla básica.
