# Formas de la pasiva (GRAM-125)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 13, §105–108, pp. 134–137.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Construir la pasiva en cualquier tiempo y con modales, y detectar formas incompletas.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- Pasiva = **be** (en el tiempo de la activa) + **participio pasado**.
  - simple: *are killed / were found*; perfect: *has been seized / had been loaded*; continuous: *is being called / were being questioned*; future: *will be destroyed / are going to be charged*.
- **Modal + be + V3**; **modal + have been + V3** (pasado).
- Phrasal/prepositional verbs conservan la partícula tras el participio: *was cut down, has been sent for, is made fun of*.
- Verbos con dos objetos (*give, offer, award, tell, show, send*): la persona suele ser el sujeto: *I've been offered a job.*
- **get + V3**: pasiva informal para hechos accidentales o incidentales (no para acciones grandes planeadas).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`be (cualquier tiempo) + V3` · `is/was being + V3` · `has/had been + V3` · `modal + be + V3` · `modal + have been + V3`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Cocaine **has been seized** by the FBI.
- Three men **were being questioned** by detectives last night.
- Stamps **can be bought** at any post office.
- This bill **ought to have been paid** weeks ago.
- The tree **was cut down** last week. / A cyclist **was awarded** £1 million damages.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The results are being analyze.* → ✅ *…are being **analyzed**.*
- ❌ *The report should be wrote by Friday.* → ✅ *…should be **written**…*
- ❌ *The house is building now.* → ✅ *The house **is being built** now.*
- ❌ *The project must have finished by engineers.* → ✅ *…must have **been** finished…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| be done away with | ser abolido | pasiva de verbo + adv + prep |
| get married / get lost | casarse / perderse | idiomas con get |
