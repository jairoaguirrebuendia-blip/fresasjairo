# Sustantivos contables e incontables (GRAM-130)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 17, §144, pp. 179–182.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Reconocer los incontables (sin *a/an* ni plural) y cuantificarlos con *a piece of / an item of*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Incontables** (sin plural, sin *a/an*, verbo singular): accommodation, advice, baggage/luggage, equipment, evidence, furniture, homework, information, knowledge, machinery, money, news, progress, research, scenery, traffic, transport, travel, weather, work…
- Se cuentan con **countable + of + uncountable**: *a piece of information, an item of furniture, a glass of water, a sheet of paper*.
- Sus "parejas" contables: *suitcase(s)* vs *luggage*, *job(s)* vs *work*, *fact(s)* vs *evidence*, *vehicle(s)* vs *traffic*.
- **number of** + plural; **amount of** + incontable.
- Doble uso con cambio de significado: *a glass / glass; a paper (periódico) / paper; an experience (evento) / experience (práctica); a business (empresa) / business; time / times (veces)*. Materia → incontable; objeto individual → contable.
- *kind/sort/type of* sirven con ambos.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`some / much / little + incontable (singular)` · `a piece/item/bit of + incontable` · `a large amount of + incontable`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I've got some information for you. NOT *an information*
- There was no news of the missing hiker. NOT *There were no news.*
- They had very little furniture. NOT *very few furnitures*
- They had very few **items of** furniture.
- an interesting experience (= an event) / experience in the job

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The researchers collected many evidences.* → ✅ *…collected **much evidence / a lot of evidence**.*
- ❌ *She gave me an advice.* → ✅ *…**some advice / a piece of advice**.*
- ❌ *a large number of equipment* → ✅ ***a large amount of** equipment*
- ❌ *Researches show…* → ✅ ***Research shows**…*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| research | investigación | incontable |
| evidence | pruebas, evidencia | incontable |
| equipment / machinery | equipo / maquinaria | incontables |
| a sheet of paper | una hoja de papel | partitivo |

```quiz
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The library has {A|recently} acquired {B|new equipments} that {C|allow} students {D|to scan} rare books."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'equipment' es incontable: 'new equipment' (sin -s)."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Scientists have gathered ____ to support the theory of continental drift."
  opciones: ["many evidences", "an evidence", "a great deal of evidence", "a large number of evidence"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "'evidence' es incontable: se usa 'a great deal of / much' (no 'many', 'an' ni 'number of')."
```
