# Cláusulas de participio (GRAM-153)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 16, §135–136, pp. 167–169; §139, pp. 169–173.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Construir y reconocer frases de participio que funcionan como cláusulas adverbiales, con el sujeto implícito correcto.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Formas:** activo *-ing* (*waiting*), pasivo *V3* (*covered*), pasivo continuo *being + V3*, perfecto *having + V3* / *having been + V3*; negativo *not* delante (*not knowing*).
- **Sujeto implícito = sujeto de la cláusula principal.** Si no coincide, la frase es errónea o absurda: *NOT Walking across the field, a plane flew past.* (dangling participle).
- **Tiempo:** acción simultánea (*Mike hurt his hand playing badminton*); acción previa → **having + V3**, obligatorio si la primera acción no es breve.
- **Conjunción + participio:** *when, while, once, until, if, although* + -ing/V3 (*when using…, once opened…*).
- **Razón:** *Not feeling very well, James decided…*; **resultado** al final: *…, killing all the fish*; **condición:** *Taken daily, vitamin pills can…*
- Con sujeto propio (absoluto): *The lights having gone out, …*; *With prices going up so fast, …*
- Elegir **activo** (el sujeto hace) o **pasivo** (el sujeto recibe).

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`V-ing …, S + V` · `Having + V3 …, S + V` · `V3 (pasivo) …, S + V` · `when/while/although + V-ing/V3, S + V`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **Having waited** an hour, the crowd were getting impatient.
- **Cut** above the right eye, the boxer was unable to continue.
- NOT *Walking across the field, a plane flew past.* → *Walking across the field, we saw a plane fly past.*
- **Once opened**, the contents should be consumed within three days.
- They pumped waste into the river, **killing** all the fish.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Built in 1889, millions of tourists visit the Eiffel Tower.* → ✅ *Built in 1889, **the Eiffel Tower** is visited by millions.*
- ❌ *Having finish the test, the students left.* → ✅ *Having **finished**…*
- ❌ *Digging a hole in the road, the men just disappeared.* → ✅ ***Having dug** a hole…*
- ❌ *Finding in 1922, the tomb…* → ✅ ***Found** in 1922, the tomb…*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "____ by the heavy rain, the farmers postponed the harvest."
  opciones: ["Delaying", "Delayed", "Having delayed", "To delay"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Los granjeros fueron retrasados (reciben la acción) → participio pasivo 'Delayed'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Having studied the data for months, ____."
  opciones: ["the conclusion was finally reached", "the researchers finally reached a conclusion", "a conclusion finally came", "it was finally concluded"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "El sujeto implícito de 'Having studied' debe ser quien estudió: 'the researchers'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "{A|Having been} {B|discovered} by accident, penicillin {C|became} one of the most {D|importance} drugs in history."
  respuesta: D
  explicacion: "Se necesita adjetivo tras 'the most': 'important'. La frase de participio es correcta (sujeto = penicillin)."
```
