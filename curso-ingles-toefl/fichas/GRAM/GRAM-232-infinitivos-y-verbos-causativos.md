# Infinitives and causative verbs (GRAM-232)

> **Fuente:** Intermediate English Grammar and Practice — Unit 70 "Infinitives", p. 73; Unit 72 "Verbs with other clauses", p. 75.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar *to*-infinitivo tras ciertos verbos, verbo + objeto + *to*-inf., *wh-word* + *to*-inf., infinitivo tras sustantivos/superlativos, y los causativos *make/let/have/get*.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
**Infinitivos (Unit 70):**
- Verbo + *to*: *agree, choose, decide, expect, hope, intend, learn, mean, offer, plan, promise, refuse, fail, manage, pretend, tend, want*.
- Verbo + objeto + *to*: *advise, ask, encourage, expect, invite, order, persuade, remind, teach, tell, allow, force, get, help, want*. *want* **no** va con *that*-clause.
- Verbo + *for* + objeto + *to*: *arrange, wait, long, pay, ask, appeal*.
- Pasiva + *to*: *He is said to have died...* (*believe, consider, know, report, say, think*).
- *wh-word* + *to*: *I didn't know what to call him; how to use it*.
- Sustantivo + *to*: propósito (*a meeting to discuss*), necesidad (*things to remember*), tras ordinal/superlativo/*only/next/last* (*the first woman to be elected*), y sustantivos abstractos (*ability, attempt, chance, need, opportunity, willingness to*).

**Otras cláusulas (Unit 72):**
- *make / let* + objeto + **base** (sin *to*): *My father made me go.*
- Percepción (*see, hear, feel, watch*) + objeto + *-ing* (acción en curso) o + base (acción completa).
- *have / get* + objeto + **participio**: encargar algo o sufrir algo (*We must get the car repaired; She had her purse stolen*).
- *help* + objeto + base o *to*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
- `V + O + to-inf` · `make/let + O + base` · `have/get + O + V3`
- `the first / the only / the oldest + noun + to-inf`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- England failed to win a place in the finals.
- They advised us not to wait around too long.
- She was the first woman to be elected to the council.
- Jenny let him talk.
- We've just had the house decorated.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *I want that you come.* → ✅ *I want **you to come**.*
- ❌ *She made him to apologize.* → ✅ *She made him **apologize**.*
- ❌ *He let us to leave.* → ✅ *He let us **leave**.*
- ❌ *I had my hair cutting.* → ✅ *I had my hair **cut**.*
- ❌ *the first man walking on the moon* (estilo TOEFL) → ✅ *the first man **to walk** on the moon*

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Valentina Tereshkova was the first woman ____ into space."
  opciones: ["traveling", "to travel", "traveled", "who travel"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Tras ordinal ('the first') + sustantivo se usa to-infinitivo: 'the first woman to travel'. 'who travel' falla en concordancia y tiempo."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The new regulations {A|make} factories {B|to reduce} their emissions {C|by} thirty percent {D|within} five years."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'make' + objeto + forma base sin 'to': 'make factories reduce'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The scientists {A|persuaded} the government {B|that} {C|fund} a new {D|research} station."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'persuade' + objeto + to-infinitivo: 'persuaded the government to fund'."
```
