# Irregular nouns: news/mathematics; phenomena/criteria; people/police (GRAM-313)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 10 — Count and Non-Count Nouns (Part 5), Grammar Notes 6 — Irregular Nouns, p. 166.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Concordar correctamente el verbo con incontables terminados en -s, plurales irregulares y sustantivos solo plurales.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Incontables con -s llevan verbo singular: news, mathematics, economics, physics. Plurales irregulares de origen latino/griego: criterion→criteria, stimulus→stimuli, phenomenon→phenomena; species no cambia. Solo plurales: people, police (verbo plural). En singular: *person*, *police officer*. *A people* = un grupo étnico.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`news/mathematics/physics + singular verb | phenomenon (sg) / phenomena (pl) | people/police + plural verb`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- **The news is** not good tonight.
- **Mathematics is** a difficult subject for me.
- Thunder is **an** atmospheric **phenomenon**. Thunder and lightning are atmospheric **phenomena**.
- **The police are** coming. NOT *The police is coming.*
- The Hutu are **a people** living in Rwanda and Burundi.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *Economics are a popular major.* → ✅ *Economics is…*
- ❌ *This phenomena is rare.* → ✅ *This phenomenon is rare.*
- ❌ *The main criterias were…* → ✅ *The main criteria were…*
- ❌ *The police is investigating.* → ✅ *The police are investigating.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| criterion / criteria | criterio / criterios |  |
| stimulus / stimuli | estímulo / estímulos |  |
| species | especie(s) | igual en singular y plural |

```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "Physics ____ the study of matter, energy, and the interactions between them."
  opciones: ["are", "is", "were", "have been"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*Physics* es un campo de estudio incontable terminado en -s: verbo singular *is*."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "Scientists still cannot fully explain {A|this} strange {B|phenomena}, which {C|has} puzzled observers {D|for} centuries."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Con *this* y *has* se necesita el singular *phenomenon*; *phenomena* es plural."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The police ____ still investigating the cause of the fire."
  opciones: ["is", "are", "has", "was"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "*Police* es siempre plural: *are*."
```
