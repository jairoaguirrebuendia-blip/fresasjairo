# Compound modifiers (a ten-year-old girl, prize-winning) and number of modifiers (GRAM-323)

> **Fuente:** *Focus on Grammar 5*, 5th ed. — autor no consta en el documento, Unit 13 — Modification of Nouns (Part 5), Grammar Notes 5–6 — Compound Modifiers; Number of Modifiers, p. 216.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Formar modificadores compuestos con guion y sustantivo en singular (*a ten-year-old daughter*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
Tipos de modificadores compuestos: número + sustantivo (*twelve-story*), sustantivo + participio presente (*prize-winning*), sustantivo + participio pasado (*crime-related*), adjetivo + participio pasado (*long-haired*). Antes del sustantivo llevan guion; después, normalmente no. El sustantivo dentro del compuesto va en singular. Evita más de tres adjetivos o más de dos sustantivos modificadores seguidos.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`number + singular noun (hyphenated) + head noun | noun + V-ing / V-ed + head noun | adjective + V-ed + head noun`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I work in a **twelve-story** building.
- It's a **prize-winning** film.
- It's a **computer-generated** program. / The program is computer generated.
- Her daughter is ten years old. She has a **ten-year-old** daughter. NOT *a ten-years-old daughter*
- The actor plays a **long-haired, one-armed** pirate in the movie.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *a ten-years-old daughter* → ✅ *a ten-year-old daughter*
- ❌ *a five-days trip* → ✅ *a five-day trip*
- ❌ *a two-hours exam* → ✅ *a two-hour exam*
- ❌ *the student portrait painter award* → ✅ reorganizar con preposición (*the award for portrait painting*)

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| prize-winning | galardonado | noun + V-ing |
| crime-related | relacionado con el crimen | noun + V-ed |
| long-haired | de pelo largo | adj + V-ed |
