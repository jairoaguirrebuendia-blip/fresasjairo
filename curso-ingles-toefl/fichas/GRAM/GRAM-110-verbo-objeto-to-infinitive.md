# Verbo + objeto + to-infinitive (GRAM-110)

> **Fuente:** Oxford Guide to English Grammar — Eastwood, J., Chapter 14, §122, pp. 151–153; §131(5), p. 162.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Usar el patrón `verbo + objeto + to + base` y reconocer los verbos que **no** lo admiten (*suggest*, *want that…*).

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- **Orden / petición:** advise, ask, beg, encourage, invite, order, persuade, remind, tell, urge, warn → *told Celia to stay*.
- **Causa / ayuda:** allow, cause, compel, enable, forbid, force, get, help, lead, oblige, permit, require, teach.
- **Decir / pensar (formal):** believe, consider, expect, find, know, report, suppose… (*believed the Mafia to have committed…*), más común en pasiva.
- **Querer / gustar:** want, wish, would like, would prefer → *I want everyone to enjoy themselves* (NO *want that…*).
- Negativo: `objeto + not to + V`.
- *suggest* **no** admite este patrón: usar *suggest that…*.
- *consider* sí (*consider ourselves (to be)*), *regard* necesita *as*.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`S + V + objeto + (not) to + V1` · pasiva: `objeto + be + V3 + to + V1`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- I expected Dave **to meet** me at the airport.
- We asked the teacher **not to give** us any homework.
- The crisis has forced the government **to act**.
- This portable phone enables me **to keep** in touch with the office.
- NOT *We suggested our neighbours to turn the music down.*

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The new law allows that citizens vote online.* → ✅ *…allows citizens **to vote**…*
- ❌ *Computers enable scientists processing data.* → ✅ *…enable scientists **to process**…*
- ❌ *I want that you come.* → ✅ *I want **you to come**.*
- ❌ *We regard him to be an expert.* → ✅ *We regard him **as** an expert.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| enable sb to | permitir/capacitar | muy frecuente en textos académicos |
| urge sb to | instar | |
| be required to | estar obligado a | pasiva de require |
