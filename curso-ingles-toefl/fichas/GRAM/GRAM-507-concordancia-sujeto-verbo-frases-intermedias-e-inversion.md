# Subject–verb agreement I: frases intermedias, inversión y "the number of" (GRAM-507)

> **Fuente:** English Grammar Master in 30 Days — Gonzales, Xyli C., Day 10 "Thirty Basic Rules in Subject-Verb Agreement", pp. 62–67; Day 12 p. 75; Day 13 "Special Cases of Agreement" (D, F), pp. 80–81.

## 🎯 Objetivo
Localizar el sujeto real aunque esté separado del verbo o vaya después de él, y hacer concordar el verbo.

## 📘 Regla / Concepto
- The verb agrees with its **subject** in person and number (*he doesn't*, *they don't*).
- A prepositional phrase after the subject (*of…*) does **not** change the verb: the head noun decides.
- Phrases introduced by *together with, as well as, along with, including, accompanied by, in addition to* do not change the number of the subject.
- If subject and complement differ in number, the verb agrees with the **subject**.
- In inverted sentences (*There/Here + verb + subject*, or a fronted phrase + verb + subject), find the real subject after the verb.
- **The number of** + plural noun → singular verb; **a number of** + plural noun → plural verb.
- *It* as introductory subject → singular verb.

## 🧩 Estructura / Fórmula
`Head noun + (of… / as well as…) + verb (agrees with head noun)` · `There + be + real subject` · `The number of Ns + is` · `A number of Ns + are`

## 💬 Ejemplos (del texto)
- Wrong: A list of books were made by Merry. → Right: A list of books was made by Merry.
- Right: The teacher, together with her pupils, was there.
- Right: The theme of the essay is the experiences of our heroes.
- Right: In this school are enrolled several alien students.
- The number of students in the class is limited. · A number of teachers are waiting for you.
- There are six school days in a week.

## ⚠️ Errores comunes (clave TOEFL)
- ❌ *The results of the survey shows…* → ✅ *The results of the survey show…*
- ❌ *The director, along with the actors, are expected…* → ✅ *…is expected…*
- ❌ *There is many reasons…* → ✅ *There are many reasons…*
- ❌ *The number of cars have increased.* → ✅ *The number of cars has increased.*

## 🔤 Vocabulario clave
| Palabra/colocación | Significado | Nota |
|---|---|---|
| together with / as well as | junto con / así como | no suman al sujeto |
| the number of | el número de | singular |
| a number of | varios | plural |

## 🧪 Práctica estilo TOEFL
```quiz
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "The results of the survey ____ that most residents support the new park."
  opciones: ["shows", "is showing", "show", "has shown"]
  respuesta: C
  explicacion: "El núcleo del sujeto es 'results' (plural); 'of the survey' no cuenta → 'show'."
- tipo: error-id
  pregunta: "The director, {A|together with} the actors, {B|are} {C|expected} to attend the {D|premiere}."
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "'together with the actors' no cambia el sujeto singular 'The director' → 'is expected'."
- tipo: structure
  pregunta: "There ____ several reasons for the decline in bee populations."
  opciones: ["is", "are", "has", "being"]
  respuesta: B
  explicacion: "Con 'there', el sujeto real va después: 'several reasons' (plural) → 'are'."
```

## 🗒️ Notas / Excepciones
- **Errores del libro (no copiar):** la regla 25 da "The number of apples was rotten" (p. 67; no tiene sentido: es *the apples* lo que se pudre); "A number of books are on reserved" (p. 66; correcto *on reserve*); "The peoples at Asia" (p. 65; correcto *of Asia*); en la p. 80 el ejemplo "incorrecto" "A number of teachers waiting inside are small" mezcla dos ideas y no ilustra la regla. La lista de la regla 5 incluye "not" sin explicación.
- La clave del ejercicio (p. 173) da "gets" en "one of those players who always ___"; en gramática formal el verbo concuerda con *players* (plural) → **get**.
